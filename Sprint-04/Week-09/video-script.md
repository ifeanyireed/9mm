### [1:11 – 1:55] Breakdown: The 4 Causes of Register Discrepancies (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI next to a diagram highlighting the 4 retail reconciliation leaks.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Why do 88% of retail stores in Nigeria suffer daily register discrepancies?
It comes down to four systemic flaws:
**1. Disconnected Card Terminals:** Cashier rings ₦45,000 on the register, but types ₦4,500 on the standalone POS terminal. Instant discrepancy.
**2. Untracked Split Payments:** Customer pays partly with card, cash, and bank transfer. Legacy POS systems fail to bind all three payment rails to a single transaction token.
**3. Delayed Bank Settlement:** Card acquirers settle T+1 or T+2, but your accountant is trying to reconcile T+0.
**4. Ghost Bank Transfers:** Cashiers release goods on fake SMS alerts or unverified transfer screenshots that never actually clear into company accounts."
