## The Phantom Strike-Off: Why 100,000+ Nigerian Companies Face Involuntary Dissolution

In Nigerian commerce, corporate executives frequently assume that incorporation is a permanent status. You register your limited liability company, pay the statutory stamp duties, collect your digital Certificate of Incorporation, and focus entirely on commercial operations, client acquisition, and revenue generation.

Inside the Corporate Affairs Commission, however, **incorporation is a conditional license to operate**.

Under Section 692(1) of CAMA 2020, where the Commission has reasonable cause to believe that a company is not carrying on business or in operation—specifically evidenced by failure to file statutory Annual Returns for two or more consecutive years—the Registrar-General is legally empowered to strike the company's name off the register.

When a company is struck off under Section 692:
1. **Corporate Dissolution**: The legal personality of the company is dissolved. It ceases to exist as a corporate entity in the eyes of Nigerian law.
2. **Asset Escheatment to the Federation**: Under CAMA Section 693, any property, bank deposits, and assets belonging to the company immediately become bona vacantia (unowned property) and vest in the Federal Government of Nigeria.
3. **Personal Director Liability**: Directors lose the protective veil of limited liability for debts incurred while operating an unregistered or struck-off entity.

## 3 Critical Warning Signs Your Company Is on the Inactive Blacklist

Before the CAC publishes your company's name in the Federal Government Gazette for final dissolution, your organization will experience distinct operational symptoms. Recognizing these three warning signs early protects your enterprise from catastrophic disruption:

### 1. Warning Sign #1: Commercial Bank KYC Review Letters and Post-No-Debit (PND) Alerts
The most immediate operational indicator of CAC delinquency is friction at your commercial bank. Under Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) AML/CFT regulations and credit risk guidelines, commercial banks are mandated to conduct periodic KYC re-validation of corporate accounts. 
* Bank compliance desks query the live CAC Companies Registration Portal (CRP) via automated API switches.
* If your status reflects "INACTIVE", the bank’s core system automatically triggers a 30-day compliance remediation notice.
* If unrectified, the bank places an automated Post-No-Debit (PND) hold on your corporate operating and escrow accounts under regulatory mandate.

### 2. Warning Sign #2: Immediate Disqualification from Public Sector Tenders & Enterprise Vendor Portals
When bidding for federal, state, or multinational enterprise contracts, technical evaluation committees conduct preliminary statutory screening:
* Procurement evaluators run your RC number against the live CAC registry.
* An "Inactive" status results in immediate non-responsiveness under Section 16(6) of the Public Procurement Act 2007, disqualifying your tender before your technical methodology or financial quote is ever examined.
* Enterprise procurement portals (telecoms, oil & gas, FMCG) automatically reject vendor onboarding applications when registry API calls return negative standing.

### 3. Warning Sign #3: Portal Lockout on CRP and Unreconciled PSC Register Queries
When attempting to execute standard post-incorporation actions—such as appointing a new director, increasing share capital, or registering an asset debenture:
* The CAC portal flags the corporate profile as "Restricted", blocking new submissions until all outstanding annual returns are settled.
* The system rejects filings due to non-compliance with CAMA 2020 Persons with Significant Control (PSC) disclosures under Section 119.
* Corporate changes remain in indefinite "Query" status, paralyzing corporate restructuring and investment syndication.

### 2. Clear Historical Annual Returns Backlog via Batch Processing
Under Section 417-421 of CAMA 2020, companies must file annual returns accompanied by certified financial statements. For companies in arrears for multiple years, do not file piecemeal. Structure a comprehensive, multi-year batch reconciliation package, recalculating statutory filing fees and accumulated penalties to secure a single, clean clearance.

### 3. Synchronize CAMA 2020 Persons with Significant Control (PSC) Register
Under CAMA 2020 Section 119, companies must unmask and declare all natural persons holding 5% or more equity, voting rights, or operational control. The CAC has made PSC compliance a mandatory prerequisite for active status. Synchronize your PSC filings with your current register of members to eliminate statutory query flags.

### 4. Formally Update Outdated Registered Office Addresses (Form CAC 3)
One of the primary triggers for Section 692 strike-off is returned regulatory mail. If your company relocated its operating office without filing a formal Notice of Change of Registered Address (Form CAC 3), the CAC presumes the entity is abandoned. File an immediate address regularization to anchor your corporate presence.

### 5. Audit and Regularize Director and Shareholder Registers
Discrepancies between physical board minutes and CAC portal records are a major cause of corporate paralysis. Audit your listed directors, remove deceased or resigned board members, formally regularize current directorships, and verify that all directors' National Identification Numbers (NIN) are correctly mapped.
