### The Showroom Window Conversion Yield (SWCY)

When we analyzed commercial retail foot traffic across Victoria Island and Lekki, we discovered that peak affluent pedestrian density occurs between **7:30 PM and 11:00 PM**—hours after most retail stores have closed.

To quantify the cost of this missed opportunity, we engineered the **Showroom Window Conversion Yield (SWCY)**:

$$\text{SWCY} = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \left[ \text{EFT}_t \times \text{PFR} \times \text{QSR} \times \text{MCVR} \times \text{AOV} \times (1 - \text{MDR}) \right] - \text{COGS}$$

When **Casa Bella Living** applied this model to their Victoria Island showroom, the data was staggering:
* 8,500 evening pedestrians walked past their locked windows weekly.
* 1,275 paused to admire the featured display pieces.
* By turning those passive glances into dynamic QR scans with 1-click checkout, they unlocked **₦7,560,000 every month in previously invisible overnight revenue**.

That’s **₦90.7 Million per year** captured from closed-door foot traffic.

### The 3 Rules of the 24/7 QR Showroom Bridge

Turning window browsers into digital buyers requires more than taping a barcode to a window. You need a dedicated omnichannel bridge:

1. **Dynamic SKU Tokens (Not Generic Homepages)**: Scanning a dining table must take the buyer directly to that specific table's checkout page—with real-time dimensions, finish options, and local showroom stock.
2. **Zero-Friction 1-Click Mobile Checkout**: No forced 12-field account registration forms on a mobile screen. Enable Apple Pay, Google Pay, card, or instant bank transfer in under 15 seconds.

$$\text{NWII} = \frac{\text{Nighttime QR Scans (8:00 PM - 6:00 AM)}}{\text{Daytime Walk-In Inquiries}} \times 100$$

* **Tactic**: Feature high-margin accent pieces (lighting, designer mirrors, side tables) directly behind glass storefronts with dedicated illuminated QR stands. Test special 5% "Night Owl VIP" promotions between 8:00 PM and midnight to maximize scan-to-checkout conversion.
