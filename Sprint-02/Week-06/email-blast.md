### Four Rules to Immunize Your B2B Wholesale Balance Sheet

1. **Replace Verbal Trust with Dynamic Algorithmic Limits**:
   Never allow sales reps to grant credit based on personal relationships. Set automated purchasing caps tied to trailing 90-day settlement velocity. If a client slows down payment, their credit ceiling contracts automatically.

2. **Automate Hard-Lock Dispatch Scanners**:
   Human dispatch clerks can be persuaded or pressured. Warehouse management scanners cannot. When an invoice crosses 24 hours overdue, barcode picking lists for that buyer must be hard-locked at the loading dock gate.

3. **Deploy 1-to-1 Virtual Escrow Accounts**:
   Eliminate reconciliation ambiguity. Give every B2B customer a dedicated Ofia virtual account. The moment they pay, the payment matches directly to the exact open invoice, instantly unlocking their credit line.

4. **Incentivize 10-Day Cash Settlement**:
   Introduce dynamic early-payment rebates (e.g., 2% off if settled within 10 days). It costs you far less than carrying an overdue loan at 28% APR and frees up working capital to buy factory stock at bulk cash discounts.
