As documented in Punch Nigeria's report on the Nigerian Railway Corporation's warning that transport inefficiencies are raising the prices of goods (featured photo above), freight and haulage costs now represent one of the most volatile variables in every Nigerian wholesale transaction. Road congestion, diesel swings, and fragmented inter-city transport networks mean the cost of moving heavy pallets from Lagos to Abuja or Port Harcourt can shift significantly within a single month.

For multi-depot distributors still generating B2B quotes on disconnected spreadsheets, this volatility creates a double trap. First, waiting 24 to 48 hours to manually verify stock across Ikeja, Trade Fair, and regional depots causes impatient procurement committees to award urgent tenders to faster competitors. Second, quoting interstate deliveries using static, memorised freight rates silently destroys net profit before the truck even leaves the loading bay.

"Your warehouses may hold fifty million naira in ready stock, but if your quoting desk takes forty-eight hours to confirm availability and freight costs, your competitor wins the purchase order."
► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR WHOLESALE DIRECTORS & COMMERCIAL HEADS:

Connecting live multi-warehouse stock counts, customer pricing tiers, automated weight-by-zone haulage tables, and timed inventory holds into one quoting engine lets your team respond first—while protecting gross margin on every single pro-forma invoice.
