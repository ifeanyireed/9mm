Here is the most dangerous assumption in Nigerian business:
"We pay Company Income Tax to FIRS every year, so our company is in good standing."

Under Section 417 of CAMA 2020, every Nigerian registered company must file an Annual Return (Form CAC 19) accompanied by audited financial statements every single year.

What happens if you skip filings for two consecutive years?
1. Your public status on the CAC CRP database is marked "INACTIVE".
2. Compounding late penalties begin accumulating daily against both the company and each individual director under Section 422.
3. Commercial banks running automated Central Bank AML sweeps flag your profile, placing an automated Post-No-Debit (PND) on your operating accounts.
4. Your enterprise enters the administrative queue for striking off in the Federal Government Gazette under Section 692.

Clearing multi-year corporate arrears is not a matter of paying ₦5,000 and hoping for the best. It requires a structured, 4-step statutory roadmap:

Compile approved balance sheets or audited accounts for each unfiled calendar year. Crucially: under Section 119, you MUST concurrently file your Persons with Significant Control (PSC) beneficial ownership disclosures. Skip PSC, and your regularization filing will be queried instantly.
