In public sector contracting across Nigeria, securing a federal ministry contract can transform an engineering or supply firm's balance sheet for an entire multi-year cycle.

Contractors spend weeks assembling technical engineering designs, negotiating with international manufacturers, and securing commercial bank bid securities.

Yet, when tender opening sessions commence across federal ministries, departments, and agencies (MDAs) in Abuja, two out of every three submitted bids are eliminated before lunch.

Under Section 16(6) of the Public Procurement Act 2007 (PPA 2007), preliminary evaluation is binary. Evaluators have zero discretion to waive statutory documentation omissions. If a single statutory certificate (TCC, PENCOM, ITF, NSITF) is missing, expired, or inconsistent with your audited accounts, your technical envelope is rejected and your financial bid is returned unopened.

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR MANAGING DIRECTORS & BID MANAGERS: In federal procurement, competitive pricing means nothing if your bid fails preliminary screening. Evaluators eliminate non-responsive bids within sixty minutes. Government contractors must institutionalize an automated pre-bid audit to verify that all four statutory clearances and the BPP Interim Registration Report are 100% synchronized before sealing technical envelopes.

The Master Preliminary Document Checklist:

  1. CAC Status Report: Verified ACTIVE status on CRP database with current directors (CAMA 2020).
  2. FIRS Tax Clearance Certificate: 3-year TCC with turnover figures matching audited accounts.
  3. PENCOM Compliance Certificate: Valid through Dec 31st of the current year (minimum 5 staff).
  4. ITF Clearance (Form 5A): Official certificate confirming 1% payroll levy (receipt slips are rejected).
  5. NSITF Compliance Certificate: Valid ECA-01 clearance under Employees' Compensation Act.
  6. BPP Interim Registration Report: Active IRR with synchronized statutory expiry dates.
  7. 3-Year Audited Financial Statements: Certified by ICAN/ANAN auditors with FRCN numbers.
  8. Verifiable Project Experience: Letters of award accompanied by practical completion certificates.
  9. High Court Sworn Affidavit: Formally attested affidavit under PPA 2007 Section 16(6).
  10. Bank Credit Line Reference: Explicit facility commitment addressed specifically to the procuring entity.
