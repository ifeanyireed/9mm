Pulling a live CAC Electronic Status Report exposes three painful blindspots:

* **A Resigned Co-Founder Still on the Public Register:** Two years ago a co-founder retired abroad and an operations head became Executive Director by board resolution—yet nobody filed **Form CAC 7A** within 14 days under Section 321 of CAMA 2020, causing the bank's CAC API check to reject the active signatory's BVN.

Chief Raymond's group has audited revenues, real assets, and willing lenders. What it lacks is a synchronized post-incorporation governance system.

### 2. Regularize Director Appointments and Resignations Immediately (Form CAC 7A)

Unfiled resignations keep former directors legally exposed while blocking newly promoted executives from passing automated bank mandate checks.

Collect signed resignation letters, pass formal Board Resolutions, and file **Form CAC 7A** immediately—enforcing the strict **14-day statutory window** under Section 321 of CAMA 2020 for every future change.

**Why it matters:** Keeps legal board authority aligned with the public registry so signatory mandates clear without AML holds.

### 3. File Return of Share Allotments and Clear FIRS Stamp Duty (Form CAC 2A)

Handing investors internal agreements without CRP filing leaves equity legally unperfected during institutional due diligence.

Settle statutory **FIRS stamp duty** on all share capital increases or new equity issuances, then file **Form CAC 2A (Return of Allotment)** within 30 days so every shareholder appears cleanly on your Certified Status Report.

**Why it matters:** Turns informal equity promises into an auditable cap table recognized by courts and lenders.

### 4. Harmonize the Section 119 Persons with Significant Control (PSC) Register

Under Section 119 of CAMA 2020, every individual or entity holding **5% or more equity or voting control** must be declared on the national PSC register—and contradictions programmatically block annual returns.

Update your Section 119 PSC ledger alongside every Form CAC 2A allotment or share transfer with exact percentages and verified identities.

**Why it matters:** Prevents CRP portal lockouts during urgent filings and satisfies beneficial ownership rules.

### 6. Standardize Board Resolutions With Clean Formal Attestation

Over 45% of CAC post-incorporation filing queries stem from casually worded or improperly attested board resolutions, stalling mandate unblocks for weeks.

Draft every resolution on official letterhead showing exact RC number, date, statutory CAMA 2020 citations, and signatures of two active directors (or one director and the accredited Company Secretary).

**Why it matters:** Eliminates avoidable CRP rejection loops for rapid first-pass approval.

### 7. Reconcile Director BVN and NIN Records Across Commercial Banks

Even a missing middle name or inverted surname between a director's CAC record and NIBSS BVN triggers automated "DIRECTOR BVN MISMATCH" freezes.

Audit every director's CAC particulars against NIBSS BVN, NIMC NIN, and passport before submitting bank mandates, filing formal CAC corrections wherever discrepancies exist.

**Why it matters:** Stops false-positive AML account locks so payroll and vendor payments stay uninterrupted.

### 8. Regularize Cumulative Late-Filing Penalties in One Clean Batch

Overdue post-incorporation changes accrue daily administrative penalties under the Companies Regulations 2021.

Compute exact accrued penalties transparently, clear historical arrears in a single coordinated batch, and store all official Remita and CAC receipts inside your corporate vault.

**Why it matters:** Halts compounding daily fines and restores unblemished legal standing cleanly.

### 9. Synchronize FIRS and State IRS Tax Profiles

Updating the CAC is only half the cycle: if TaxPro-Max or LIRS still displays retired directors or old share capital, annual Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC) renewals stall.

Submit your approved CAC Status Report, Form CAC 7A, and Form CAC 2A to FIRS and State IRS within 14 days of approval so your TIN mirrors your corporate charter.

**Why it matters:** Guarantees smooth TCC renewals and zero-query tender eligibility.
