If you accept that cash, your business didn't make a profit. It committed a strict liability criminal offense.

Under Section 2 of the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 (MLA 2022):

• No corporate body may make or accept cash payments exceeding ₦10,000,000.
