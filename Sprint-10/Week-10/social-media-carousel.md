Official Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) data reveals a brutal statistic:
Over 65% of all bids submitted to Nigerian federal ministries are disqualified during preliminary evaluation—before the financial bid envelope is ever opened.

Contractors spend months conducting site inspections, calculating competitive bills of quantities (BOQ), and paying millions for bank guarantees.
Then their bid is eliminated in the first 30 minutes of the public bid opening.

Why? Because preliminary evaluation under the Public Procurement Act 2007 is 100% binary:
You either pass every statutory criterion, or you are marked "NON-RESPONSIVE" and thrown out on the spot.

Here are the 3 paperwork blunders that destroy federal tenders in Abuja:

1. The "Lawyer's Stamp" Affidavit Blunder
Under Section 16(6) of PPA 2007, every bidder must provide a sworn affidavit covering 5 mandatory limbs (including declaring that no BPP or procuring entity staff has any financial interest in your company).
Many contractors have their retained corporate lawyer draft the document and stamp it with an NBA seal.
Under Nigerian law (Oaths Act), a private lawyer cannot administer an oath to their own client. An affidavit MUST be sworn before a Commissioner for Oaths in a High Court or a recognized Notary Public.
An NBA stamp is not an oath. Result: Immediate disqualification.

2. The "Application Slip" Illusion
When ministerial adverts appear in national newspapers, contractors have only 6 weeks to submit.
Your PENCOM or ITF renewal is pending in Abuja, so you attach your Remita payment receipt and stamped acknowledgement letter to Envelope A.
BPP Circular BPP/R&M/08/Vol.I/01 explicitly forbids tenders boards from accepting application slips or payment receipts in place of certified compliance certificates.
The rule is absolute: No certified certificate = Disqualification.

3. The Three-Way Financial Contradiction
Tenders board evaluators place 3 documents side-by-side:
• Your 3-year Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC)
• Your 3-year Audited Financial Statements
• Your BPP National Database Profile (IRR)
If your tax accountant minimized turnover on TaxPro-Max to ₦380M, while your external auditor declared ₦450M to demonstrate high capacity, the ₦70M variance is flagged as conflicting statutory records. Disqualified for financial misrepresentation!
