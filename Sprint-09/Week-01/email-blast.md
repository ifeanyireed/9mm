### Cover Story: Corporate Governance & Statutory Enforcement
**HEADLINE: [100,000 Firms Risk Delisting as CAC Enforces CAMA 2020 Compliance](https://punchng.com/100000-firms-risk-delisting-as-cac-enforces-compliance/)**

* <img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https://cdn.punchng.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/24150114/WhatsApp-Image-2026-09-24-at-3.00.43-PM_1790258474.webp&w=800&output=jpg" alt="Corporate Affairs Commission and Private Sector Regulatory Summit" referrerpolicy="no-referrer" width="100%" />
* **Source**: Punch Nigeria · [Read Full Story →](https://punchng.com/100000-firms-risk-delisting-as-cac-enforces-compliance/)

**ABUJA & LAGOS** — The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) has placed approximately 100,000 registered companies on notice of imminent delisting and deregistration pursuant to Section 692 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA 2020). As captured in Punch Nigeria's coverage of national corporate regulatory proceedings (featured photo above), the commission's enforcement actions target enterprises that have failed to file statutory annual returns and beneficial ownership disclosures.

Under CAMA 2020, companies that remain dormant or delinquent on annual returns face total removal from the federal registry. The Registrar-General confirmed that once an entity is struck off, its corporate legal personality ceases to exist, rendering its assets and brand names vulnerable to forfeiture.

> "A company that fails to file annual returns is legally presumed to have ceased operations. We are sanitizing the corporate registry to protect investors and guarantee that only active, compliant legal entities maintain commercial banking privileges."

👉 **[Read full story at Punch Nigeria →](https://punchng.com/100000-firms-risk-delisting-as-cac-enforces-compliance/)**

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR MANAGING DIRECTORS & COMPANY SECRETARIES:**
> Do not wait for a bank account restriction to audit your corporate standing. Even if your business had zero commercial revenue during the year, CAMA 2020 mandates the filing of dormant annual returns. Regularizing your corporate status now eliminates compounding late penalties and insulates your banking relationships from sudden KYC freezes.
