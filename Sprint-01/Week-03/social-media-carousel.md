• 3 colorways
• 4 sizes (S, M, L, XL)

If you are tracking 72 SKUs with a ballpoint pen while managing Instagram DMs and WhatsApp chats, here is your mathematical reality:

Variant Drag = Orders × 14.2% Error Rate × Average Order Value.

1. Phantom Stockouts: Taking 10 minutes to verify if Size 10 Green is in the stockroom.
2. Double-Selling: Two customers paying for the last item on two different apps.
3. Packing Disconnect: Warehouses guessing sizes because payment screenshots lack itemized SKU details.
