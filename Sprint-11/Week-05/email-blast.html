EFCC and SCUML Expand Automated Surveillance on DNFBP Transactions

Source: Punch Nigeria · Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering Stakeholder Assembly in Abuja.

ABUJA — The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) and the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML) have expanded algorithmic surveillance across commercial banking rails and Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs). As captured in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national financial compliance proceedings (featured photo above), regulatory agencies are focusing on automated threshold reconciliation, PEP transactions, and transaction structuring.

"The regulatory net is now fully automated. When a commercial bank processes a ₦30,000,000 transfer, our goAML platform checks whether the receiving entity has filed a matching Currency Transaction Report. Discrepancies immediately trigger algorithmic flags and automated Post-No-Debit directives."
► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR HIGH-VALUE OPERATORS & DEVELOPERS:

Treating AML compliance as a framed paper certificate leaves your enterprise completely exposed. When high-value transactions settle, your business must submit symmetrical CTR filings within 7 days, screen clients against PEP databases, and reject structured payments. Automating this compliance workflow permanently protects your corporate bank accounts.
