### Cover Story: Enterprise Data Privacy & NDPA 2023 Governance
**HEADLINE: [NDPC Escalates Enforcement Against Data Controllers of Major Importance: Mandatory Registry Compliance and DPCO Audits](https://punchng.com/ndpc-escalates-enforcement-against-data-controllers-of-major-importance/)**

* <img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https://cdn.punchng.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/20162529/NDPC.jpg&w=800&output=jpg" alt="Nigeria Data Protection Commission Headquarters and Regulatory Stakeholders" referrerpolicy="no-referrer" width="100%" />
* **Source**: Punch Nigeria · [Read Full Story →](https://punchng.com/ndpc-escalates-enforcement-against-data-controllers-of-major-importance/)

**ABUJA** — The Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) has issued a nationwide regulatory directive mandating all commercial organizations categorized as Data Controllers of Major Importance (DCMI) to finalize statutory registration and file their annual Data Protection Audit Reports through licensed DPCOs before the statutory deadline. As captured in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national regulatory proceedings in Abuja (featured photo above), the Commission has established automated compliance tracking to identify non-compliant consumer platforms.

National Commissioner and CEO of the NDPC, Dr. Vincent Olatunji, highlighted the Commission's rigorous stance on digital rights and corporate compliance:

> "Data protection compliance in Nigeria has transitioned from voluntary guidance to strict statutory enforcement. Platforms processing the personal information of Nigerian citizens cannot operate in regulatory obscurity. Organizations deploying algorithms, biometric tools, and digital financial services must be officially registered on the national database and demonstrate active, continuous compliance through accredited DPCO audit certifications. Defaulting organizations face statutory enforcement orders and administrative penalties reaching 2% of gross annual turnover."

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR CTOs & DATA PROTECTION OFFICERS:**
> Managing data privacy through annual Word documents or static legal retainers guarantees eventual regulatory sanctions. Technology organizations must integrate automated privacy scanning into developer CI/CD pipelines, ensure all mobile tracking SDKs are consent-gated, and maintain a rolling compliance dossier with accredited DPCO partners.
