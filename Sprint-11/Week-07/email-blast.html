NFIU and Commercial Banks Deploy Direct API Bridge for Real-Time DNFBP Transaction Reporting

Source: Punch Nigeria · Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering Assembly in Abuja.

ABUJA — The Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) and commercial banking compliance committees have activated automated API bridges linking commercial banking switches directly to the goAML gateway for Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs). As reported in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national financial crime coordination proceedings in Abuja (featured photo above), the integrated network reconciles incoming funds with electronic threshold filings submitted by real estate developers and luxury merchants.

"The regulatory system no longer relies on periodic on-site inspections. When a commercial bank processes a high-value inflow, our central switch verifies whether the receiving merchant has transmitted a matching Currency Transaction Report. If seven days elapse without a corresponding report, automated risk directives mandate the bank to restrict account debits immediately."
► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR HIGH-VALUE OPERATORS & DEVELOPERS:

Treating anti-money laundering compliance as manual paperwork is a dangerous gamble. High-value operators must connect sales and invoicing directly to automated goAML XML engines, file CTRs within 24 hours of settlement, and dispatch verified filing receipts to their commercial banking switches to ensure permanent operating continuity.
