### Editor's Opening Letter

**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

Walk through Ikeja Industrial Estate, Alaba International Market, Trade Fair Complex, or Kano's commercial depots on any weekday morning, and you will see extraordinary commercial energy: thirty-ton flatbed trailers loading, forklifts moving hundreds of cartons, and eight-figure wholesale deals moving fast.

Yet look inside the dispatch office, and you will frequently find an astonishing contradiction: *multi-billion-Naira distribution networks still operating on carbon-copy waybill booklets, laminated price lists, and handwritten pallet bin-cards.*

Paper works through sheer human grit when you move ten bulk orders a day. The moment a distributor attempts to scale past fifty heavy truckloads a week, paper stops being a tracking record and becomes a rigid revenue ceiling. In this edition, we diagnose the hidden cost of paper waybills across Lagos port and wholesale corridors and lay out ten practical ways to modernize into digital B2B commerce.
