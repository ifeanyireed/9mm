**HEADLINE: [‘Heavy Freight Corridors Face Operational Strain’ — Fleet Logistics and Commercial Terminal Delays Impact Regional Supply Chains](https://punchng.com/inadequate-holding-bays-trigger-450-container-transport-surge/)**

**LAGOS** — Severe congestion, protracted physical vehicle gate inspections, and manual documentation bottlenecks across commercial freight corridors continue to penalize Nigerian distributors and haulage fleets, triggering extensive turnaround delays and escalating operating overheads.

As captured in Punch Nigeria's freight logistics investigation (featured photo above), commercial trailers face extended holding times outside reception depots because dispatch manifests and cargo delivery notes are processed manually on paper clipboards. When vehicles spend days waiting for manual gate clearances, fleet utilization drops precipitously while demurrage penalties and driver allowances drain distributor margins.

> *"When a 30-ton trailer sits idle outside a regional depot waiting for physical tally clerks to verify paper waybills, the distributor is losing money every hour. Without digital pre-clearance and instant mobile proof of delivery, wholesale logistics cannot scale."* — Commercial Haulage Operators Forum.

Supply chain economists warn that distributors who fail to connect their central warehouse dispatch systems directly to digital fleet telemetry absorb massive unrecoverable operational drag that severely impairs working capital liquidity.

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR OPERATORS:**  
> Relying on drivers to carry paper delivery notes across interstate corridors introduces an unavoidable 14 to 21-day reconciliation lag. If a client disputes four damaged cartons on a handwritten waybill, the entire invoice is frozen. Implementing mobile electronic proof of delivery with automated damage split-invoicing allows distributors to bill 99% of accepted stock immediately while eliminating dispatch-to-cash delays.
