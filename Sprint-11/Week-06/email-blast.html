NFIU and Commercial Banks Deploy Real-Time Telemetry to Combat DNFBP Laundering

Source: Punch Nigeria · Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering Stakeholder Assembly in Abuja.

ABUJA — The Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) in collaboration with commercial banking compliance committees has deployed automated real-time transaction reconciliation across Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs). As captured in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national financial compliance proceedings (featured photo above), the automated system cross-references transaction metadata submitted by commercial banks with electronic filings by luxury merchants and real estate developers.

"The regulatory net is now fully automated. When a commercial bank processes a ₦30,000,000 transfer, our goAML platform checks whether the receiving entity has filed a matching Currency Transaction Report. Discrepancies immediately trigger algorithmic flags and automated Post-No-Debit directives."
