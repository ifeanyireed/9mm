## The Busy Wholesale Depot That Was Losing Corporate Contracts

On a Tuesday morning, Bayo's electrical and building materials distribution depot in Ikeja is in full motion.

Forklifts are moving pallets of armoured cables, industrial switchgear and lighting fixtures across the loading bay. Sales representatives are answering phone inquiries, dispatch clerks are checking waybills, and haulage trucks are lining up outside the gate.

From the outside, the business looks like a thriving wholesale operation.

But Bayo has a problem.

A procurement manager from a major real estate construction firm sends a request for quotation (RFQ) for four thousand metres of industrial cable and sixty distribution boards. She needs a confirmed pro-forma invoice before close of business so her finance committee can approve payment.

Bayo's sales representative opens an Excel spreadsheet to prepare the quote.

Because the spreadsheet is not connected to the warehouses, the representative has to call the Ikeja, Trade Fair and Sagamu depot supervisors to confirm how many coils are actually sitting on the shelves. One supervisor is busy offloading a container and does not return the call until the next afternoon.

By the time Bayo's team sends the pro-forma invoice forty-eight hours later, the construction company has already paid a competing distributor who responded the same morning.

That same week, a junior sales clerk sends a bulk quote to a hotel developer in Abuja using last month's haulage rate. Diesel and transit costs have risen since then, and fulfilling the order wipes out nearly all of Bayo's profit margin.

Meanwhile, the accounts team spends hours searching through paper files and bank statements to confirm whether a returning corporate buyer has cleared their previous thirty-day credit balance before approving a new order.

Bayo begins to notice an uncomfortable pattern.

His business has quality products, multiple warehouses and serious corporate buyers. What it lacks is a connected quoting system.

The warehouses are working. The quoting desk is slowing everything down.

This is the challenge facing many growing wholesale distributors across Nigeria: their sales desks, warehouse stock records, haulage pricing and credit approvals are operating separately when they should be working together.

## The Real Problem: Your Sales Desk, Warehouse and Finance Team Are Working Separately Instead of Together

Consider Bayo's distribution business again.

His sales representatives know the customers. His warehouse supervisors know the stock. His transport coordinator knows the current haulage rates, and his accountant knows each buyer's credit history.

But these four parts of the business are not connected when a quote request arrives.

The sales desk calculates prices on a spreadsheet without seeing live warehouse stock.

The warehouse holds inventory across three depots, but nobody can reserve items automatically while a quote is awaiting approval.

The transport coordinator updates haulage rates on paper, but the sales team keeps quoting outdated delivery fees.

The finance team tracks customer credit in a separate ledger, delaying invoice approval while they verify past payments manually.

Every department is working hard, but the quoting process is slow, error-prone and disconnected.

This is why connected B2B quoting matters.

It means bringing tiered pricing, live multi-warehouse stock, haulage calculations and credit rules into one system so your team can turn an inquiry into a verified pro-forma invoice in minutes.

## 10 Ways to Connect Your Wholesale Quoting to Faster Enterprise Sales

### 1. Replace Manual Spreadsheet Price Lists with Automated Customer Pricing Tiers

When a corporate buyer requests a quote for fifty SKUs, looking up wholesale discounts across separate Excel tabs slows down your response and invites pricing mistakes.

One sales representative might accidentally apply a standard retail price to a longtime distributor, while another might grant a deep volume discount on a small order that does not qualify.

In `/erp/admin/inventory`, you can assign each corporate buyer to a specific pricing tier—such as Tier-1 Distributor, Regional Wholesaler or Project Contractor—with built-in volume brackets.

When a representative selects the customer and adds items to a quote, the system applies their approved contract pricing and volume discounts automatically.

**Why it matters:** Your sales team generates accurate, margin-protected price quotes in seconds without hunting through spreadsheets.

### 2. Connect Your Quoting Desk to Live Multi-Warehouse Inventory

Imagine Bayo's sales representative receives an urgent order for 2,000 bags of industrial adhesive.

Because the representative cannot see current stock levels from his desk, he spends hours calling warehouse supervisors in Ikeja and Sagamu while the customer waits.

Even worse, if he guesses that the stock is available and sends the quote, the company risks accepting payment for goods that were already sold that morning.

By connecting quoting directly to `/erp/admin/inventory/warehouses`, sales staff can see exact available pallet and bin counts across every depot on one screen before issuing a quote.

**Why it matters:** Representatives only quote stock that actually exists, eliminating embarrassing backorders and multi-day verification calls.

### 3. Place Automatic Timed Stock Holds on Quoted Inventory

In busy wholesale depots, two sales representatives often quote the same batch of stock to two different corporate buyers on the same afternoon.

If both buyers approve their pro-forma invoices the next morning, the warehouse suddenly does not have enough stock to fulfil both orders.

A connected B2B quoting workflow solves this by placing a temporary soft reserve on the quoted stock in `/erp/admin/inventory` for a set validity window, such as 24 or 48 hours.

If the buyer pays or confirms within that window, the stock is locked in for dispatch; if the quote expires unused, the units return to general availability automatically.

**Why it matters:** You protect confirmed buyers from stockouts without tying up warehouse inventory indefinitely on unconfirmed quotes.

### 4. Calculate Haulage and Delivery Fees Automatically by Weight and Destination

<img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https://c76c7bbc41.mjedge.net/wp-content/uploads/tc/2024/05/MOVAM.jpg&w=800&output=jpg" alt="Automated Goods in Transit Management and B2B Freight Calculation" referrerpolicy="no-referrer" loading="lazy" style="width: 100%; max-width: 720px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 16px 0;" />

Estimating delivery charges by guesswork or outdated rate sheets is one of the fastest ways for a wholesale distributor to lose money on a large order.

If a sales clerk underestimates the weight of a steel or cable shipment going from Lagos to Port Harcourt, the extra haulage cost comes straight out of the company's profit margin.

By linking SKU weight and volume data in `/erp/admin/inventory` with regional freight rate tables in `/erp/admin/logistics/dispatch`, the system calculates the exact haulage surcharge as soon as the delivery destination is entered.

The customer sees a clear, itemised delivery fee on their pro-forma invoice right away.

**Why it matters:** Every quote reflects real transport costs, protecting your profit margin from fuel and freight surprises.

### 5. Set Automatic Margin Floor Rules for Sales Manager Approvals

When a corporate buyer asks for an extra discount to close a large deal, waiting all day for the managing director to review the numbers on paper can cost you the sale.

At the same time, allowing sales representatives to slash prices freely without oversight can quietly erode your gross margins.

A connected quoting system lets management set clear gross-margin floors for every product category in `/erp/admin/inventory`.

Quotes that stay within approved margin limits are issued immediately, while quotes that dip below the floor trigger an instant mobile approval notification for the commercial director.

**Why it matters:** Standard quotes go out without delay, while special enterprise discounts get fast, controlled executive approval.

### 6. Automate Customer Credit Limit Checks Before Issuing Net-Term Quotes

Many corporate buyers and regional distributors purchase on 14-day or 30-day credit terms rather than paying full cash upfront.

When credit records sit in an isolated accounting file, sales representatives often waste hours preparing quotes for accounts that have already exceeded their credit limit or have overdue invoices.

Connecting the quoting screen to `/erp/accountant/ledger` allows the system to check a customer's live credit limit and aging balance automatically.

If an account is in good standing, net-term quoting proceeds smoothly; if an overdue balance exists, the system alerts the representative immediately and switches the quote to payment-before-dispatch terms.

**Why it matters:** Sales and finance stay aligned on credit risk without hours of manual ledger checks.

### 7. Replace Paper Pro-Forma Invoices with Interactive Digital Quotes

Sending a static PDF attachment or a photographed paper quote forces the buyer to print, sign, scan and email documents back before an order can move forward.

If the buyer wants to adjust a single line item—for example, changing 300 units to 250 units—the entire document has to be recreated from scratch.

With digital B2B quotes linked to your `/shop` wholesale portal, the buyer receives a secure link they can open on their phone or laptop.

They can review live pricing, approve the quote with a single tap and download their official pro-forma invoice immediately.

**Why it matters:** Removing paperwork friction makes it easier for busy procurement managers to approve your quote first.

### 8. Link Approved Quotes Directly to Payment Confirmation and Order Conversion

Even after a customer accepts a pro-forma invoice and makes a bank transfer, many distributors lose another day waiting for accounts to match the payment alert to the right quote.

During that delay, the warehouse cannot begin picking the order, and the customer is left calling the sales representative for updates.

When every digital pro-forma invoice carries a unique payment reference linked to `/erp/accountant/ledger`, incoming payments reconcile against the quote automatically.

The moment payment or approved credit is confirmed, the quote converts into an active sales order without manual re-entry.

**Why it matters:** Orders move from payment to warehouse picking in minutes instead of sitting in an email inbox.

### 9. Split Multi-Depot Fulfilment Automatically When One Warehouse Is Short

Suppose a corporate contractor orders 1,000 lighting panels, and Bayo has 600 units in Ikeja and 400 units in his Trade Fair depot.

In a manual operation, coordinating a split order across two locations requires multiple phone calls, separate paper waybills and constant follow-up to ensure nothing is missed.

A connected warehouse system in `/erp/admin/inventory/warehouses` handles multi-depot allocation automatically when the quote converts to an order.

It generates separate pick lists for Ikeja and Trade Fair while keeping the customer's invoice and delivery tracking unified under one master order.

**Why it matters:** You can fulfil large enterprise orders across multiple depots smoothly without confusing your warehouse teams or your buyer.

### 10. Give Returning Corporate Buyers a Self-Service Reorder Portal

Your best wholesale customers—such as retail chains, factories and facility managers—frequently reorder the same core supplies every month.

Forcing them to call a sales representative and wait for a manual quote every time they need a routine restock slows down their purchasing and ties up your sales desk.

By setting up approved corporate accounts on your `/shop` B2B portal, returning buyers can log in, see their negotiated tier prices, check live depot availability and generate their own pro-forma invoices at any hour.

Your sales team can then focus their energy on winning new large tenders and managing complex custom projects.

**Why it matters:** Routine wholesale reorders happen automatically around the clock while your sales team focuses on high-value accounts.
