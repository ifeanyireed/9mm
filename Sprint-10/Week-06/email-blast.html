As documented in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national procurement proceedings (featured photo above), the evaluation committees placed unprecedented emphasis on verifiable statutory compliance, contractor BPP categorization, and synchronized financial turnover.

"The contractors who secured awards in this cycle were not those who simply wrote attractive proposals. They were the firms that demonstrated complete statutory responsiveness from day one. Institutional compliance is the true determinant of procurement success."
► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR CONTRACTORS & BIDDERS:

Winning public tenders requires institutional compliance discipline. Never submit a tender package without auditing your Quad certificates against the live BPP database, reconciling financial turnover across FIRS and audited statements, and verifying professional council licenses for all technical personnel.
