### Editor's Note
**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

In the early days of building a wholesale distribution business, managing dispatches on paper feels tangible and controllable. You write the carbon-copy waybill, stamp the yellow duplicate for the driver, and file the pink slip in a binder.

At five trucks a week, manual diligence keeps things moving.

At fifty trucks a week, paper stops being an accounting tool. Paper becomes an operational ceiling that strangles your cash flow.

When your trailers sit idle at loading gates during peak morning rush hours waiting for handwritten paperwork, and enterprise clients freeze eight-figure payments over disputed handwritten delivery slips, your business has hit the **Paper Ceiling**.

This week, we break down the three unmistakable warning signs that your wholesale network has outgrown analog methods—and how digital dispatch manifests restore operational speed.
