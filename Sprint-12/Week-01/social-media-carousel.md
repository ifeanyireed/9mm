Here is the hard regulatory truth every Nigerian CTO, General Counsel, and Managing Director must confront:

A website privacy policy is not compliance. It is merely a notice.

Under the NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID), if your systems store personal data for more than 1,000 Nigerian citizens, you are legally classified as a Data Controller of Major Importance (DCMI).

1. You must maintain an automated Record of Processing Activities (RoPA).

3. You must provide a verified 72-hour Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) deletion pipeline.
4. You must conduct an independent data protection audit through an accredited DPCO and submit certified returns to the NDPC by MARCH 15th annually.

Missing the March 15 audit cutoff does not just mean administrative fines up to ₦10M or 2% of your annual turnover.

It lands your enterprise on the National Non-Compliance Register, prompting tier-1 commercial banks and payment switches to disconnect your production API credentials to protect their own regulatory covenants.
