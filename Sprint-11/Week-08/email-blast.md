### Part 3: Executive Implementation Blueprint (The 5 Boardroom AML Questions)

Table these 5 questions at your next quarterly board review:

1. **"Are We Accepting Cash Payments Exceeding Section 2 Statutory Thresholds?"**
   * *The Standard*: Absolute ban on cash above ₦5M for individuals or ₦10M for corporate bodies. Splitting cash deposits into smaller tranches is a statutory felony under Section 2(2).
2. **"Is Our NFIU goAML Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR) Automated or Handled Manually?"**
   * *The Standard*: All transactions exceeding ₦5M (Individual) or ₦10M (Corporate) must be reported to the NFIU within 7 calendar days. Manual spreadsheets inevitably miss deadlines, triggering regulatory sanctions.
3. **"Do We Screen Every High-Net-Worth Buyer Against Real-Time PEP & Sanctions Lists?"**
   * *The Standard*: Mandatory automated screening of buyers against Nigerian (NFIU) and global watchlists (UN, OFAC) before contracts are executed. Documented Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) required for Politically Exposed Persons.
4. **"Has Our SCUML Biometric Registration Been Synchronized Within the Last 12 Months?"**
   * *The Standard*: Registered directorship and bank signatory data must match CAC CAMA 2020 registers 100%. Discrepancies cause commercial bank compliance desks to freeze accounts on PND.
5. **"Do We Provide Documented Annual AML/CFT Training to Our Sales and Frontline Staff?"**
   * *The Standard*: Annual certified compliance training is mandatory under Section 11(2). Missing employee training logs are the #1 reason companies fail SCUML on-site inspections.
