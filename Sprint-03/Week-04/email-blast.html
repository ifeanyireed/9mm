Let us talk about an uncomfortable truth in modern e-commerce. For years, independent merchants were told that horizontal aggregator marketplaces were their saviors. List your products, get discovered by millions of buyers, and let the platform handle the rest.

Today, merchants carry the inventory risk and storage costs while aggregators extract a 15% to 20% commission on gross sales—while holding merchant payouts for 21 days in "fraud auditing" lock. In categories where gross margins hover around 20%, platform commissions devour up to 95% of net operating profit. Here is how sovereign marketplace infrastructure solves the multi-party split-payout bottleneck.
