As illustrated in Punch Nigeria's coverage of Ecobank's Lagos commercial enterprise initiative engaging 150 scaling brands (featured photo above), Tier-1 commercial banks are actively expanding corporate commerce, merchant settlement, and trade finance ecosystems across Nigeria. Yet behind every modern corporate banking relationship sits an automated AML Know-Your-Customer engine connected straight to the CAC and NIBSS BVN registries.

Under Sections 119, 321, and 722 of CAMA 2020, unrecorded director changes (Form CAC 7A), unfiled share allotments (Form CAC 2A), mismatched Section 119 PSC registers, and outdated office addresses (Form CAC 3) routinely trigger sudden "DIRECTOR BVN MISMATCH" debit restrictions—paralyzing payroll and vendor settlements overnight.

"In modern Nigerian commerce, signed board minutes inside an Ikoyi conference room do not protect your bank account. Only formally approved filings on your live CAC Electronic Status Report keep debit mandates and credit lines moving."

Pull your live CAC Electronic Status Report today and reconcile every director name, BVN/NIN record, issued shareholding, PSC disclosure, and office address before approaching a commercial bank for mandate updates or credit drawdowns.
