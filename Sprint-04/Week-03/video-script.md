## Dual-Track Master Production Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] Cold Open: The Dead-End Showroom (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: Close-up on IFEANYI standing in an expansive, luxury furniture showroom. Three hundred customers walk through the aisles over a weekend. Only twelve buy on the spot.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"You pay forty-five million naira a year for rent on Victoria Island.
Your showroom is beautiful. Three hundred affluent people walk through your doors every single Saturday.
They touch the Italian leather, test the oak dining table, take a photo with their phone...
And then they walk out the door.
What do you give them? A paper business card.
No digital link. No saved cart. No way to browse whether the grey velvet finish is in stock when they get home to discuss with their spouse.
Welcome to the Showroom Dead End—where 82% of your physical store foot traffic evaporates into thin air."

---

### [0:25 – 1:15] Scene 1: The High (The Saturday Afternoon Foot Traffic)
**[VISUAL]**: Casa Bella Living showroom. Ronke is admiring a modern 6-seater solid oak dining table. Yemi approaches with a warm smile.

**YEMI**:
"Good afternoon! Beautiful piece, isn’t it? Handcrafted kiln-dried oak with brass inlay. ₦1,450,000."

**RONKE** *(stroking the wood)*:
"I love it. The dimensions look perfect for our new apartment in Ikoyi. Do you have it in walnut finish, and what are the exact clearance measurements for the chairs?"

**YEMI**:
"Let me check our paper product folder at the front desk. And here is my business card! Just call the number on Monday morning and we can confirm availability."

**[VISUAL]**: Yemi hands Ronke a white printed card. Ronke slips it into her handbag.

---

### [1:15 – 2:40] Scene 2: The Crash (The Evaporated Customer)
**[VISUAL]**: Sunday evening, 8:30 PM. Ronke and her husband sit on their living room sofa reviewing home decor.

**RONKE** *(searching her handbag)*:
"Honey, where is that business card the lady gave me yesterday? I want to show you the oak table."

**HUSBAND**:
"Why don't you just look at their website?"

**RONKE** *(typing on phone)*:
"I searched their brand name on Google. Their website is just a static 'About Us' landing page with no prices, no product catalog, and no stock indicators. It says: 'Visit our showroom in Victoria Island to view our collection'."

**HUSBAND**:
"I’m not driving through Sunday traffic to check a dining table. Look at this competitor’s site: 3D room preview, exact dimensions, walnut finish in stock, 1-tap checkout. Let's order this one instead."

**[VISUAL]**: Ronke taps *Order Now* on the competitor’s site. The paper business card sits forgotten at the bottom of her handbag.

**[VISUAL]**: Split-screen display:
* *Left Screen (Yemi's Showroom Dead End)*: Yemi arriving at her quiet showroom on Monday morning, staring at zero sales inquiries, wondering why 300 weekend visitors produced zero reorders.
* *Right Screen (Ofia Digital Showroom Engine)*: A modern showroom using **Ofia Omnichannel** (`client_slug.ofia.shop`). Every furniture piece has an interactive QR plaque. Walk-in shoppers scan the plaque with their phone camera to instantly view 360-degree finishes, download PDF dimension sheets, and save the item directly to a digital wishlist with their WhatsApp number. The showroom's automated CRM triggers a personalized follow-up on Sunday evening, closing 42% of weekend visitors.

### [2:40 – 3:20] Talking Head: The Mathematical Cost of Showroom Churn
**[VISUAL]**: Cut to IFEANYI in the analytical studio. On-screen graphic illuminates the Physical Store Churn Equation.

$$\text{Lost Showroom Equity} = \text{Monthly Walk-In Visitors} \times \text{Digital Churn Rate (82\%)} \times \text{Repeat AOV}$$

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Look at the mathematics of premium physical retail.
If your showroom attracts **600 qualified visitors per month**, but you capture zero digital contact data:
**82% of those visitors will never return**.
That represents **492 lost customer relationships every single month**.
At an average furniture or homeware order value of **₦450,000**:
That is over **₦200 million in potential pipeline value** walking out your front door and buying from digital competitors.
A physical showroom is the ultimate top-of-funnel customer acquisition channel—but only if it connects to a digital capture engine."
