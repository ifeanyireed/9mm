SCUML and EFCC Step Up Nationwide Inspections of Real Estate Developers and Luxury Auto Merchants

Photo: Regulatory taskforce operatives and financial crime investigators inspect commercial records in Lagos. DNFBPs operating without valid SCUML certificates face immediate bank account freezes. Source: Punch Nigeria.

LAGOS & ABUJA — As documented in Punch Nigeria’s regulatory enforcement report (featured photo above), the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML) under the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) has expanded compliance audits targeting Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs).

Under Sections 6 and 30 of the Money Laundering Act 2022, operating a high-value real estate, automotive, or consulting enterprise without an authentic SCUML certificate is a criminal violation. Banks have been directed to freeze outward transactions for delinquent entities immediately.

"High-value non-financial sectors must not serve as conduits for illicit capital. Enterprises accepting sums above ₦5M for individuals or ₦10M for corporations must log timely CTRs and maintain verifiable KYC registers. Non-compliance results in immediate operational suspension."

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR PROPERTY DEVELOPERS, CAR DEALERS & PARTNERS:

A bank account freeze in the middle of a major project halts contractor payroll and damages commercial credibility. Deploying automated transaction monitoring and certifying front-office staff on AML red flags insulates your business from regulatory interdiction.
