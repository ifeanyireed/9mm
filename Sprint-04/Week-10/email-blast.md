**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

Scaling a retail business from one showroom to three is hard work, but manageable.

* **Regional Inventory Islands**: Abuja is screaming for velvet sectionals that are sitting unsold in Port Harcourt.

Instead of your profits multiplying by 10X, your margins get crushed by administrative overhead. 

Welcome to **The Complexity Trap**.
