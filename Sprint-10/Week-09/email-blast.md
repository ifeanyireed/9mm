The most heartbreaking reality in Nigerian procurement is that these disqualifications are almost never caused by engineering flaws. They are caused by administrative oversight:
• Submitting an ITF application payment slip instead of the certified Form 5A clearance letter.
• A ₦15,000 variance in monthly pension remittances stalling the issuance of a certified PENCOM compliance letter.
• A mismatch between the annual turnover stated on your FIRS TCC and your 3-year audited financial statements.
• An un-notarized BPP sworn affidavit.

Under PPA 2007, evaluation committees possess zero discretion to grant waivers for statutory documentation omissions. If an envelope fails a single preliminary check, it is formally disqualified and returned unopened.

### Part 3: Executive Implementation Blueprint (The Master Preliminary Checklist)

Verify these 10 mandatory preliminary artifacts before sealing Envelope A:

1. **CAC Legal Standing**: Certified Status Report issued within the current fiscal year showing **STATUS: ACTIVE** (CAMA 2020).
2. **FIRS Tax Clearance Certificate**: Covering the past 3 consecutive years; turnover must match audited financial statements exactly.
3. **PENCOM Compliance Certificate**: Valid through December 31st of the bidding year, covering minimum 5 staff with group life insurance.
4. **ITF Compliance Certificate**: Certified Form 5A clearance letter confirming 1% payroll levy contribution (receipt slips are strictly rejected).
5. **NSITF Compliance Certificate**: Valid ECA-01 clearance certificate under the Employees' Compensation Act 2010.
6. **BPP National Database Registration**: Active Interim Registration Report (IRR) with updated statutory clearance expiry dates.
7. **Audited Financial Statements**: 3 past consecutive years, stamped by ICAN/ANAN auditors with active FRCN registration numbers.
8. **Verifiable Past Experience**: Minimum 3 verifiable letters of award with corresponding practical completion certificates or interim valuation certificates (IPC).
9. **High Court Sworn Affidavit**: Formally deposed at a Federal or State High Court confirming non-conviction, solvency, and lack of conflict of interest.
10. **Bank Reference / Credit Line Commitment**: Explicit credit availability commitment addressed specifically to the procuring entity.
