Why? 

Because the Lekki Mathematics teacher had modified the Continuous Assessment curve on his personal laptop spreadsheet without Senate approval—weighting class tests at 40% instead of the official 20%.

Because the campuses operated on disconnected spreadsheets, her first-term Continuous Assessment scores never ported over. Her report card displayed a zero for first term, wrongfully dropping her from 1st place to 14th in the overall class ranking.
