# 🎬 Video Script: "The Walk-In Ghost"

* **Sprint & Week**: Sprint 04 (Omnichannel Retail) · Week 01 (The Core Problem)
* **Format**: Talking Head (TH) cut with Drama Scenes (DRAMA)
* **Target Runtime**: ~4:15 (618 spoken words)
* **Cast**: Host (TH), Funke (Showroom General Manager), Mrs. Adele (High-Net-Worth Customer), Cashier (Cameo), Smart Retailer (Cameo)
* **Brand in the Skit**: Royale Home & Living (a premier multi-floor furniture and decor showroom in Lekki)
* **Target Audience Focus**: Multi-branch retailers, furniture showrooms, electronics superstores, supermarket owners
* **Product Pitched in CTA**: **Ofia POS Terminal & Enterprise ERP** (`https://www.ofia.ng/products`)

---

## Script Breakdown

### COLD OPEN: TH (0:00 – 0:25)

**Visual**: Host standing in front of a sleek retail checkout counter holding a heavy, printed physical sales receipt roll that unfurls and drops onto the floor.

**HOST**:
Here is a riddle that keeps retail executives awake at night:

You have five hundred 5-star Google reviews.
Your showroom parking lot is full every Saturday.
You just rang up thirty million naira in walk-in sales.

So why are your online sales sitting at absolute zero?

*(Host looks at the long paper receipt.)*

Because the second your customer walks out your front door, they become an anonymous ghost to your business.

**SFX**: Cash drawer chime, followed by the wind whistling through an empty street.

---

## SCENE 1: THE IN-STORE TRIUMPH (0:25 – 1:15)

**Visual**: Inside the bright, luxurious Lekki showroom of Royale Living. Soft jazz plays. Spotlights illuminate velvet sofas and dining sets. Funke, the GM, is warmly ushering Mrs. Adele to the checkout counter.

**FUNKE** *(beaming)*:
Mrs. Adele, that emerald velvet armchair is magnificent. It will look incredible in your living room!

**MRS. ADELE** *(swiping card on POS)*:
It’s gorgeous, Funke! I’m going to need the matching three-seater sofa and the two marble side tables once my renovation is done next week.

**CASHIER**:
Payment approved, ma! ₦450,000. Here is your thermal paper receipt!

**MRS. ADELE**:
Thank you! Can I just reorder the matching pieces on your website when I’m ready?

**FUNKE**:
Oh absolutely, ma! Check our website or just send a WhatsApp message to our info line!

**Visual**: Mrs. Adele smiles, leaves a 5-star Google review on her phone right outside the door, and drives off. Funke claps her hands in triumph.

**SFX**: Cheerful upbeat jazz.

---

## SCENE 2: THE MIDNIGHT ATTEMPT (1:15 – 2:40)

**Visual**: Two weeks later. 10:30 PM on a Tuesday. Mrs. Adele sits in bed with her iPad, ready to buy the matching ₦1.8M sofa set.

**MRS. ADELE** *(typing on iPad)*:
Okay, Royale Living website... let’s find the matching emerald sofa.

**Visual**: iPad screen view:
- The website displays a generic photo grid.
- Under the emerald sofa: *"Call for stock availability"*.
- The website inventory shows: *"Out of Stock"*.

**MRS. ADELE** *(sighing)*:
Out of stock? But Funke told me they had four in the warehouse. Let me try their WhatsApp line.

**Visual**: Mrs. Adele sends a WhatsApp message: *"Hello, I bought the armchair two weeks ago. I want the matching 3-seater sofa. Do you have stock?"*

**Visual**: Automated bot reply pops up: *"Thank you for contacting Royale Living. Our business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We will respond tomorrow."*

**MRS. ADELE** *(frustrated)*:
I want to pay right now. I don’t want to wait until tomorrow afternoon.

**Visual**: She opens Google, searches the sofa manufacturer, and lands on *Smart Retailer’s* online store (`decorhub.ofia.shop`).
The product page shows:
- Exact color match: Emerald Velvet.
- Real-time showroom stock: *2 units available at Ikeja, 1 at Lekki*.
- Instant checkout button with Moniepoint/Paystack.

**Visual**: Mrs. Adele taps "Pay Now". Order confirmed in 20 seconds.

**Visual**: Cut to Funke the next morning at 10:00 AM, arriving at the showroom. She checks the WhatsApp phone. 

**FUNKE** *(typing)*:
"Good morning ma, yes we have two in stock!"

**Visual**: Two blue checkmarks appear. No response. Mrs. Adele’s ₦1.8M order is gone forever.

**SFX**: Melancholy music. Host reappears.

---

## TH: THE MATH (2:40 – 3:20)

**Visual**: Host at desk with interactive retail data graphic.

**HOST**:
Physical retail foot traffic without digital continuity is a leaky bucket.

Retail research shows that **82% of walk-in shoppers** intend to make secondary or replenishment purchases online from brands they visited physically.

$$\text{Omnichannel Revenue Drag} = (\text{Daily Walk-Ins} \times 82\%\text{ Churn}) \times \text{Repeat AOV}$$

**On-screen text**:
- 200 walk-ins/day × 82% uncaptured = 164 lost buyers
- At ₦35,000 repeat AOV = ₦5,740,000 lost potential revenue every single day
- The friction: Showroom POS doesn't talk to the online inventory.

When your in-store POS register does not capture the customer's digital profile, phone number, and purchase history, you force that buyer to restart their customer journey from scratch every time they want to buy.

High foot traffic masks a broken digital checkout.
