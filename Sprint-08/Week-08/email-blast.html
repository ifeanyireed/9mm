National Economic Council Directs States to Deploy Digital Cockpits for CapEx Education Monitoring

Photo: National Economic Council directs states to deploy digital tracking cockpits. Source: Punch Nigeria / National Economic Council.

ABUJA — The National Economic Council (NEC) has advised state governments to institute digital capital expenditure tracking systems and ministerial telemetry cockpits to monitor ongoing educational infrastructure disbursements across all 774 Local Government Areas. As documented in the national policy summit proceedings (featured photo above, Source: Punch Nigeria / National Economic Council), the resolution follows a multi-state governance review revealing that over 40% of public classroom renovation projects approved between 2023 and 2025 suffered project abandonment or substandard delivery due to lack of real-time geotagged oversight. Under the new frameworks, state ministries are integrating satellite-geotagged inspection reports and edge biometric gate feeds directly into executive cockpits, ensuring that contractor disbursements only unlock upon verifiable physical completion.
