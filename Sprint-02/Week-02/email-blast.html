‘Heavy Freight Corridors Face Operational Strain’ — Fleet Logistics and Commercial Terminal Delays Impact Regional Supply Chains

Photo: Heavy commercial freight logistics and fleet terminal operations. Source: Punch Nigeria.

LAGOS — Severe congestion, protracted physical vehicle gate inspections, and manual documentation bottlenecks across commercial freight corridors continue to penalize Nigerian distributors and haulage fleets, triggering extensive turnaround delays and escalating operating overheads.

As captured in Punch Nigeria's freight logistics investigation (featured photo above), commercial trailers face extended holding times outside reception depots because dispatch manifests and cargo delivery notes are processed manually on paper clipboards.
