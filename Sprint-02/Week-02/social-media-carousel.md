### Long-Form Thought Leadership Post
```text
In wholesale distribution, revenue isn't earned when goods leave the warehouse.
It is earned when the buyer’s finance department verifies delivery.

If your distribution business moves ₦300M a month, but your drivers take 18 days to bring back signed paper waybills from regional depots, here is your mathematical reality:

Working Capital Drag = Monthly GMV × (Reconciliation Lag ÷ 30) × (Annual Interest Rate ÷ 12)

₦300M × (18 ÷ 30) = ₦180,000,000 in floating unbilled capital.
At 24% annual borrowing rates, that paper delay costs your business ₦3.6M in pure bank interest every single month.

Why? Because 26.8% of paper-based wholesale invoices are delayed due to:
• Grease-stained, illegible delivery slips
• Disputed carton damages with zero photo proof
• Drivers on long-haul routes carrying the only copy of the receipt

Top-tier distributors don't wait for trucks to return.
They link warehouse bin picking directly to mobile electronic Proof of Delivery (ePOD).

1. Barcode scanning at loading docks deducts warehouse bin inventory in real time.
2. Drivers capture digital signatures and photo damage proof on glass at customer depots.
3. Clean, verified electronic invoices arrive in the buyer's ERP in under 4 seconds.
```
