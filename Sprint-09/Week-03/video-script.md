## SCENE 3: THE STRATEGIC TAKEAWAY (2:40 – 3:35)

**Visual**: Cut back to Host in the studio.

**HOST**:
What happened to Apex Maritime happens to thousands of Nigerian companies every month.

Under CAMA 2020:
* **The 14-Day Clock**: Director appointments and resignations must be filed within 14 days.
* **The Stamp Duty Mandate**: New share allotments must be stamped by FIRS and filed on Form CAC 2A within 30 days.
* **The PSC Synchronization**: Every shareholding change must be reflected on the national Section 119 beneficial ownership register.

When you make internal changes without filing them on the CAC portal, you create a "Phantom Board" that paralyzes your banking channels.

When you maintain synchronized post-incorporation filings, your company operates with institutional authority.
