### Lead News Cover Story: Breaking News · In-Store Payment Infrastructure
**Nigerian Fintechs Roll Out 250,000 Next-Gen SmartPOS Terminals as Major Retail Chains Phase Out Dial-Up Card Machines**

**LAGOS** — In an industry-wide infrastructure modernization reported by commercial banking authorities this week, leading payment aggregators including Moniepoint, OPay, and PalmPay have accelerated the nationwide replacement of legacy dial-up POS machines with touchscreen Android SmartPOS devices across more than 250,000 commercial retail counters.

The transition comes as major supermarkets, fashion houses, and luxury furniture showrooms seek to eliminate painful end-of-day reconciliation delays and capture customer contact information at checkout. According to industry audit figures released by the Retailers Association of Nigeria, legacy standalone card machines caused average checkout delays of 4.2 minutes per customer, while over 91% of walk-in transactions generated unlinked thermal paper slips that were discarded immediately.

> *"A standalone card reader that merely prints an anonymous paper receipt is obsolete. Modern retailers require a terminal that captures customer phone numbers in three seconds, issues a digital SMS receipt, and automatically deducts the inventory item from both the showroom floor and the e-commerce website."* — Tunde Ogundipe, Head of Merchant Acquiring.

Retail chains deploying the new touchscreen terminals reported reducing evening cashier reconciliation from two hours to under 90 seconds, while interactive SMS receipts drove an immediate 28% increase in secondary online reorders from walk-in shoppers within 21 days of purchase.

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR RETAILERS:**  
> SmartPOS devices are no longer just payment readers; they are mobile ERP terminals. If your in-store checkout fails to capture a customer profile and synchronize floor inventory in real time, you are treating high-paying walk-in customers as anonymous one-time visitors. Unifying your physical counter with your digital catalog is the key to unlocking consistent secondary reorders.
