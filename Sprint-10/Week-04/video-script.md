## SCENE 1: THE TENDER OPENING HALL (0:30 – 1:35)

**Visual**: Federal Ministry of Works auditorium in Mabushi, Abuja. Large banners: *"PUBLIC BID OPENING: HIGHWAY EXPANSION LOT 4"*. Over a hundred contractors in suits and native attire are seated nervously. At the high table, Dr. Usman, the Director of Procurement, sits with evaluation officers and civil society observers.

**DR. USMAN** *(speaking into a microphone, stern and methodical)*:
Bidder Number 22: Apex Civil Engineering. 
Let us examine the mandatory preliminary statutory documents. 
PenCom compliance certificate declared 14 employees. 
Audited financial statements declare 48 full-time personnel. 
Discrepancy in violation of Section 16(6) of the Public Procurement Act. 
**Non-responsive. Disqualified.**

**Visual**: An executive in the audience puts his head in his hands.

**DR. USMAN**:
Bidder Number 23: Kaduna Paving Consortium. 
Industrial Training Fund certificate submitted expired on December 31st.

**Non-responsive. Disqualified.**

**DR. USMAN**:
Bidder Number 24: Lekki Infrastructure Consortium.
Let us query the Bureau of Public Procurement database.

The auditorium projection screen flashes green: *"BPP REGISTRATION: ACTIVE · PENCOM: VERIFIED (45 STAFF) · ITF: VERIFIED (₦145M PAYROLL) · NSITF: VERIFIED · FIRS TCC: CONFIRMED MATCH"*.

**DR. USMAN** *(nodding with approval)*:
Lekki Infrastructure Consortium: 100% mathematical and statutory compliance across all Quad parameters. Certified fully responsive. Proceeding to financial bid evaluation!
