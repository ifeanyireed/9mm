**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

Manual operational friction is the silent killer of institutional scale and distributor margins.

Tuition revenue is the lifeblood of private educational institutions. Unlike conventional commercial enterprises that maintain flexible payment terms or rolling inventory cycles, private schools operate on rigid, non-negotiable fixed operational overheads: faculty payroll must clear promptly, campus generators must run twelve hours daily, and campus suppliers demand strict settlement.

Yet in hundreds of private school networks across West Africa, bursary operations remain dangerously manual: parents submit doctored bank transfer receipts that slip past busy clerks, and direct deposits sit unallocated for weeks.
