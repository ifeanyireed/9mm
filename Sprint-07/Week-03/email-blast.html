EdTech Retention Study Reveals 48.6% of Online Student Support Tickets Stem from Local Setup Errors

Photo: Technical learning classroom. In-browser cloud IDEs eliminate terminal configuration errors and elevate cohort retention above 90%. Source: Digital Education Science Review.

NAIROBI & LAGOS — A comprehensive analysis across 320 online programming and data academies documented that nearly half of all student dropouts occur within the first 14 days, triggered by local operating system dependency errors.

Institutions deploying browser-based cloud computing sandboxes with pre-configured development tools and 1-click verifiable credentials have eliminated setup friction entirely, driving course completion rates from 18% to over 91%.

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR OPERATORS

Never require beginners to configure complex terminal packages on local laptops. Provide zero-install browser IDEs and autograded exercises that allow learners to experience immediate technical success on Day 1.
