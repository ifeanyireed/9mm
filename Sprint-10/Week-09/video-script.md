### ACT II: TH — THE STATUTORY QUAD RULES (1:35 – 2:40)

**Visual**: Host walks across the stage. A high-tech digital graphic breaks down the 10-point preliminary evaluation matrix.

**HOST**:
Twelve million Naira down the drain. Because under Nigerian procurement law, evaluators have **zero discretionary power**. 
Preliminary screening is strictly pass or fail.

If you want your bid to reach Stage 2 technical scoring and Stage 3 financial opening, you must master the **Statutory Quad**:

**Pillar 1: FIRS Tax Clearance.**
Three consecutive years. And here is the fatal trap: your TCC turnover must match your audited financial statements. If FIRS shows ₦200 million and your balance sheet shows ₦250 million, you are disqualified for misrepresentation.

**Pillar 2: PENCOM Compliance Certificate.**
Must be valid through December 31st of the current year. Minimum five employees, with group life insurance. Payment slips are strictly rejected.

**Pillar 3: Industrial Training Fund (ITF Form 5A).**
The certified clearance letter confirming one percent payroll contribution. As Apex Infra learned, Remita receipts mean nothing to evaluators.

**Pillar 4: NSITF ECA-01 Certificate.**
Social insurance clearance under the Employees' Compensation Act.

**And the Anchor: The BPP Interim Registration Report (IRR).**
Evaluators scan the QR code on your BPP certificate. If your TCC or PENCOM dates on the BPP portal are expired, your bid is dead on arrival.

Here is how Apex Infra fixed their entire procurement engine.
