### [0:25 – 1:15] Scene 1: The Bottleneck (The 11:00 AM Dispatch Chaos)
**[VISUAL]**: Chioma's Lagos boutique showroom packing area. Complete chaos. Cardboard cartons, clothing racks, and poly-mailers cover the floor. Tunde is sitting cross-legged copying customer names from an iPhone screen into a physical carbon-copy courier booklet. Musa (the courier rider) stands over him, tapping his watch.

**MUSA (Rider)**:
"Oga Tunde, my bike has been parked outside for thirty minutes. I have eight other pickups in Lekki. Where are the twenty parcels for Victoria Island and Ikeja?"

**TUNDE** *(sweating, scribbling furiously with a dying ballpoint pen)*:
"Hold on, Musa! Customer number twelve typed her phone number as eleven digits, but missed one zero, so I'm waiting for her to reply in DMs! And customer fourteen said 'Opposite the yellow gate after the transformer'—I don't know which street that is!"

**CHIOMA** *(rushing over with three garments)*:
"Tunde! Which bag is the Champagne Silk in Size 10? Did we put Zainab's dress in the bag with the Surulere label or the Abuja label?!"

**TUNDE** *(looking at the unlabelled poly-mailers in horror)*:
"I... I think they both have emerald silk inside..."

**MUSA** *(shaking his head)*:
"I cannot wait anymore. I'm taking only the four packages that have clear phone numbers. The rest will have to wait until tomorrow."
