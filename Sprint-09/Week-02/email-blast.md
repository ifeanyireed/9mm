### Editor's Opening Letter
**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

In high-growth business operations, momentum is everything. Yet many managing directors discover too late that commercial growth without statutory governance is built on shifting sand.

When an enterprise misses statutory annual returns, the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) changes its status to "INACTIVE". Almost instantaneously, automated Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) compliance feeds flag your Tax Identification Number and RC number across commercial banking switches. Debit cards stop working, vendor transfers bounce, and credit lines freeze.

The solution is not ad-hoc panic filings—it is constructing an institutional compliance roadmap. In this edition, we examine the legal mechanics of CAMA 2020 annual returns, the strict Section 119 Persons with Significant Control (PSC) mandate, and how continuous governance keeps your corporate accounts active and tender-ready.

Entities that fail to comply will face continuous delisting and banking restrictions."
