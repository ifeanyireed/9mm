# Content Production Rules & Media Guidelines

## 1. Absolute Ban on Unsplash & Generic Stock Imagery
- **ZERO Unsplash Everywhere**: Never use Unsplash, `images.unsplash.com`, stock photography, or unrealistic generic imagery anywhere in email copies, newsletters, or blog posts. Any occurrence of Unsplash is strictly forbidden.
- All news references, images, and links must be sourced exclusively from genuine Nigerian and African business, technology, education, and governance publications:
  - **Punch Nigeria**: `https://cdn.punchng.com/...` / `https://punchng.com/...`
  - **Nairametrics**: `https://cdn.nairametrics.com/...` / `https://nairametrics.com/...`
  - **TechCabal**: `https://techcabal.com/...`
  - **BusinessDay Nigeria**: `https://businessday.ng/...`
  - **Daily Trust**: `https://dailytrust.com/...`
  - **Techpoint Africa**: `https://techpoint.africa/...`
  - **Paystack Engineering**: `https://paystack.com/...`

## 2. Absolute Ban on Isolated Logos, Crests, Badges & Signboards
- **NO Standalone Logos or Badges**: No image across any blog post, email blast, newsletter, or outreach copy may be merely a company logo, school crest, institutional shield, agency badge, coat of arms, or static signboard.
- **Authentic Photojournalism Required**: Every visual asset must feature authentic, real-world Nigerian photojournalism depicting:
  - Students taking CBT or WAEC/NECO exams in actual halls
  - Teachers and administrators in training workshops or school assemblies
  - Real classroom instruction, campus grounds, laboratory facilities, and ICT infrastructure
  - Commercial retail stores, merchants, logistics hubs, dispatch riders, and warehousing operations
  - Government policy stakeholder summits and SUBEB administrative assemblies
- When citing institutions (such as WAEC, JAMB, Chrisland Schools, TRCN, NUT, or state SUBEB boards), never display their isolated logo graphic. Instead, depict genuine photojournalistic coverage of their students, workshops, exam centers, or field operations.

## 3. Mandatory Narrative Visual Integration & Captions
- Visual assets must never stand alone as decorative fillers.
- Every image (Hero Cover, Figure 1, Figure 2, newsletter cover, Curated Briefs 1–3, and Industry Spotlight) must be actively cited and contextualized in the surrounding prose (e.g., *"As illustrated in Punch's coverage of secondary school examinations (featured photo above)..."*).
- Every image must be tied to empirical arguments, sector statistics, or concrete regulatory developments.
- Blog images must be formatted in responsive centered containers (`max-width: 720px; margin: 24px auto; text-align: center;`) with descriptive journalistic captions and image proxy wrapping (`https://wsrv.nl/?url=...` with `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"`).

## 4. Curated Industry Intelligence Briefs (Section 5) — Mandatory on All Campaigns
- Mandatory across all 120 email campaigns (Ofia.ng Sprints 01–04, ResultsPro.ng Sprints 05–08, and ComplianceHub.ng Sprints 09–12).
- Each campaign must feature exactly 3 numbered briefs with clickable headlines, verified Nigerian news images, source publication links, and operational takeaways.
