Validating course credits, library returns, hostel liabilities, bursary settlements, and cumulative GPAs for thousands of seniors simultaneously cannot survive on green cardboard folders and manual desk registers.

National regulatory audits highlight the steep cost of legacy paper clearance. According to the National Universities Commission (NUC) Institutional Governance Review, universities operating decentralized paper clearance experience an average 28-business-day delay in securing Academic Senate approval for graduation lists—putting NYSC mobilization batches at constant risk. Simultaneously, joint ICPC forensic audits revealed that 14.2% of manually stamped graduation dossiers contain forged signatures or counterfeit rubber stamps.

"Physical rubber stamps offer zero forensic security. Any ink seal can be replicated outside a university gate in minutes—turning what administrators think is a safeguard into an institutional liability that delays honest scholars and drains campus revenue."
