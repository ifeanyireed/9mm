### Editor's Opening Letter

**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

Every founder running an Instagram or TikTok retail brand in Lagos, Abuja, or Port Harcourt celebrates the day their Direct Message inbox finally explodes. At first, waking up to sixty unread *"Price please?"* messages feels like proof that your brand has arrived.

Yet ask any boutique founder who has crossed the 100-DM-a-day mark, and you will hear an exhausted confession: *manual chat selling eventually turns your best marketing days into your worst operational bottlenecks.*

When every single sale requires a human assistant to answer pricing questions, verify sizes on a rack, paste a bank account number, check a transfer screenshot, and type a delivery address for a rider, growth hits a hard physical wall. In this inaugural edition, we unpack why Nigerian social brands stall at 100 DMs a day, examine key trends across African fashion and mobile consumer behavior, and share the ten operational fixes that turn social scrolls into instant automated checkouts.
