## Technical & Casting Notes

* **Visual Aesthetic:** Authentic heavy-distribution warehouse realism—high steel pallet racks, forklifts, 30-ton loading bays, stacks of carbon-copy waybill booklets contrasting against crisp handheld Android barcode scanners and clean Ofia B2B portal dashboards.
* **Sound Design:** Diesel truck engines idling, forklift reverse beeps, tearing carbon-copy paper slips, ringing desk phones, transitioning to sharp barcode scanner chirps and crisp digital e-POD confirmation chimes.
* **Tone:** Executive, grounded in West African trade realities, authoritative, and practical. Zero mathematical formulas or academic equations.
