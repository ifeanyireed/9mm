**LAGOS** — The Office of Education Quality Assurance (OEQA), in formal collaboration with State Universal Basic Education Boards, has issued a comprehensive regulatory circular mandating all accredited private nursery, primary, and secondary schools to transition from handwritten paper broadsheets to auditable, cloud-based Continuous Assessment (CA) repositories.

The policy directive follows a statewide investigation into private school grading integrity, which revealed that manual paper mark sheets exhibited an alarming 12.4% error rate in class position rankings and cumulative averages, triggering parent disputes and transcript delays.
