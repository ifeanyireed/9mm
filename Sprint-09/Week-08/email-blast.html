CAC Steps Up Enforcement on Corporate Status Delisting and CAMA 2020 Director Penalties

Source: Punch Nigeria · Read Full Story →

Registrar-General of the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), Hussaini Magaji (SAN), has reaffirmed the Commission's mandate to strike off inactive companies from the national register and enforce daily compounding default penalties under CAMA 2020. Over 100,000 registered companies face de-registration, commercial bank mandate freezes, and loss of corporate status.

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR BOARD CHAIRMEN & MANAGING DIRECTORS:

A company’s legal health is determined on the public CAC register, not in confidential board minutes. Board directors must institute mandatory, automated quarterly registry audits to verify that the live CRP portal reflects 100% of boardroom resolutions, share allotments, and PSC disclosures before regulatory or banking sanctions strike.

The 5 Questions Every Chairman & MD Must Ask Before Next Board Meeting

1. "What is our exact live status on the CAC public search portal today?"

Demand a digital snapshot of the CRP Company Search for the parent company and operating subsidiaries. A status of "INACTIVE" means automated bank freezes under CBN directives.
2. "Are our registered directors on CAC 100% identical to our boardroom roster?"

Notice of director changes must be filed within 14 calendar days under CAMA Section 321. Unfiled changes trigger daily personal penalties on defaulting directors.
3. "Have we officially certified our Persons with Significant Control (PSC)?"

Under Section 119, all 5%+ equity or voting holders must be documented down to the natural person level. Non-compliance carries statutory penalties up to ₦5,000,000.
4. "Are all issued shares fully paid up and free of unissued share capital?"

Authorized capital was abolished under Section 124. Any company with unissued shares cannot register new debentures or expand capital until regularized.
5. "Do we maintain automated continuous monitoring or rely on periodic paper checks?"

Replace annual manual secretarial clerk visits with automated 24/7 software telemetry connecting your boardroom directly to the CAC CRP portal.
