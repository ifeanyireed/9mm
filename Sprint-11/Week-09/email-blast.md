### Part 3: Executive Implementation Blueprint (The 4-Step DNFBP Roadmap)

Follow this 4-step sequence to achieve bulletproof SCUML certification and uninterrupted banking continuity:

1. **Step 1: Complete SCUML Registration & Directorship Biometrics**
   * *The Action*: Align registered CAC business objectives with DNFBP operations. Synchronize director biometric details (NIN, BVN) to ensure exact name matches across NIMC, CAC, and bank portals.
2. **Step 2: Formulate an Institutional AML/CFT/CPF Policy Manual**
   * *The Action*: Adopt a comprehensive corporate policy manual compliant with Section 9 of the MLA 2022. Formally appoint an executive-level Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) and establish documented Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) protocols for Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs).
3. **Step 3: Establish NFIU GoAML Reporting Infrastructure**
   * *The Action*: Register your compliance desk on the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) GoAML portal. Automate the mandatory 7-day filing of Currency Transaction Reports (CTR) for transactions exceeding statutory cash thresholds (₦5M individual, ₦10M corporate).
4. **Step 4: Automate PEP Screening & Secure Bank Clearance Validation**
   * *The Action*: Implement real-time client screening against global sanctions watchlists (OFAC, UN, EU). Compile your verified DNFBP Regulatory Clearance Dossier and submit directly to commercial bank compliance desks to permanently clear PND risk.
