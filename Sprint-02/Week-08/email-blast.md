When quoting delays cause an enterprise buyer to split orders or switch distributors, you don't just lose one transaction—you destroy compounded lifetime value. Here is the formula:

$$\text{Lifetime Margin Bleed} = \sum (\text{Average Order Value} \times \text{Annual Frequency} \times \text{Margin \%} \times \text{Retention Years}) + \text{CAC}$$
