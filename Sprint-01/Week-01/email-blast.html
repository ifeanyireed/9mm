Across Nigeria's urban mobile commerce market, the active impulse buying window lasts barely four minutes; every extra chat exchange drives abandonment higher.

"Instagram and TikTok are extraordinary engines for sparking desire. They were never designed to function as your inventory ledger, payment verification desk, or courier manifest."
► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR SOCIAL COMMERCE OPERATORS:
If your sales staff spend more than two hours a day answering "How much?" and "Is Medium available?" inside direct messages, your brand is leaving massive evening and cross-city revenue on the table. Publish transparent pricing upfront, link every post to a multi-variant digital storefront, and let automated virtual-account verification confirm payments instantly around the clock.
