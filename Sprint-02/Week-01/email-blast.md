### Lead News Cover Story: Breaking News · B2B Procurement Policy
**Dangote and BUA Enforce 24-Hour Digital Purchase Order Mandate, Ending 3-Day Paper Pro-Forma Quoting Across Major Depots**

**LAGOS** — Nigeria’s leading industrial manufacturing conglomerates, including Dangote Cement and BUA Group, have jointly issued an executive procurement directive requiring all Tier-1 and regional wholesale distributors to process bulk allocations exclusively through verified digital B2B portals within 24 hours of release.

The joint procurement circular officially ends the acceptance of handwritten pro-forma quotation letters, faxed waybills, and physical bank teller attachments. Under the new protocol, distributors who fail to verify inventory allocations and submit automated purchase orders within the 24-hour window forfeit their scheduled depot loading slots to automated logistics aggregators.

> *"We cannot have hundreds of heavy haulage trailers idling at factory gates for 48 hours because a regional distributor’s accounting desk is manually calculating freight differentials on carbon paper. Real-time digital purchase orders are now the baseline condition for distribution quotas."* — Alhaji Mansur Ahmed, Director of Supply Chain Operations.

Industry data reveals that the transition will affect more than 4,200 apex wholesale distributors across building materials, FMCG, and agricultural commodities nationwide, forcing operators to digitize multi-depot stock management immediately.

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR WHOLESALE OPERATORS:**

### ⚡ B2B Growth Hack of the Week: Detailed Operational Framework
**Calculate Your B2B Quoting Drag & Deploy the First 2 Wholesale Fixes**

To comply with the new 24-hour manufacturer digital mandates, calculate your sales desk operational drag using the **B2B Quoting Drag Formula**:
$$\text{Quoting Friction Hours} = \frac{\text{Daily Bulk Inquiries} \times 15\text{ Minutes}}{60}$$
$$40\text{ Inquiries} \times 15\text{ Mins} \div 60 = 10\text{ Full Working Hours per Day}$$

If your sales desk spends 10 hours a day checking paper bin cards and calling warehouse floors, your quotation turnaround slips to three days. Here are the first two immediate operational fixes from our supply chain playbook:

* **Hack 1: Replace Paper Price Lists with Self-Service Tiered B2B Portals**  
  Wholesale customers do not purchase at uniform prices. Sub-distributors, key accounts, and retail chains each operate on contract tiers. When this logic is kept on printed paper sheets, every pro-forma quote requires manual managerial sign-off. By giving buyers dedicated portal logins (`/erp/admin/marketplace`), their pre-negotiated tier discounts apply automatically, allowing apex clients to confirm eight-figure purchase orders in 12 seconds.

* **Hack 2: Implement Real-Time Multi-Warehouse Bin Card Telemetry**  
  Stop relying on physical bin cards tacked to wooden pallets. Equip warehouse floor workers with handheld Android barcode scanners during put-away and picking (`/erp/admin/inventory/warehouses`). Central dispatch instantly views exactly how many pallets are in Ikeja Depot, Kano Hub, or bonded terminal storage, eliminating phantom stock variances and phone calls between depots.
