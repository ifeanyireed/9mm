**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI beside a digital whiteboard highlighting the 3 points of gradebook failure.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Why do private schools with multi-million Naira tuition still suffer end-of-term grading disasters?
It comes down to three structural vulnerabilities:
**1. The Decentralized Spreadsheet Trap:** Every teacher maintains their own Excel workbook on personal laptops. Formulas get altered, weightings drift, and Continuous Assessment curves differ from classroom to classroom.
**2. The White-Out Blind Spot:** Manual paper score sheets have zero audit trail. Anyone with a red pen or correction fluid can modify a student's grade without authorization.
**3. The Re-Ranking Avalanche:** When one grade changes, every single student's percentile rank in that subject must be recalculated manually—creating hours of cascading errors."
