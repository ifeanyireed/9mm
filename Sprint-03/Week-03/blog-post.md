## The 3 Ways Commissions Destroy Merchant Viability

### 1. The Margin Compression Squeeze
When inflation raises raw material and import costs, merchants cannot easily raise prices on aggregators because algorithm search bots prioritize the absolute cheapest vendor. You are forced to absorb supply chain inflation while paying the platform an unyielding 15% cut.

### 2. The Return Penalty Asymmetry
When an aggregator's courier fails to deliver a parcel or an impulse buyer changes their mind at the doorstep, the platform charges the merchant a "Return Logistics Penalty" (often ₦1,500–₦2,500 per failed drop). The merchant loses the sale, pays the penalty, and receives shop-soiled inventory.

### 3. The Forced Pay-to-Play Advertising Squeeze
As categories saturate with thousands of third-party vendors, organic search algorithms suppress non-advertising listings. To maintain previous order volume, merchants are forced to buy platform sponsored product ads, pushing total effective platform take-rates from 15% to over 24%.
