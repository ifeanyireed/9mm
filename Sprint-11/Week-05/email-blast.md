### Editor's Opening Letter
**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

In high-value Nigerian commerce, customer discretion has historically been viewed as a virtue. When an influential buyer offers to pay via multiple split transfers or an offshore holding vehicle, sales directors are eager to accommodate.

Under modern Nigerian anti-money laundering law, accommodating unconventional payment structures is the single fastest way to invite financial crime investigators to your doorstep.

Under the Money Laundering Act 2022 and Nigeria’s UNODC-backed goAML architecture, commercial banks no longer wait for manual regulatory inquiries. High-value wire transfers exceeding statutory thresholds generate automated telemetry. If your company lacks an accredited compliance officer, fails to file symmetrical 7-day Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs), or accepts unverified PEP funds, your accounts are flagged automatically.

In this edition, we examine the three unmistakable warning signs that your enterprise is triggering an AML audit and provide 10 operational fixes to protect your business.
