**Visual**: Host steps forward with a digital tablet displaying an interactive diagram of the CAC Companies Registration Portal (CRP).

**HOST**:
Eight weeks? That's how companies lose contracts, lose banking facilities, and get published in the Federal Gazette for striking off.

Regularizing corporate arrears requires a forensic 4-step sequence:

**Step 1: The Registry Forensic Audit.**
Before you file anything, pull the live CRP extract. Identify the exact last approved year. You might think you filed in 2023, but if an examiner queried a balance sheet signature, the filing was never completed, and penalties have been compounding ever since.

**Step 2: Multi-Year Financial & Secretarial Reconciliation.**
You cannot submit annual returns without approved financial statements for every delinquent year. And here is the trap: you must file your **Persons with Significant Control (PSC)** disclosures under Section 119 at the same time. If you skip PSC, the portal queries your submission instantly.

**Step 3: Sequential Digital Ingestion.**
You must file chronologically—oldest year to newest. The CRP engine generates automated Remita invoices with exact late penalty calculations. Pay through verified RRR channels to avoid portal freezes.

**Step 4: Status Reinstatement & Bank Push.**
Once the status flips back to "ACTIVE", download your QR-coded Status Report and Certified True Copies. Deliver the verified pack directly to your bank compliance desk to lift the PND.
