Breaking News · Educational Policy

Ministry Quality Assurance Service Issues Binding Digital Gradebook Mandate for Accredited Private Schools

Photo: Private school administrative complex. State quality assurance evaluators are mandating digital Continuous Assessment repositories to eliminate report card computation errors. Source: Education Quality Assurance Service / NAN.

LAGOS — The Office of Education Quality Assurance (OEQA), in formal collaboration with State Universal Basic Education Boards, has issued a comprehensive regulatory circular mandating all accredited private nursery, primary, and secondary schools to transition from handwritten paper broadsheets to auditable, cloud-based Continuous Assessment (CA) repositories.

The policy directive follows a statewide investigation into private school grading integrity, which revealed that manual paper mark sheets exhibited an alarming 12.4% error rate in class position rankings and cumulative averages. The errors frequently resulted in formal parent grievances during speech day celebrations and delayed international transcript transfers to foreign boarding schools.

"Elite private education cannot continue to rely on manual ballpoint arithmetic executed in the middle of the night. Schools charging high tuition fees must maintain auditable digital gradebooks with verified student registration IDs and tamper-proof evaluation trails." — Mrs. Abiola Seriki-Ayeni, Director General of Education Quality Assurance.

Under the new guidelines, accreditation renewals for private schools enrolling over 200 learners will require proof of an active, centralized school ERP system capable of generating cryptographically verified student academic records.

K-12 Education News Events

1. NAPPS National Audit Finds 74% of School Bursars Lose 15+ Hours Weekly on Transfer Tracking

At the 2026 National Association of Proprietors of Private Schools annual summit, bursary audit reports revealed that 74% of private schools suffer weekly reconciliation lags caused by unidentified bank deposits.

Generic bank narrations like "School Fees" or corporate payments from uncle/aunt accounts lacking student admission numbers were identified as the primary source of billing friction and uncollected termly balances. Source: NAPPS Executive Communique.

2. WAEC and Cambridge Assessment Expand API Portals for School Continuous Assessment Uploads

International and regional examination councils announced real-time API integrations that accept school-based Continuous Assessment scores directly from accredited school management software.

The transition officially phases out physical bulk CSV disc submissions, penalizing institutions that cannot provide digitally validated, term-by-term learner evaluation trails. Source: Examination Council Gazette.

3. Commercial Banks Launch Dedicated Virtual Account Numbers for Student Fee Collection

Tier-1 commercial banks have launched automated education collection APIs assigning each enrolled pupil a dedicated virtual NUBAN account.

When parents transfer funds from any mobile app, the system reconciles the student’s ledger in 3 seconds, generates an automated receipt, and eliminates the need for parents to submit WhatsApp transaction screenshots. Source: Banking & Fintech Review.
