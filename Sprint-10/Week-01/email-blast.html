Breaking News · Public Procurement

Bureau of Public Procurement and PenCom Intensify Enforcement on Federal Contractor Statutory Clearances

Photo: Regulators and corporate bidding leaders evaluate statutory pre-qualification guidelines in Abuja. Tenders boards now deploy automated cross-checks across PenCom, ITF, and FIRS databases. Source: Punch Nigeria.

ABUJA — As documented in Punch Nigeria's coverage of federal procurement policy meetings (featured photo above), the Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) alongside the National Pension Commission (PenCom) and the Industrial Training Fund (ITF) has issued stringent pre-qualification directives across all Federal Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs).

Under Section 16(6) of the Public Procurement Act 2007, any numerical mismatch or expiration across the "Statutory Quad"—PenCom, ITF, NSITF, and FIRS Tax Clearance Certificates—results in automatic technical disqualification. Tenders boards have been barred from issuing post-deadline compliance waivers.

"Public procurement law in Nigeria is uncompromising. If your PenCom schedule lists 20 employees while your Tax Clearance Certificate reflects 40, your bid is legally classified as non-responsive. We are digitizing compliance verification to ensure fair competition and protect public revenues."

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR MANAGING DIRECTORS & BID DIRECTORS:

Tenders are won through engineering and pricing, but lost in the first 3 minutes on statutory compliance. Establishing automated 90-day renewal countdowns and synchronizing employee rosters between your HR, pension, and tax registers eliminates technical disqualification.
