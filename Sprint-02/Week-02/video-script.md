## Dual-Track Master Production Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] Cold Open: The Waybill Void (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: Close-up on IFEANYI standing in the middle of a massive logistics transit hub with twenty 30-ton trailers lined up in the background.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"In the B2B wholesale distribution business, paper does not record inventory.
Paper buries it.
Right now, between the time a 30-ton truck leaves your warehouse and the time the driver hands a stamped physical invoice to a supermarket depot...
Your finance team is operating completely blind.
You don’t know what was damaged. You don’t know what was short-delivered.
And you cannot issue a final invoice for twenty-one days.
Welcome to the Dispatch Void—where your working capital disappears between the warehouse gate and the steering wheel."

---

### [0:25 – 1:15] Scene 1: The High (The 500-Carton Dispatch)
**[VISUAL]**: Apex Logistics loading dock in Ikeja. Two forklifts load heavy pallets of edible oil and flour into a long-haul truck. Alhaji Sanusi stands proudly beside Emeka.

**ALHAJI SANUSI** *(checking off a master paper sheet)*:
"Five hundred cartons of vegetable oil dispatched to the Ibadan regional retail consortium! That is ₦24 million in revenue closed before 10:00 AM. Emeka, hand the driver the three carbon invoices!"

**EMEKA** *(tearing pink and yellow slips from a ledger booklet)*:
"Driver has the original and duplicate, Alhaji. Once he gets the physical signature and physical rubber stamp in Ibadan, he brings the slip back next Monday."

**ALHAJI SANUSI** *(smiling, patting the truck side)*:
"Excellent. By next week we reconcile and collect our payment."

---

### [1:15 – 2:40] Scene 2: The Crash (The 18-Day Reconciliation Nightmare)
**[VISUAL]**: Two weeks later. Alhaji Sanusi’s private office. He is on speakerphone with the Chief Procurement Officer of the supermarket chain.

**CPO (V.O., on speakerphone)**:
"Alhaji, we cannot process your ₦24 million payment. Your driver’s paper waybill arrived soaked in motor oil, the rubber stamp is illegible, and our depot supervisor reported forty cartons damaged on transit. Until you provide an authenticated, reconciled credit note with barcode proof, this payment is on hold for ninety days."

**[VISUAL]**: Alhaji slams the phone down. He calls Emeka into the office.

**ALHAJI SANUSI** *(furious)*:
"Where is the driver?! Where is our warehouse bin record?! Why are we discovering forty damaged cartons fourteen days after dispatch?!"

**EMEKA** *(shuffling through five cardboard folders)*:
"Alhaji, the driver is currently in Kaduna on another trip! The paper bin card in the warehouse still shows the five hundred cartons left the shelf intact. The loading bay supervisor wrote the batch number on a piece of cardboard that got lost in the rain!"

**[VISUAL]**: Split-screen display:
* *Left Screen (Apex Logistics Paper Chaos)*: Stacks of grease-stained paper delivery receipts, delayed payments, warehouse reconcilers arguing over missing cartons, and millions tied up in receivables.
* *Right Screen (Ofia Digital Supply Chain)*: An automated wholesale operator using Ofia. The driver uses a rugged mobile device for digital Proof of Delivery (ePOD). As goods are unloaded, barcodes are scanned. The client signs digitally on glass. A verified electronic invoice and instant credit note are dispatched to the client's finance department in four seconds.

### [2:40 – 3:20] Talking Head: The Physics of the Supply Chain Lag
**[VISUAL]**: Cut to IFEANYI in the studio. On-screen graphic illuminates the Dispatch-to-Cash Formula.

$$Working Capital Drag = Monthly Dispatched GMV × ( (Paper Reconciliation Days (18)) ÷ (30) ) × Cost of Capital (24%)$$

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Look at the mathematics of wholesale logistics.
If your distribution company moves **₦200 million** in monthly inventory, but your paper waybills take **18 days** to physically return and reconcile with your warehouse ledger:
You are floating **₦120 million in unbilled working capital** at all times.
At an annual commercial borrowing cost of 24%, that paper reconciliation delay is costing your business **₦2.4 million in pure interest drain every single month**.
You don't have a sales problem. You have a data latency problem."

### [3:20 – 4:00] Scene 3: The Rewind (The Ofia Connected Loop)
**[VISUAL]**: HARD REWIND SFX. Cut back to the Apex loading dock. Emeka holds a digital tablet running **Ofia Logistics Command Center** (`/erp/admin/logistics/dispatch`).

**EMEKA** *(scanning the final pallet with a handheld barcode scanner)*:

The driver presents the phone. The receiving manager scans thirty cases, flags three defective seals.

"Payment approved by their finance system within forty-eight hours."
