In modern corporate governance, a board resolution is only as valid as its statutory record.

When directors vote to appoint new executives, allot equity to strategic partners, or adjust share structures, the legal clock starts immediately. Yet across corporate Nigeria, secretarial files routinely sit unformalized for weeks while waiting for paper signatures.

Under CAMA 2020, secretarial latency is an expensive liability. Late director filings attract daily penalties of up to ₦20,000, unfiled PSC returns trigger corporate blacklisting, and commercial banks freeze credit facilities the moment a company's CAC status lapses to "INACTIVE". In this edition, we examine how to bridge the board-to-registry gap.

LEAD STATUTORY GOVERNANCE COVER STORY

CAC Mandates Real-Time Digital PSC and Board Resolution Filings via CRP 2.0

Source: Punch Nigeria · Corporate Affairs Commission Stakeholder Assembly in Abuja.

ABUJA — The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) has upgraded its digital Companies Registration Portal (CRP), deploying real-time API verification rails for post-incorporation filings, director updates, and beneficial ownership disclosures. As highlighted in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national corporate governance proceedings in Abuja (featured photo above), the Commission is eliminating paper-based secretarial backlog and enforcing strict automated penalties on late returns.

"The Commission will no longer accommodate delayed secretarial filings. Under Section 119 and Section 321 of CAMA 2020, companies must register director changes within 14 days and disclose Persons with Significant Control in real-time. Automated validation engines on CRP 2.0 now reject backdated resolutions and apply statutory late filing penalties automatically."
► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR COMPANY SECRETARIES & GENERAL COUNSEL:

Relying on manual drafting and paper courier workflows guarantees statutory default. Secretarial desks must adopt digital resolution extraction, Evidence Act compliant electronic signatures, and automated CRP filing gateways to execute director and share filings within statutory windows, permanently insulating corporate accounts from banking freezes.
