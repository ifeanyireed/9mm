You can own 50 Caterpillar excavators and secure a ₦30M bank tender guarantee.

And yet, 65% of federal bids are disqualified in the first sixty minutes of public bid opening in Abuja.

Why?
Not because your concrete batching plant was questioned or your financial bid was uncompetitive.

It happened because of a single Statutory Quad documentation defect:
• An Industrial Training Fund (ITF) Form 5A that expired 3 days before submission.
• A PENCOM compliance certificate stalled over an uncredited staff RSA PIN variance.
• An Interim Registration Report (IRR) on the BPP portal displaying "EXPIRED AUDIT STATUS".

Under Section 16(6) of the Public Procurement Act 2007 (PPA 2007), preliminary evaluation is binary.
There is no "substantial compliance." A bid either contains 100% valid statutory quad certificates or it is eliminated on the spot.
Worse, procuring entities are legally forbidden from requesting updated documents after the submission deadline.

Before you apply the red wax seal to your next federal tender envelope, table these 5 executive questions:

1. "Are all four statutory quad certificates valid through the ENTIRE evaluation window?"
Never submit a bid if any certificate has less than 60 days of remaining validity. If tender evaluation extends into a new quarter or year, an expired certificate triggers immediate Stage 1 disqualification.

2. "Does our BPP National Database classification match the project scope and threshold?"
Verify your Interim Registration Report (IRR) classification tier. Submitting a bid exceeding your approved financial category or with outdated audited accounts invalidates the IRR barcode.

3. "Are our PENCOM remittance schedules 100% reconciled across all PFAs?"
Resolve all uncredited RSA PINs 60 days before certificate renewal. Submitting bank payment slips or PENCOM acknowledgment receipts is strictly prohibited under BPP procurement guidelines.

4. "Does our ITF Form 5A assessment match the payroll in our audited accounts?"
Evaluators execute mathematical cross-checks between Audited Accounts Notes (Staff Costs) and the ITF Form 5A declaration. Discrepancies trigger disqualification for inconsistent statutory declaration.

5. "Have our Joint Venture (JV) partners been audited against the exact same standards?"
Under public procurement law, every consortium partner must satisfy statutory quad requirements independently. A single partner's expired NSITF certificate disqualifies the entire joint bid.
