### Four Rules to Eliminate Transaction Fraud and Unlock Cash Flow

1. **Lock Buyer Funds in Isolated Trustee Escrow**:
   Give customers total peace of mind. When they pay at checkout, funds are held in an independent, regulated smart-contract vault—not transferred directly to the seller, and not commingled with platform funds.

2. **Mandate the 6-Digit Cryptographic Delivery Handshake (OTP)**:
   Never rely on paper signature slips. When the dispatch rider arrives at the gate, the customer inspects the parcel and exchanges a private 6-digit OTP code sent to their smartphone.

3. **Trigger Instant Bank Payouts Upon OTP Validation**:
   Eliminate 21-day holding periods. The exact millisecond the courier enters the verified OTP at the customer's gate, the escrow vault unlocks and transfers the cash directly to your commercial bank account.

4. **Deduct Return Shipping on Arbitrary Rejections**:
   Protect your business from fickle buyers. If a customer refuses an undamaged, verified item simply because they "changed their mind," the system automatically deducts round-trip courier fees from their deposit before refunding the balance.
