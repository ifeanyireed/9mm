Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},

In Nigeria’s current macroeconomic environment, corporate leadership is intensely focused on navigating commercial headwinds: managing foreign exchange volatility, optimizing supply chain expenses, and defending operating margins.

Yet, an invisible statutory hazard threatens the operational life of established businesses: multi-year annual returns arrears on the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registry.

As confirmed by Punch Nigeria's regulatory coverage, Registrar-General Hussaini Magaji (SAN) has reaffirmed the CAC's statutory mandate under Section 692 of CAMA 2020 to strike inactive corporate entities off the national register. Over 100,000 registered companies face de-registration, commercial bank account restrictions, and compounding late default penalties under Section 422.

The most dangerous assumption in Nigerian business is believing that paying annual Company Income Tax (CIT) to FIRS guarantees good standing. FIRS manages tax revenue; the CAC manages your corporate charter. When commercial banks conduct automated quarterly compliance sweeps, any profile marked "INACTIVE" on the CRP portal faces an instant Post-No-Debit (PND) freeze.

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR FOUNDERS & CFOS: A company’s corporate life depends entirely on the public CAC register. If annual returns have lapsed, paying taxes will not prevent bank freezes or gazetted striking-off. Enterprise leaders must conduct an immediate historical registry audit, reconcile unfiled audited accounts, and execute sequential CRP regularization.

The 4-Step Regularization Roadmap:

  1. Execute a Registry Forensic Audit: Pull the live CRP extract, verify the last approved filing year, and calculate accrued Section 422 penalties.
  2. Reconcile Audited Accounts & PSC: Compile signed financial statements for each unfiled year and draft the digital Person with Significant Control (PSC) return under Section 119.
  3. Sequential Digital Filing Ingestion: Ingest annual returns chronologically starting from the oldest unfiled year and clear Remita statutory fee invoices.
  4. Status Verification & Bank Reinstatement: Download the QR-coded Status Report showing "ACTIVE" and deliver certified clearance dossiers to commercial banks to remove PND tags.
