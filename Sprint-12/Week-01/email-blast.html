Nigeria Data Protection Commission Issues March 15 Statutory Audit Deadline for Data Controllers

Photo: NDPC leadership and licensed DPCO audit partners review statutory compliance guidelines during a national regulatory assembly in Abuja. Data Controllers of Major Importance face strict March 15 filing cutoffs. Source: Punch Nigeria.

ABUJA & LAGOS — As documented in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national corporate data governance assemblies (featured photo above), the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) has issued a formal regulatory advisory to all enterprises classified as Data Controllers of Major Importance (DCMI).

Under Section 48 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), organizations processing personal data of over 1,000 data subjects must file an annual statutory Data Protection Compliance Audit through a licensed DPCO by March 15th. Failure to comply triggers fines up to ₦10,000,000 or 2% of annual gross revenue.

"A generic website privacy notice is not compliance. The NDPA demands verifiable accountability: a live Record of Processing Activities (RoPA), certified third-party vendor agreements, and an annual DPCO audit. We will enforce compliance without fear or favor."

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR GENERAL COUNSEL, CTOS & FOUNDERS:

Statutory privacy audits cannot be completed in 48 hours. Engaging an accredited DPCO partner and automating your data inventory today ensures your enterprise meets the March 15 cutoff, protecting your business from ₦10M fines and vendor disqualification.
