## Dual-Track Master Production Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] Cold Open: The Human Notification Machine (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI standing center-frame. On the digital screen behind him, a montage of five hundred Instagram comments rapidly scrolls: *"Price?", "How much?", "DM me", "Is price in bio?", "Check DM"*.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"If you run a fashion boutique, cosmetics brand, or retail store on Instagram, answer this honestly:
Did you start your business because you love curating beautiful products, building a brand, and creating wealth...
Or did you start your business so you could spend six hours every single night typing the words 'Price is in the bio' five hundred times into mobile chat screens?
If your fingers are cramping before you make your first million naira, you don't have a retail enterprise.
You have turned yourself into a human notification machine.
Today, we break down the three unmistakable warning signs that your social store has hit the manual ceiling."

### [0:25 – 1:15] Scene 1: The High (The Viral Reel Wave)
**[VISUAL]**: Chioma's Closet showroom. 2:00 PM. Chioma and Tunde are staring at Chioma's iPhone mounted on a tripod. The screen shows their latest styling transition Reel: 180,000 views and climbing.

**CHIOMA** *(screaming with joy)*:
"Tunde! Look at the engagement! One hundred and eighty thousand views in four hours! The comments are pouring in from Abuja, Port Harcourt, London, everywhere!"

**TUNDE** *(scrolling furiously)*:
"Chioma, there are four hundred new comments asking for the price of the Sahara Linen Jumpsuit, and the DM queue just crossed two hundred and fifty unread messages!"

"Take my personal phone! Copy our standard message: 'Hi darling, price is thirty-two thousand naira, link in bio or send your details here.' Let's reply to every single person before they scroll away!"

**TUNDE** *(typing rapidly)*:
"On it! Replying to comment number one: 'Price is in the bio, love!' Comment number two: 'Price is in the bio, love!'"

### [1:15 – 2:40] Scene 2: The Crash (The 7:00 PM Burnout & Split-Screen)
**[VISUAL]**: 7:30 PM. Five and a half hours later. The showroom looks like a battlefield. Chioma is slumped on the sofa, rubbing her forehead. Tunde has an ice pack taped to his right thumb.

**TUNDE** *(voice hoarse, eyes bloodshot)*:
"Chioma... I have typed 'Price is in the bio' four hundred and twelve times today. My thumb is physically swollen.
And look at what happened: while I was answering people in the comments, eighty people in the direct messages got tired of waiting and bought from another boutique.
Then ten people paid for the same Size 12 jumpsuit because I didn't update the notebook in time!"

**CHIOMA** *(holding an open shipping box, voice cracking)*:
"We had two hundred thousand views today, Tunde. Two hundred thousand!
And after seven hours of non-stop typing, we only closed nine paid orders?
And now we have to refund three customers because we sold the same piece twice?"

**[VISUAL: SPLIT-SCREEN CONTRAST]**:
* *LEFT (Manual Chioma's Closet)*: Chioma and Tunde buried under two smartphones and paper stock cards, frantically typing apologies and issuing manual bank refunds.
* *RIGHT (Automated Ofia Merchant)*: A boutique owner sitting comfortably at a dinner table. Her bio link runs `shop.ofia.shop`. When a shopper comments *"Price?"*, an automated bot drops the exact product link. The buyer taps the link, chooses their size, transfers funds via dynamic virtual account in 4 seconds, and the thermal printer at her studio prints the dispatch waybill automatically. Zero human typing.

**INSTAGRAM COMMENTER** *(Voice-over over left screen)*:

$$Daily Typing Overhead (Hours) = (Comments + Inquiries) × (Typing Seconds ÷ 3600)$$

Warning sign number one: Your DM queue is full, but your bank account is stagnant.
Warning sign number two: You are terrified of going viral because you cannot handle the messages.
