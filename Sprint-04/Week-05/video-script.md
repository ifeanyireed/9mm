### [1:11 – 1:55] Diagnostic: Warning Sign #1 — The WhatsApp Intercom (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI walks past a screen displaying 47 unread WhatsApp voice notes titled *"Do we have size 42 in Lekki?"* and *"Check back of warehouse!"*

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Warning Sign Number One: **Your inventory management system is a WhatsApp group chat.**
If a customer in your Lekki store asks for a product, and your sales associate has to pick up a phone, send three voice notes, and wait ten minutes for another branch to reply—you are operating blind.
In modern retail, a ten-minute wait time reduces in-store checkout conversion by 62%.
When your branches run disconnected local POS software, every showroom becomes an isolated island. 
You end up over-ordering stock for Branch A because you have no idea Branch B has three units collecting dust in the backroom."
