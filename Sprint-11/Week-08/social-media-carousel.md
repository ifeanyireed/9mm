How does this happen to established, legitimate Nigerian corporations?

It happens because of the dangerous Paper SCUML Fallacy:
Most board members assume that having a framed paper SCUML certificate in reception means the company is protected.

Under the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 (MLA 2022), that assumption is fatal:
1. Section 2: Accepting cash payments above ₦5M (Individual) or ₦10M (Corporate) is strictly illegal. Splitting transactions into multiple cash tranches is "Structuring"—a federal felony.
2. Section 10: Every transaction exceeding statutory limits must be reported to the NFIU on the goAML portal within 7 CALENDAR DAYS.
3. Personal Liability: Default fines start at ₦10,000,000, and Section 11 strips away corporate veil protection, exposing directors to personal criminal liability and fines.

Meanwhile, commercial banks operating under CBN supervisory regulations run automated AML monitoring bots.
If high-value deposits hit your account without matching DNFBP regulatory clearance tokens, your bank freezes debits immediately to protect its own operating license.

Before your next board review, table these 5 questions:

1. "Are we accepting cash payments exceeding Section 2 statutory thresholds?"
Absolute ban on cash above ₦5M for individuals or ₦10M for corporate bodies. Splitting cash deposits into smaller tranches is a statutory felony under Section 2(2).

2. "Is our NFIU goAML Currency Transaction Reporting automated or handled manually?"
All transactions exceeding ₦5M (Individual) or ₦10M (Corporate) must be reported to the NFIU within 7 calendar days. Manual spreadsheets inevitably miss deadlines, triggering regulatory sanctions.

3. "Do we screen every high-net-worth buyer against real-time PEP & sanctions lists?"
Mandatory automated screening of buyers against Nigerian (NFIU) and global watchlists (UN, OFAC) before contracts are executed. Documented Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) required for Politically Exposed Persons.

4. "Has our SCUML biometric registration been synchronized within the last 12 months?"
Registered directorship and bank signatory data must match CAC CAMA 2020 registers 100%. Discrepancies cause commercial bank compliance desks to freeze accounts on PND.

5. "Do we provide documented annual AML/CFT training to our sales and frontline staff?"
Annual certified compliance training is mandatory under Section 11(2). Missing employee training logs are the #1 reason companies fail SCUML on-site inspections.
