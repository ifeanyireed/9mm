### Part 3: Executive Implementation Blueprint (The 5 Executive Privacy Questions)

Table these 5 questions before your next quarterly sprint release:

1. **"Have We Formally Registered with the NDPC as a Data Controller of Major Importance (DCMI)?"**
   * *The Standard*: Platforms with 10,000+ data subjects or operating in sensitive sectors (FinTech, health, banking) must be officially registered on the National Register of Data Controllers under Section 5 & 24.
2. **"Do We Conduct a Documented Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) on Every High-Risk Feature?"**
   * *The Standard*: Section 28 mandates documented DPIA filings prior to deploying automated profiling, facial biometrics, or behavioral AI tracking. Skipping this leaves engineering squads directly liable.
3. **"Are Our Customer Database Backups and Cross-Border Cloud Replications Compliant with Section 41?"**
   * *The Standard*: Storing Nigerian consumer records on AWS, GCP, or Azure data centers abroad requires documented legal adequacy mechanisms or binding corporate rules, with AES-256 encryption at rest.
4. **"Can Our Infrastructure Detect, Contain, and Report a Personal Data Breach Within 72 Hours?"**
   * *The Standard*: Under Section 40, controllers have exactly 72 hours to notify the NDPC following a personal data breach. Automated breach alerting and isolation runbooks are statutory necessities.
5. **"Is Our Annual DPCO Data Protection Audit Filed and Certified Before March 15th?"**
   * *The Standard*: Every DCMI must engage a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organization (DPCO) to audit its architecture and file statutory returns by March 15th annually.
