## The Banking Trap: Why Having a CAC Certificate Is No Longer Enough

In Nigeria's high-value merchant sectors, business founders frequently make a critical legal mistake:
> *"Our company is incorporated with the CAC, we have our Tax Clearance Certificate from FIRS, and our bank account has been active for six years. We don't need any special police or EFCC certificate to sell cars or luxury apartments."*

Under Nigerian law, this assumption is legally invalid.

Under Section 30 of the **Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 (MLA 2022)**, businesses operating in designated high-risk commercial sectors are classified as **Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs)**. This statutory definition encompasses:
* Real estate developers, real estate agents, brokers, and property syndicates;
* Automobile dealerships (luxury, new, and certified pre-owned);
* Dealers in precious metals, jewelry, and gemstones;
* Legal practitioners, notaries, and chartered accountants handling client funds or property transfers;
* Casinos, betting operators, lottery agencies, and gaming floors;
* Private investment pools, trustees, and asset managers outside SEC licensing.

Under Section 11 of the MLA 2022, together with Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) AML supervisory directives:
1. Every commercial bank in Nigeria is legally prohibited from opening or maintaining an operating account for a DNFBP that lacks a verified, active **SCUML Certificate of Registration**.
2. Commercial banks are required to execute continuous quarterly automated audits against the SCUML central database.
3. If your company's profile is missing, un-renewed, or flagged for an unaddressed inspection query, the bank’s compliance system automatically executes a **Post-No-Debit (PND)** restriction on all operating accounts without human discretion.

## The 4-Step DNFBP Regularization Roadmap

If your business is operating without a SCUML certificate, with a queried application, or with an outdated AML manual that leaves you vulnerable to bank freezes, execute this **4-Step Regularization Roadmap**:

### Step 1: Complete SCUML Registration & Biometric Verification
1. **Auditing Business Classification**: Ensure your registered CAC business objectives (Memorandum of Association) explicitly reflect your DNFBP operational activities (e.g., real estate development or motor vehicle trading). Discrepancies between CAC objectives and your SCUML application trigger immediate rejection.
2. **Director Biometric Synchronization**: Under recent EFCC directives, all company directors must submit verified National Identification Numbers (NIN), Bank Verification Numbers (BVN), and biometric proofs. Discrepancies in name spellings across NIMC, CAC, and bank accounts are the leading cause of portal delays.
3. **Tax Clearance & Evidence of Address**: Submit current FIRS Tax Clearance Certificates and physical utility inspection proofs for your registered commercial office.

### Step 2: Draft an Institutional-Grade AML/CFT/CPF Policy Manual
Under Section 9 of the MLA 2022, every DNFBP must formulate, adopt, and implement internal anti-money laundering, counter-terrorist financing, and counter-proliferation financing (AML/CFT/CPF) programs.

A generic, copy-pasted policy downloaded from the internet will be rejected by SCUML evaluators. Your policy manual must contain:
* A designated, executive-level **Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO)** with direct reporting lines to the Board of Directors;
* Clear Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) procedures detailing how high-value buyers are identified before transaction execution;
* Explicit Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) protocols for Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and foreign nationals;
* Documented annual staff training curricula on detecting suspicious financial transactions.

### Step 3: Establish NFIU GoAML Reporting Infrastructure
Possessing a SCUML certificate is only the license to operate; the law requires continuous operational reporting to the **Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU)**:
* **Currency Transaction Reports (CTR)**: Under Section 10 of the MLA 2022, any single transaction, payment, or transfer exceeding **₦5,000,000** for an individual or **₦10,000,000** for a corporate body must be formally reported on the NFIU GoAML portal within **seven (7) calendar days**.
* **Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR)**: Any transaction that deviates from a customer's known commercial profile or has no apparent economic justification must be reported to the NFIU within **twenty-four (24) hours**.

### Step 4: Automate PEP Screening & Secure Bank Mandate Validation
Once your SCUML certificate is approved and your GoAML account is active:
* Deploy algorithmic screening against Nigerian and international PEP watchlists (OFAC, UN Sanctions, EU Lists).
* Compile your **DNFBP Regulatory Clearance Dossier** (Certified SCUML Certificate + Board-Approved AML Policy + GoAML Registration ID).
* Formally submit this clearance dossier to the compliance desks of all corporate banking partners, securing permanent unfreezing and clean tier-1 KYC classification.

## 4 Expensive Mistakes High-Value Merchants Make

1. **The "Cash Acceptance" Violation**: Under Section 2 of the MLA 2022, no person or corporate body shall make or accept cash payments exceeding ₦5,000,000 (individual) or ₦10,000,000 (corporate) without passing through a financial institution. Splitting a ₦50M property purchase into ten cash installments of ₦5M is classified as criminal structuring (smurfing) and carries severe prison sentences for directors.
2. **Operating with an Inactive AML Officer**: Appointing an administrative receptionist as your statutory AML Compliance Officer without certified compliance training or board authority fails regulatory scrutiny during on-site SCUML inspections.
3. **Ignoring the 7-Day CTR Deadline**: Failing to file Currency Transaction Reports on GoAML within 7 days of receiving high-value payments triggers statutory administrative penalties of up to **₦1,000,000 per default day**.
4. **Failing to Re-Certify Upon Corporate Changes**: If your company alters its share capital, appoints new directors, or changes its registered operating address on the CAC portal, you must formally notify SCUML within 30 days to update your master certificate.
