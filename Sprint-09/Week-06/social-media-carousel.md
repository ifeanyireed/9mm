During a routine quarterly KYC review, Zenith Bank ran an automated API check against the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) database.

Crestview’s status came back as "INACTIVE".

2. The Missing PSC Register: CAMA 2020 Section 119 mandates unmasking Persons with Significant Control (5%+ equity). Without it, the portal restricted all corporate actions.
