### Anatomy of Zero-Variance Settlement

To eliminate till discrepancies permanently, Ofia replaces manual paper-based reconciliation with three automated controls:

2. **Dynamic 1-Time Virtual Bank Accounts**: No static account numbers on showroom counters. When a customer pays by transfer, Ofia generates a dynamic virtual account. The moment funds land, the POS completes the transaction in under 4 seconds. Zero fake screenshot risk.
