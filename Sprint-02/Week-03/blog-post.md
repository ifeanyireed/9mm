On a Tuesday morning, Bayo's electrical and building materials depot in Ikeja is moving at full pace—mirroring the institutional scale seen across Nigerian distribution leaders pictured in Punch Nigeria's UAC corporate coverage above.

Forklifts load armoured cables and industrial switchgear while haulage trucks queue at the gate. From the outside, the wholesale business looks unstoppable.

But Bayo has a problem.

A procurement manager from a major construction firm emails an urgent RFQ for 4,000 metres of armoured cable and 60 distribution boards, asking for a pro-forma invoice before close of business. Because Bayo's Excel price sheet is not linked to his warehouses, his sales rep spends two days phoning depot supervisors in Ikeja, Trade Fair and Sagamu to count coils. His business has quality products, multiple warehouses and serious corporate buyers. What it lacks is a connected quoting system.

## 10 Ways to Connect Your Wholesale Quoting to Faster Enterprise Sales

### 1. Replace Manual Spreadsheet Price Lists with Automated Customer Pricing Tiers

Looking up wholesale discounts across Excel tabs slows down 50-SKU quotes and causes costly tier mistakes—especially as industrial buyers scale local procurement (as noted in [Nairametrics' November 2026 Valmont Nigeria factory report](https://nairametrics.com/2026/11/22/fg-partners-us-firm-to-establish-irrigation-equipment-factory-in-nigeria/) above).

In `/erp/admin/inventory`, assigning each buyer to a contracted tier (Tier-1 Distributor, Regional Wholesaler, Project Contractor) applies approved volume brackets automatically the second items enter a quote.

**Why it matters:** Sales reps issue accurate, margin-protected proposals in seconds without hunting through spreadsheets.

### 2. Connect Your Quoting Desk to Live Multi-Warehouse Inventory

Imagine Bayo's rep gets an urgent order for 2,000 bags of adhesive and spends half a day calling Ikeja and Sagamu supervisors while the buyer waits.

Linking quoting directly to `/erp/admin/inventory/warehouses` surfaces live pallet and bin counts across every depot on one screen.

**Why it matters:** Reps only quote stock that physically exists, eliminating backorders and multi-day phone tag.

### 3. Place Automatic Timed Stock Holds on Quoted Inventory

Two reps often quote the same limited batch of goods on the same afternoon—leaving the warehouse short when both corporate buyers pay the next morning.

A connected quoter places a timed 24-to-48-hour soft hold in `/erp/admin/inventory`, locking stock for confirmed buyers and releasing expired quotes automatically.

**Why it matters:** Confirmed buyers never face stockouts while unconfirmed RFQs stop freezing warehouse stock indefinitely.

### 4. Calculate Haulage and Delivery Fees Automatically by Weight and Destination

  <img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https://media.nairametrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Image-83-2.webp&w=800&output=jpg" alt="Unilever Nigeria logistics managers and haulage drivers recognised during 2026 Transport Safety Week (Source: Nairametrics)" referrerpolicy="no-referrer" style="max-width: 720px; width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid #E2E8F0; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />

Guessing freight weight on a Lagos-to-Port-Harcourt cable shipment eats directly into gross profit. As reflected in [Nairametrics' November 2026 coverage of Unilever Nigeria's haulage partners](https://nairametrics.com/2026/11/20/2026-transport-safety-week-unilever-nigeria-rewards-transporters-drivers-for-safety-performance/) (pictured above), structured transport economics must sit inside every commercial transaction.

Linking SKU weights in `/erp/admin/inventory` to regional rate tables in `/erp/admin/logistics/dispatch` calculates exact haulage fees automatically on every pro-forma.

**Why it matters:** Every quote reflects real-time transport costs, protecting net margin against diesel and freight swings.

### 5. Set Automatic Margin Floor Rules for Sales Manager Approvals

Waiting all day for a director to sign off on a discount loses deals, yet unrestricted discounting quietly destroys profitability.

Setting category gross-margin floors releases standard quotes instantly while routing below-floor requests to the commercial director's phone for one-tap approval.

**Why it matters:** Routine deals move fast while special enterprise discounts stay tightly governed.

### 6. Automate Customer Credit Limit Checks Before Issuing Net-Term Quotes

When aging balances live in isolated accounting files, reps waste hours quoting overdue accounts on 30-day credit.

Connecting quoting to `/erp/accountant/ledger` verifies live credit limits automatically—clearing healthy accounts or switching overdue buyers to payment-before-dispatch immediately.

**Why it matters:** Sales and finance enforce credit discipline without hours of manual file checks.

### 7. Replace Paper Pro-Forma Invoices with Interactive Digital Quotes

Static PDFs force procurement managers to print, sign, scan and email back—and any line-item edit forces the whole document to be rebuilt.

Interactive B2B quote links on `/shop` let buyers review live totals, approve with one tap and download official pro-formas instantly.

**Why it matters:** Zero paperwork friction makes it effortless for procurement teams to approve your quote first.

### 8. Link Approved Quotes Directly to Payment Confirmation and Order Conversion

Even after a buyer transfers funds, manual bank-alert matching often stalls warehouse picking for another full day.

Unique payment references tied to `/erp/accountant/ledger` reconcile transfers automatically and convert paid quotes straight into active warehouse orders.

**Why it matters:** Paid orders hit the loading bay in minutes instead of sitting idle in an inbox.

### 9. Split Multi-Depot Fulfilment Automatically When One Warehouse Is Short

Fulfilling a 1,000-unit order across Ikeja (600 units) and Trade Fair (400 units) manually spawns messy phone calls and duplicate paperwork.

Multi-depot allocation in `/erp/admin/inventory/warehouses` generates separate depot pick lists automatically while keeping one unified invoice and tracking thread for the buyer.

**Why it matters:** Large enterprise orders ship smoothly across multiple warehouses with zero dispatch confusion.

### 10. Give Returning Corporate Buyers a Self-Service Reorder Portal

Forcing repeat contractors and retail chains to wait for manual quotes on routine monthly restocks ties up your sales desk unnecessarily.

Approved accounts on your `/shop` B2B portal see their negotiated tier prices, inspect live stock and generate their own pro-forma restocks 24/7.

**Why it matters:** Routine reorders run automatically while your sales team hunts new high-value tenders.
