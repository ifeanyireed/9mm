### ACT II: TEARDOWN — THE 3 TECHNICAL ENVELOPE PITFALLS (1:40 – 3:10)

**Visual**: Host steps back on screen. Behind him, three massive digital evidence binders appear:

**HOST**:
Engineer Mustapha’s story plays out in federal ministries across Abuja every single week.
Over 65% of all bids never make it past the first hour of technical opening!

Here are the 3 traps that kill federal tenders:

#### 1. The "Lawyer’s Stamp" Affidavit Trap
Section 16(6) of PPA 2007 demands an affidavit covering five specific limbs:
* Non-insolvency
* No criminal convictions for directors
* Document authenticity
* Technical competence
* And limb number 5: declaring that *no staff of the procuring entity or BPP has any pecuniary interest in your company*.
If your affidavit misses one sentence, or if it isn't sworn in a High Court or before a Notary Public, it is legally invalid. It is not an affidavit. It is just a piece of paper.

#### 2. The "Application Slip" Illusion
When ministerial adverts appear in national newspapers, contractors have only 6 weeks to submit.
Your PENCOM or ITF renewal is pending in Abuja.
So what do you do? You slip a Remita payment receipt and an application acknowledgement letter into Envelope A.
**BPP Circular BPP/R&M/08/Vol.I/01 explicitly forbids tenders boards from accepting application slips!**
You either have the final certified certificate in that envelope, or you are disqualified. Period.

#### 3. The Three-Way Financial Contradiction
Evaluators compare three numbers:
1. The turnover on your 3-year Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC).
2. The turnover in your 3-year Audited Financial Statements.
3. Your classification on the BPP National Database (IRR).
If your tax accountant minimized turnover on TaxPro-Max to ₦380M, while your audit report shows ₦450M to look big, evaluators flag it as conflicting statutory records. Disqualified for financial misrepresentation!
