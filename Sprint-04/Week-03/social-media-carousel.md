## 1. LinkedIn Suite (B2B Executive & Retail Strategy)

### A. Long-Form Thought Leadership Post

```text
Last Saturday, 90 qualified shoppers walked through Tunde's home appliances and interior showroom in Lekki Phase 1.

Couples furnishing new apartments tested velvet sectional sofas. Interior designers compared chandelier finishes. Families inspected double-door inverter refrigerators across two floors.

By Sunday evening, only 11 of those 90 visitors had bought on the spot.

The other 79—over 80% of Tunde's weekend foot traffic—walked out of the exit door without leaving a phone number, a WhatsApp contact, or a saved wishlist.

Here is why that happens in almost every Nigerian showroom:

A couple spends 40 minutes admiring a six-seater walnut dining set and a smart refrigerator worth ₦2,000,000. Before paying, they need to go home, measure their dining room, and confirm cabinet width with their carpenter.

Tunde's sales associate scribbles the product names and prices on the back of a paper business card.

By Sunday afternoon, that paper card is lost at the bottom of a handbag. When the couple checks Tunde's website on Monday night, the dining set isn't listed and live stock isn't visible.

Earlier that same afternoon, another buyer wanted a display armchair in emerald green instead of beige. The associate spent 20 minutes trying to phone the Isolo warehouse supervisor to check stock—until the impatient shopper walked out to a competitor down the street.

With prime commercial rents climbing across Lekki, Victoria Island, and Ikeja, paying millions a year for a showroom only works if every walk-in visitor stays connected to your brand after they leave.

Tunde didn't have a foot-traffic problem. He had an offline-to-online handoff problem.

High-performing showrooms bridge the floor to the customer's phone with 5 operational upgrades:
1. Scannable acrylic QR plaques beside every display—delivering exact dimensions, power ratings, and live prices directly to the shopper's phone.
2. One-tap "Send Spec Sheet to My WhatsApp" buttons that give couples shareable specs while saving verified leads into your CRM.
3. Mobile floor tablets showing real-time stock across the showroom and central warehouse in Isolo—zero 20-minute phone chases.
4. Attributed digital wishlists and pro-forma links so undecided visitors pay online from home in one click—and the showroom associate still gets full commission credit.
5. Split-tender checkout combining POS cards, bank transfers, and flexible financing on one invoice so single-card limits never stall a ₦2M sale.

Your showroom gives customers the confidence to fall in love with your products in person. Your connected system gives them the convenience to finish buying from anywhere.
```
