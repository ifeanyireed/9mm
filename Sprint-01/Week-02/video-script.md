# 🎬 Video Script: "The Three-Tab Trap"

* **Sprint & Week**: Sprint 01 (Impulse Buy) · Week 02 (The Core Vision)
* **Topic**: One automated checkout system across WhatsApp, Instagram, and TikTok
* **Format**: Dual-Track Talking Head (TH) cut with Drama Skit (DRAMA)
* **Target Runtime**: ~4:15 (~630 spoken words at 140 WPM)
* **Cast**: Host (@Ifeanyi Felix), Chioma (Fashion Boutique Founder), Tunde (Customer Support Intern), TikTok Impulse Buyer (Voice)
* **Brand in the Skit**: Chioma's Closet (Lagos Boutique)
* **Product Pitched**: **Ofia Shopfront & Shop Manager** (`client_slug.ofia.shop`, `https://www.ofia.ng/products`)

---

## Technical & Casting Notes
* **Visual Style**: High-energy split-screen e-commerce reality. Vibrant ring light glow contrasting with chaotic browser tabs and blinking phone notifications.
* **Casting**:
  * **Host (@Ifeanyi Felix)**: Sharp, analytical, empathetic commercial advisor. Slate-grey modern blazer over dark polo.
  * **Chioma**: Energetic 28-year-old fashion entrepreneur wearing her signature mustard jumpsuit.
  * **Tunde**: Stressed 21-year-old intern clutching two smartphones while typing on an open laptop.
* **Sound Design**: Triple sync pings (Instagram chime + WhatsApp triple tone + TikTok coin sound) overlapping into cacophony. Bass drops on lost revenue metrics.

---

## Dual-Track Master Production Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] Cold Open: The Multi-App Chaos (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: Close-up on IFEANYI standing in front of three glowing vertical monitors displaying live Instagram DMs, WhatsApp Business chats, and TikTok Shop comments simultaneously.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"If you are running a modern social brand in Lagos, Nairobi, or London, you do not have one store.
You have three unlinked chat apps fighting for the same piece of inventory.
A customer on TikTok claims the last Size 10 linen dress at 2:01 PM.
At 2:03 PM, your assistant sells that exact same dress on WhatsApp.
And at 2:05 PM, an Instagram buyer sends a Paystack transfer receipt for it.
Welcome to the Three-Tab Trap—where more social channels don't double your revenue; they multiply your refunds."

---

### [0:25 – 1:15] Scene 1: The High (The Cross-Channel Drop)
**[VISUAL]**: Chioma's boutique headquarters. Ring light on full brightness. Chioma is doing an energetic quick-change try-on video for TikTok while Tunde watches the laptop screen.

**CHIOMA** *(to TikTok camera)*:
"Fam! The Santorini Linen Two-Piece just dropped! Only twenty pieces cut in tangerine and emerald! Link in bio, WhatsApp link in comments, or DM us on Instagram to claim before it vanishes!"

**[VISUAL]**: She ends the live video. Immediately, three different phones begin chiming with different ringtones.

**TUNDE** *(holding up two phones at once)*:
"Chioma! TikTok live comments have sixty people asking for the account number! WhatsApp has forty new chats! And Instagram DM is blowing up with 'Size 10 pls'!"

**CHIOMA** *(fist-pumping)*:
"Yes! We are doing multi-channel marketing! Take the WhatsApp orders, I’ll take Instagram, and send the bank details to everyone!"

---

### [1:15 – 2:40] Scene 2: The Crash (The Triple-Sold Inventory Catastrophe)
**[VISUAL]**: Forty-five minutes later. The energy has collapsed into panic. Sheets of paper with handwritten orders cover the packing table.

**TUNDE** *(staring in horror at his WhatsApp screen)*:
"Chioma... wait. How many Tangerine Size 10s did we actually cut?"

**CHIOMA** *(holding an open shipping box)*:
"Four. Exactly four."

**TUNDE** *(voice cracking)*:
"I just confirmed payment on WhatsApp for five people. And someone on Instagram just posted a payment receipt in your DMs. And an Abuja customer on TikTok is demanding tracking details."

**CHIOMA** *(grabbing her hair)*:
"No, no, no! Stop! Did you deduct the inventory in the ledger?"

**TUNDE**:
"Which ledger?! The notebook on your desk, or the Google Sheet on my phone?!"

**[VISUAL]**: Incoming phone calls ring simultaneously on both lines. Angry voice notes blare on speaker:
*VOICE NOTE 1*: "You people debited my account ₦42,000 thirty minutes ago and now you are telling me it's sold out?!"
*VOICE NOTE 2*: "I am taking this dress to a wedding tomorrow morning! If my parcel is not on dispatch right now, I will post our chats on Twitter!"

**[VISUAL]**: Split-screen contrast:
* *Left Screen (Chioma's Multi-App Nightmare)*: Tunde desperately drafting apology messages and processing manual bank refunds while Chioma cancels courier pickups.
* *Right Screen (Automated Competitor)*: A calm boutique merchant using Ofia. One universal bio link (`boutique.ofia.shop`) automatically routes TikTok, WhatsApp, and Instagram shoppers to a single live stock pool. As Size 10 sells out, it disables across every channel instantly.

---

### [2:40 – 3:20] Talking Head: The Cross-Channel Inventory Drag
**[VISUAL]**: Cut to IFEANYI in the analytical studio. On-screen graphic illuminates the Multi-Channel Desync Formula.

$$\text{Channel Desync Variance} = (\text{Inbound Traffic Sources} \times \text{Manual Handoff Lag (Mins)}) \times \text{Oversell Risk Index}$$

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Let's look at the operational physics.
When you split your attention across three manual chat apps, you introduce an average of **14 minutes of reconciliation lag** between when a buyer commits and when stock is logged.
In a flash sale, that 14-minute gap causes a **22% double-sell rate**.
That means for every 100 sales you make, 22 result in angry customers, payment chargeback fees, and hours spent processing manual bank transfers.
You don't need three separate teams to manage three social channels.
You need **one single inventory and checkout brain** that feeds all three."

---

### [3:20 – 4:00] Scene 3: The Rewind (The Unified Ofia Storefront)
**[VISUAL]**: HARD REWIND SFX. Cut back to Chioma's boutique. Same video drop, but Chioma points to her link in bio.

**CHIOMA** *(to camera)*:
"Santorini Linen Drop is live! Tap our bio link on Instagram, TikTok, or WhatsApp. Live stock only!"

**[VISUAL]**: Chioma and Tunde sit back calmly watching an iPad showing the **Ofia Shop Manager** dashboard (`/erp/admin/marketplace`).
Orders flash green in real time:
- *TikTok shopper buys Size 10 Tangerine (Stock updates: 3 left).*
- *WhatsApp shopper buys Size 10 Tangerine (Stock updates: 2 left).*
- *Two Instagram shoppers checkout simultaneously (Stock updates: 0 left).*
The dashboard automatically flags Tangerine Size 10 as "SOLD OUT" on the storefront, prompting subsequent visitors to join the restock pre-order waitlist.

**TUNDE** *(grinning)*:
"Twenty pieces sold out in four minutes. Zero double-sells. Delivery waybills already generated."

---

### [4:00 – 4:15] The Close: Actionable CTA
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI steps to center screen. The Ofia Shopfront interface appears on a floating display.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Unify your social channels before your next product launch.
One bio link. One live inventory pool. Zero human typing at checkout.
Deploy your branded storefront today at **ofia.ng/products**."
