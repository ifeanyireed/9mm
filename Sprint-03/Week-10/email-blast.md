### Editor's Opening Letter
**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

For the past fifteen years, African digital commerce was dominated by first-generation horizontal aggregators: massive digital shopping malls attempting to sell everything from car batteries to lipstick under a single generic brand.

To sustain their massive burn rates, these platforms relied on an extractive commercial model:
* Charging merchants **15% to 20% in commissions**.
* Locking seller payouts for **21 days** to fund their own corporate float.
* Hiding customer contact data to prevent direct buyer-seller relationships.
* And copying their best-selling merchants' products under private labels.

Today, that dinosaur model is in structural decline.

Merchants are tired of being treated like disposable tenants.
And consumers are tired of chaotic, uncurated catalogs.

The next decade belongs to **Sovereign Vertical Marketplaces**: curated digital platforms built specifically for dedicated commercial categories—fashion, electronics, construction materials, beauty, or automotive parts.

By replacing predatory commissions with 0% merchant rails, automating delivery-triggered split escrow, and consolidating multi-origin fulfillment into single-box deliveries, sovereign marketplace operators are building hyper-liquid, capital-efficient distribution machines moving past ₦200,000,000 monthly.
