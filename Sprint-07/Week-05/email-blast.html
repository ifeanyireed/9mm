As documented in Punch Nigeria's coverage of university academic milestones (featured photo above), academic directors noted that while top institutions celebrate graduation milestones, private academies and digital bootcamps face severe attrition without early intervention pipelines, losing up to 70% of enrolled learners before mid-cohort assessments when tracking is restricted to passive attendance logs.

Tracking passive logins on legacy LMS platforms hides student dropouts until tuition defaults occur. Deploy real-time behavioral telemetry: monitor terminal dwell paralysis and repetitive compiler failures to dispatch proactive coaching before at-risk learners quit your cohort.
