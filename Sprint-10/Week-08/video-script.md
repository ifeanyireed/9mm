### ACT II: TH — THE ASYNCHRONOUS EXPIRY TRAP (1:35 – 2:30)

**Visual**: Cut back to Host in the studio, with sharp holographic motion graphics illustrating the Statutory Quad interlock.

**HOST**:
Engr. Babatunde was staring right down the barrel of the **Asynchronous Expiry Trap**.

In Nigeria, the Statutory Quad runs on completely different clocks:
• FIRS Tax Clearance Certificates expire every December 31st.
• PENCOM compliance certificates run on a rolling 12-month payroll cycle.
• ITF Form 5A assessments are tied to annual audited accounts.
• NSITF ECA-01 clearances renew semi-annually.

When contractors track these certificates on disconnected spreadsheets, disasters happen.
You submit a tender in November. Evaluation extends into January.
Your TCC lapses. Your PENCOM certificate expires.
And under BPP regulations, the procuring entity is **strictly forbidden** from asking you to submit updated documents after the submission deadline.

That's why Nigeria's top government contractors never submit a tender without tabling these **five questions**.
