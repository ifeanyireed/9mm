Breaking News · In-Store Payment Infrastructure

Banks, Interswitch Push Phone Taps to Cut Payment Friction and Accelerate In-Store Checkouts

Photo: Banks, Interswitch push phone taps to cut payment friction. Source: Punch Nigeria.

LAGOS — In an aggressive push to eliminate retail checkout bottlenecks, leading financial institutions alongside payment processing giant Interswitch have initiated a nationwide rollout of contactless NFC phone taps and next-generation SmartPOS registers across commercial retail centers.

As documented in the commercial payment rollout photo above (Source: Punch Nigeria), the infrastructure upgrade specifically targets high-volume supermarkets, fashion chains, and electronics superstores where cashier queue latency has historically caused up to 14% cart abandonment at the counter. With contactless phone taps reducing counter transaction times from 45 seconds to under 4 seconds, retail operators are combining payment velocity with instant customer contact capture.

"Payment friction at the counter is the single largest leak in physical retail profitability. When customers face delayed dial-up POS terminals or cashiers cross-checking bank alerts, customer goodwill evaporates. Contactless tap-to-pay infrastructure turns the checkout counter from an operational bottleneck into a seamless data capture point."

Next-generation SmartPOS devices are no longer just payment readers; they are mobile ERP terminals. If your in-store checkout fails to capture a verified customer phone number and synchronize floor inventory with your online catalog in real time, you are treating high-paying walk-in shoppers as anonymous one-time visitors. Unifying your physical counter with your digital catalog is the key to unlocking predictable repeat revenue.
