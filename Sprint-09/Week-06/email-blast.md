**ABUJA** — The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) has intensified digital registry enforcement across the country, utilizing automated API cross-referencing to flag non-compliant entities. As documented in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national corporate governance proceedings (featured photo above), the Commission’s automated systems are directly interconnected with commercial banking switches and public procurement portals.

A principal registrar at the Commission highlighted the necessity of institutional compliance:

> "The Commission has transitioned to a live, automated registry environment. When a company fails to file annual returns or ignores CAMA Section 119 Persons with Significant Control (PSC) disclosures, the portal flags the entity as 'Inactive'. Commercial banks interrogation switches immediately detect this status, restricting credit lines and operating accounts. Companies that adopt automated compliance infrastructure resolve these issues in hours; those that rely on informal intermediaries face operational paralysis."
