**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI returns. Graphic on screen breaks down the "Triad of Transit Bleed": Demurrage Surcharges (₦180k/day), Pilferage Leakage (4.2%), and Invoice Acceptance Disputes (18 days delay).

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"When you run blind in-transit freight, you aren't just losing sleep—you're hemorrhaging cash on three distinct fronts:
First: Demurrage and idle truck retention fees that drivers pass back to you.
Second: Transit shrinkage—cartons mysteriously disappearing during unscheduled roadside stops.
And third: Payment freezes. When a destination depot signs a paper slip saying *'received with 14 damaged cartons'*, your accounting team spends three weeks arguing over credit notes before a single kobo is paid.
You cannot scale an enterprise wholesale network when your primary proof of custody is a handwritten carbon receipt."
