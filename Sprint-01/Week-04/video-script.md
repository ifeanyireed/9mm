## Dual-Track Master Production Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] Cold Open: The Impulse Half-Life (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI standing center-frame in a minimalist dark-grey studio. Behind him, a giant high-definition phone screen shows an Instagram Reel playing: a model spinning in an emerald evening dress, with a glowing comment section flying by.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"When an impulse buyer spots your dress on Instagram or TikTok at eleven o'clock at night, you have exactly four minutes before that desire evaporates.
Think about the psychology of impulse buying: the shopper is lying in bed, scrolling on a mobile phone, experiencing a momentary dopamine peak. They love the design, they want the look, and they are prepared to spend forty-five thousand naira right now.
But if your checkout requires them to leave comments, send a direct message, wait for an assistant to reply with pricing, and manually copy a bank account number into a separate banking app... you haven't built an e-commerce storefront.
You have built an administrative obstacle course.
Every additional manual tap gives their rational brain time to talk them out of the purchase.
Today, I am taking you behind the scenes to show you how top social brands engineer zero-friction checkouts that convert traffic before the impulse cools."

### [1:15 – 2:40] Scene 2: The Crash (The 11:34 PM Drop-Off & Split-Screen)
**[VISUAL]**: 11:32 PM. Cut to ZAINAB lying in bed in Lekki. She sees Chioma's post: *"Midnight Emerald Silk - LIVE NOW. Tap link in bio or DM for details."*
Zainab taps the bio link, which opens an Instagram DM window with an automated greeting: *"Hi! Drop your size and phone number, our team will reply shortly."*

**ZAINAB** *(sighing, whispering)*:
"Wait shortly? It's eleven-thirty at night. I just want to pay and sleep."

**[VISUAL]**: Cut back to Chioma's studio. Chaos. Tunde's phones are buzzing non-stop. Notifications pile up: 87 unread DMs in four minutes.

**TUNDE** *(sweating, typing frantically)*:
"Chioma! I'm on inquiry number twelve! People are asking if Size 12 is true to size, and three people asked for Zenith Bank instead of GTBank! My WhatsApp web just disconnected!"

**CHIOMA** *(looking over his shoulder)*:
"Reply to Zainab first! She asked five minutes ago!"

**[VISUAL: SPLIT-SCREEN CONTRAST]**:
* *LEFT (Manual Chioma's Closet)*: Tunde finally copies the bank account number into Zainab's DM at 11:38 PM—six minutes after her inquiry. Cut to Zainab: her phone screen is locked; she is already asleep. The sale is lost.
* *RIGHT (Automated Ofia Merchant)*: A customer taps a clean bio link (`brand.ofia.shop`). Tap one: selects Size 10. Tap two: selects Pay with Bank Transfer. A dynamic virtual account appears. The customer transfers funds in 4 seconds. An automated green confirmation badge checks out the order while inventory reserves in real time.

**[SFX]**: Heavy, low bass drop on the left side of the screen.

**CHIOMA** *(staring at her stock rack)*:
"It's midnight. We had two hundred people in our DMs... and we only closed seven orders?"

### [2:40 – 3:20] TH Segment: The Mathematics of Impulse Friction (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI walks forward as a dynamic digital graphic illuminates the screen.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Chioma didn't fail because her design was weak. Her collection was stunning and her engagement was exceptional. She failed because of the **Impulse Decay Formula**:

$$Checkout Probability = Base Interest × (0.78)^{Wait Minutes}$$

When a shopper discovers a product on social media, their buying intent peaks in the first sixty seconds.
Every single minute they spend waiting for a human assistant to send pricing, confirm stock, or provide bank details reduces their completion probability by twenty-two percent.
By minute four, your conversion probability has dropped by more than sixty percent.
By minute eight, it is virtually zero.
The customer closes the app, checks another notification, or decides to think about it tomorrow.
If your checkout requires human conversation, you are burning your paid advertising budget to pay staff to watch carts abandon in real time."
