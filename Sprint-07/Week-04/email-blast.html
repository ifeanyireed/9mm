JAMB releases 2026 UTME mock results, warns against fraud

Photo: JAMB releases 2026 UTME mock results, warns against fraud. Source: Punch Nigeria.

ABUJA — The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board has released the results of the 2026 Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination mock test, warning institutions and candidates against syndicates offering fraudulent score upgrades and manual grading manipulation. As documented in Punch Nigeria's coverage of computerized examination halls (featured photo above), over 260,000 candidates sat for the nationwide computer-based mock exercise, with examination directors highlighting that automated scoring engines and tamper-proof audit trails are essential to processing high-volume submissions without human intervention or grading discrepancies.

"Disjointed manual procedures create hidden operational traps that erode institutional credibility and enterprise margins. Transitioning to integrated automated ledgers guarantees transparency, regulatory compliance, and frictionless scaling."
