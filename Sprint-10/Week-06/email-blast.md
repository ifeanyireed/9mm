As documented in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national procurement proceedings (featured photo above), the evaluation committees placed unprecedented emphasis on verifiable statutory compliance, contractor BPP categorization, and synchronized financial turnover.

A senior procurement director highlighted the critical difference between winning and losing bidders:

When a company's Statutory Quad clearances are current, their financial turnover is reconciled across FIRS and audited statements, and their engineers possess verified COREN licenses, the evaluation committee can focus entirely on technical delivery. Institutional compliance is the true determinant of procurement success."
