Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},

In Nigeria’s luxury commerce sectors, business moves fast. Real estate developers close multi-billion Naira land deals, automotive dealerships deliver high-end luxury fleets, and corporate legal practitioners manage major client escrows.

Yet, behind this commercial velocity lies an existential regulatory risk: the sudden placement of Post-No-Debit (PND) restrictions on corporate operating accounts.

Under Section 11 of the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 (MLA 2022) and Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) supervisory guidelines, commercial banks are legally mandated to freeze accounts of any Designated Non-Financial Business and Profession (DNFBP) operating without an active, verified SCUML registration.

Many business owners mistakenly believe that having a Certificate of Incorporation from the CAC is enough. Under the MLA 2022, selling real estate, trading luxury automobiles, or holding client trust funds places your business under the same statutory oversight as a commercial bank. Over 50,000 businesses face PND freezes, daily administrative penalties, and personal director liabilities.

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR REAL ESTATE DEVELOPERS & DEALERSHIP EXECUTIVES: Operating without verified SCUML registration is an existential threat to your banking facilities. Central Bank guidelines mandate commercial banks to restrict uncertified accounts. Corporate directors must conduct an immediate audit of their SCUML status, formulate an institutional-grade AML policy manual, and automate NFIU GoAML transaction reporting before routine regulatory sweeps trigger an account freeze.

The 4-Step DNFBP Compliance Roadmap:

  1. SCUML Registration & Biometric Verification: Align CAC business objectives with DNFBP operations and synchronize director biometrics across NIMC, CAC, and bank portals.
  2. Formulate an Institutional AML/CFT Policy: Adopt a board-approved manual compliant with Section 9 of the MLA 2022, appointing an executive AML Compliance Officer (AMLCO).
  3. Establish NFIU GoAML Infrastructure: Automate mandatory 7-day Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR) for transactions exceeding statutory cash limits (₦5M individual, ₦10M corporate).
  4. Automate PEP Screening & Bank Clearance: Deploy real-time screening against global watchlists and submit certified clearance dossiers to commercial banks to remove PND tags.
