## Technical & Casting Notes
* **Visual Style**: High-contrast dramatic tech realism. Dark, sleek corporate studio for the host with floating 3D social media algorithm reach graphs collapsing, intercut with Chioma's brightly styled boutique showroom and Adanna relaxing in her living room in Port Harcourt.
* **Casting**:
  * **Host (@Ifeanyi Felix)**: Sharp, authoritative commercial strategist. Wearing a minimalist black mock-neck shirt under a slate-grey structured blazer.
  * **Chioma**: Fashion brand founder, 28, ambitious but frustrated by declining organic post reach and skyrocketing Meta ad costs.

## Dual-Track Master Production Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] Cold Open: The Landlord of Your Audience (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI standing center-frame. On the digital screen behind him, an Instagram profile with *"150,000 Followers"* slowly fades out while an organic reach meter drops from 100% down to 4.2%.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"If you run an e-commerce brand with one hundred thousand Instagram followers, let me tell you an uncomfortable truth:
You do not own your audience.
Mark Zuckerberg owns your audience.
And every morning when you wake up to launch a new product drop, you have to pay rent to reach the exact same people who already followed you.
When you sell through direct messages without capturing a real database, you are building your retail empire on rented land.
The second the algorithm changes, your reach collapses by eighty percent.
Your ad spend doubles, and your repeat orders dry up.
Today, I am taking you behind the scenes to show you how top social commerce brands use first-party CRM and automated cart recovery to generate millions in cash flow with zero ad spend."

---

### [0:25 – 1:15] Scene 1: The Trap (The Rent Payment)
**[VISUAL]**: Chioma's showroom. 10:00 AM. Chioma and Tunde are reviewing their Meta Ads Manager dashboard on an open laptop.

**CHIOMA** *(groaning, staring at the screen)*:
"Tunde, look at this. We spent three hundred and fifty thousand naira on Instagram Ads this week just to promote our Holiday Velvet Collection. Cost per click has doubled since last quarter!"

**TUNDE** *(frowning)*:
"Chioma, we have seventy-five thousand followers on our page! Why do we have to spend so much money just to let our own followers know we dropped new pieces?"

**CHIOMA** *(sighing)*:
"Because only four percent of our followers ever see our posts unless we boost them! And half the people commenting 'Price?' are brand-new cold visitors who take four hours to convert."

**TUNDE**:
"What about the five hundred customers who bought dresses from us between January and June? Why aren't we messaging them?"

**CHIOMA** *(throwing her hands up)*:
"Where are their numbers, Tunde?! Their orders are scattered across six hundred old Instagram chat threads and three different WhatsApp phones! If we try to copy and paste messages to all of them, WhatsApp will ban our phone number for spam!"

---

### [1:15 – 2:30] Scene 2: The Crash (The 2-Hour Cart Abandonment)
**[VISUAL]**: Cut to ADANNA in Port Harcourt. She is scrolling on her phone, spots Chioma's new velvet dress, clicks through to a WhatsApp link, types *"Hi, I want the velvet dress in Size 14"*, and inputs her name.
Before transferring, her phone rings—it's her manager calling about a budget review. She closes the app.

**[VISUAL]**: Cut back to Chioma's desk. 4:00 PM.

**TUNDE** *(scrolling through uncompleted chats)*:
"Chioma, forty-two people added items to their carts or asked for accounts today, but never finished paying. That's over ₦1.8 million in abandoned sales just sitting there."

**CHIOMA**:
"Can we message them?"

**TUNDE**:
"By the time I scroll through sixty conversations to find who dropped out, it's already 8:00 PM. They've moved on."

**[VISUAL: SPLIT-SCREEN CONTRAST]**:
* *LEFT (Manual Boutique)*: Founder burning advertising budget month after month chasing new cold strangers, with zero record of past customer buying habits.
* *RIGHT (Automated Ofia Merchant)*: The second a customer enters checkout, first-party data captures into a central CRM. When a cart is abandoned, an automated 2-hour WhatsApp webhook triggers: *"Hi Adanna, we saved your Velvet Dress in Size 14 for 60 minutes. Tap here to complete your order."* Adanna taps once and pays via virtual account.

**[SFX]**: Heavy cash-register drain on the left; bright upward chime on the right.

$$Blended CAC = (Total Ad Spend + Manual Chat Labor Cost) ÷ New Customers Acquired$$

When customers purchase through a dedicated digital storefront, software captures verified phone numbers, preferred clothing sizes, delivery cities, and average order values.
