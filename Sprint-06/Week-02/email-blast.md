### Editor's Note
Good morning, university leaders. Every convocation season, thousands of graduating seniors spend three weeks marching across campus holding paper green folders, seeking eight physical ballpoint signatures and ink rubber stamps from eight different offices. If an officer is on leave or a library card index is misplaced, graduation dockets stall. Physical rubber stamps are not only an administrative bottleneck; they are the primary vulnerability for degree forgery.
