### Part 3: Executive Implementation Blueprint (The 5 Tender Readiness Questions)

Table these 5 questions before sealing any federal tender package:

1. **"Are All Four Statutory Quad Certificates Valid Through the Entire Tender Evaluation Window?"**
   * *The Standard*: Never submit a bid with less than 60 days of remaining certificate validity. TCC, PENCOM, ITF, and NSITF must remain valid through ministerial tender board approvals.
2. **"Does Our BPP National Database Classification Match the Exact Project Scope and Value?"**
   * *The Standard*: Verify that your Interim Registration Report (IRR) classification tier and financial threshold cover the lot value. Outdated audited accounts on the BPP portal invalidate the IRR barcode.
3. **"Are Our PENCOM Remittance Schedules 100% Reconciled Across All PFAs Down to the Last Kobo?"**
   * *The Standard*: Resolve all uncredited RSA PINs 60 days before certificate renewal. Submission of PENCOM payment receipts or application slips is strictly prohibited under BPP rules.
4. **"Does Our ITF Form 5A Assessment Match the Gross Payroll Declared in Our Audited Accounts?"**
   * *The Standard*: Evaluators perform mathematical cross-checks between the Audited Accounts Notes (Staff Costs) and the ITF Form 5A declaration. Discrepancies trigger disqualification for inconsistent statutory declaration.
5. **"Have Our Joint Venture (JV) Consortium Partners Been Audited Against the Exact Same Quad Standards?"**
   * *The Standard*: Under public procurement law, every consortium partner must satisfy statutory quad requirements independently. A single partner's expired NSITF certificate disqualifies the entire joint bid.
