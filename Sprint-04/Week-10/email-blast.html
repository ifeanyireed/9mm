Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},

Scaling a retail business from one showroom to three is hard work, but manageable. You can visit all three stores in a day, you know your cashiers by name, and if inventory gets tight, a quick phone call sorts it out.

Then you expand. You open Store #4 in Ikeja, Store #5 and #6 in Abuja, Store #7 in Port Harcourt, and Store #8 in Ibadan. Suddenly, your dream of national retail dominance becomes an operational nightmare: regional inventory islands, pricing anarchy across branches, and a finance team needing 20 days just to compile month-end reports.

Instead of your profits multiplying by 10X, your margins get crushed by administrative overhead. Welcome to The Complexity Trap.
