Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},

Take a walk through your warehouse loading bays this morning. Trailers are queuing up. Forklifts are maneuvering wrapped pallets. Your order clerks are stamping physical waybills. On paper, your gross sales run rate looks staggering: ₦350,000,000 or ₦500,000,000 per month.

Yet when you sit down with your accountant at the end of the week, the atmosphere turns grim: your company bank accounts are running on thin overdraft margins, factory suppliers are demanding upfront payment before releasing the next container, and your top retail supermarket buyers owe tens of millions with invoices dating back 74 days.

Meanwhile, commercial bank prime lending rates remain elevated near 28% to 30% APR on the overdraft facility you took out to finance that very merchandise. You are not just a wholesale distributor. You have unwittingly become an unpaid, unregulated microfinance bank for your customers.

Lead Investigation · B2B Trade Finance & Receivables

FMCG Firms' Credit Sales Hit 5-Year High on Inflation, Weak Consumer Spending

Photo: FMCG production and distribution lines in industrial corridors. Source: BusinessDay Nigeria.

LAGOS — Credit sales across Nigeria’s major fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) manufacturers and distributors have surged by over 55.4% to exceed ₦325 billion, as brands and bulk stockists deploy extended open-account credit terms as a survival tool to maintain stock circulation amidst weakened consumer purchasing power.

However, financial sector analysts warn that while credit sales keep factory pallets moving onto retail shelves, they expose distributors to lethal default risks and severe working capital starvation. When retail supermarket chains stretch payment terms from 30 days to 70 or 90 days, wholesale merchants absorb devastating commercial bank overdraft interest rates hovering near 28% to 30% APR.

"Distributors who rely on manual ledgers and informal trust find themselves carrying millions in delayed receivables while paying double-digit bank interest to keep warehouse doors open. Automated credit gating is no longer an administrative preference; it is an existential balance sheet safeguard." — Commercial Banking Review.

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR B2B MERCHANTS & DISTRIBUTORS:

Audit your 90-day aging receivables ledger immediately and replace subjective credit terms with dynamic algorithmic thresholds. Configure warehouse management scanners to hard-lock picking manifests the moment an account crosses 24 hours past due. Eliminating unmonitored trade credit protects cash margins and unlocks trapped liquidity for prompt factory restocking.
