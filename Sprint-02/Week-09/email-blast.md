### Four Rules to Eliminate Wholesale Haulage Bleed

1. **Calculate Dynamic Landed Cost per Pallet**:
   Never price wholesale stock based solely on factory purchase price. Incorporate real-time origin freight, handling, and fuel indices before quotes are authorized.

2. **Automate GPS Geofence Demurrage Billing**:
   Remove sales rep emotion from detention billing. When a customer holds a trailer past the 4-hour window, the system automatically posts an itemized demurrage surcharge ticket to their open ledger.

3. **Reconcile Haulier Invoices Every 24 Hours**:
   Stop waiting for month-end paper binders. Match carrier manifests against cryptographic digital ePOD timestamps daily. Reject unverified delays within 24 hours.

4. **Tag Inter-Depot Repositioning to Orders**:
   When stock is moved between regional depots to fulfill an order, tag the freight cost directly to that fulfillment batch so you see true net margin per transaction.
