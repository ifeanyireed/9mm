Every week, Nigerian technology scaleups, financial institutions, and corporate enterprises deploy new cloud APIs, analytics pixels, and third-party SaaS tools—yet most boards still point to a static privacy policy drafted years ago as their proof of data protection compliance.

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and the NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID), that static document fails on arrival. Any Data Controller of Major Importance (DCMI) must maintain an auditable Living Data Inventory — Record of Processing Activities (RoPA) under Section 48 and submit certified annual audit returns via an accredited DPCO by March 15th—or risk penalties up to ₦10,000,000 or 2% of annual gross revenue alongside Tier-1 banking API restrictions.

In this edition, we review surging Nigerian cyberattack metrics, fintech and public-agency data protection shifts, local 100MW data centre investments, and the ten operational tactics required to make March 15 audit readiness effortless.
