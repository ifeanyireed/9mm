# 🎬 Video Script: "The 3-Day PDF Quote Trap"

* **Sprint & Week**: Sprint 02 (Supply Chain B2B) · Week 01 (The Core Problem)
* **Format**: Talking Head (TH) cut with Drama Scenes (DRAMA)
* **Target Runtime**: ~4:15 (615 spoken words)
* **Cast**: Host (TH), Bayo (Wholesale Warehouse Managing Director), Emeka (Operations Clerk), Apex Buyer Voice (V.O.), Modern Distributor (Cameo)
* **Brand in the Skit**: Apex Global FMCG Logistics (a commercial bulk distribution hub in Ikeja/Alaba)
* **Target Audience Focus**: Wholesale distributors, FMCG suppliers, building materials stockists, bulk logistics operators
* **Product Pitched in CTA**: **Ofia Inventory IMS & Logistics Command Center** (`https://www.ofia.ng/products`)

---

## Script Breakdown

### COLD OPEN: TH (0:00 – 0:25)

**Visual**: Host sitting at a clean industrial-style desk with a towering, 2-foot-tall stack of crumpled carbon-copy paper waybills. The Host taps a thick red pen against a clipboard.

**HOST**:
Let me tell you how a multi-million-naira wholesale distributor loses their biggest client in broad daylight.

It’s not bad goods. It’s not a competitor undercutting by fifty naira.

It’s a 72-hour delay because your clerk is manually recalculating bulk tier margins on a scrap piece of paper.

*(Beat. Host pushes the paper stack over.)*

Your apex buyer wanted three truckloads of goods yesterday. You gave them a PDF quote three days later.

**SFX**: Dull crash of papers hitting the desk, followed by an urgent telephone ring.

---

## SCENE 1: THE VOLUME FLOOD (0:25 – 1:15)

**Visual**: Inside the dusty, echoing warehouse office of Apex Global Logistics. Forklifts beeping faintly in the background. Bayo, the MD, is pacing between three filing cabinets holding two mobile phones. His junior clerk, Emeka, is hunched over a calculator, surrounded by physical ledger binders.

**BAYO** *(shouting into Phone 1)*:
Yes Chief! Three hundred cartons of vegetable oil and two hundred bags of flour. I’m confirming stock right now! Hold on!

*(Bayo slaps the desk, turning to Emeka.)*

**BAYO**:
Emeka! Do we have forty-five pallets left in Warehouse 2, or did the Kano dispatch take twenty this morning?

**EMEKA** *(sweating, flipping pages frantically)*:
Oga, let me check the physical bin card! Mallam Garba went to warehouse floor with the blue tally book!

**BAYO** *(covering mouthpiece)*:
Bin card?! In 2026?! Chief has five trucks parked outside! Calculate his tier-3 discount and print the pro-forma quote now!

**Visual**: Emeka types frantically on an old desktop monitor, calculator clicking like a machine gun.

**SFX**: Calculator beeps, printer grinding noisily, phone buzzing.

---

## SCENE 2: THE PAPER COLLAPSE (1:15 – 2:40)

**Visual**: Split-screen time lapse. The office clock spins from 10:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Emeka is scribbling calculations, crossing out figures, erasing numbers, and searching through cardboard file boxes.

**EMEKA** *(distressed)*:
Oga, the diesel supplier changed freight surcharges by ₦40 per bag, so the margin formula we used yesterday is wrong. If we bill Chief at yesterday’s rate, we lose two million naira on the freight!

**BAYO** *(rubbing temples, bloodshot eyes)*:
Then recalculate! Call the logistics desk!

**Visual**: Two days later. The clock reads 48 hours later. Bayo finally holds a stamped, typed paper quote. He snaps a photo on WhatsApp and taps send.

**BAYO** *(sigh of relief)*:
Sent. ₦48 million order secured.

**SFX**: Harsh WhatsApp voice note ping.

**APEX BUYER VOICE (V.O., icy and detached)**:
Bayo, I waited 48 hours. I couldn't wait for your accountant to finish audit. Universal Bulk gave me real-time inventory and pricing on their portal in two minutes. Their trucks are already offloading at my depot. Cancel the quote.

**Visual**: Bayo freezes. The paper slips from his fingers. 

**Visual**: Cut to split screen. On the right: *Modern Distributor* (relaxed in an air-conditioned office, sipping black tea). His tablet shows a clean dashboard: *Order #4812 — ₦48,000,000 — Payment Escrow Verified — Dispatch Fleet Assigned: 3 Trucks*.

**MODERN DISTRIBUTOR** *(smiling to camera)*:
B2B buyers don't wait for manual invoices. They buy where inventory is transparent.

**SFX**: Somber piano chord. Fade to Host.

---

## TH: THE MATH (2:40 – 3:20)

**Visual**: Host at desk. Clear on-screen graphic overlay illustrating the B2B paper penalty.

**HOST**:
This is the hidden tax of paper-based wholesale.

When you run a distribution network on physical waybills, carbon paper, and manual WhatsApp quotes, your sales capacity is restricted to how many calculations your clerk can execute per hour.

**On-screen text**:
- Average B2B quote cycle: 48 to 72 hours
- In-transit inventory blind spot: 18% discrepancy rate
- Abandoned wholesale inquiries: 42%

Impulse retail buyers lose interest in ninety seconds. Wholesale enterprise buyers lose interest in four hours. When an apex distributor has capital sitting idle, they move their millions to whichever warehouse can confirm stock, price, and logistics instantly.

You are paying millions in warehouse rent and logistics overhead, only to choke the revenue flow with manual paper.

**Visual**: Screen recording style:
1. Bayo’s apex buyer logs into `b2b.apexlogistics.ofia.shop`.

3. Buyer’s pre-negotiated Tier-3 wholesale pricing applies automatically.

**Visual**: Bayo looks at his tablet. ₦48 million order booked, warehouse pick-list automatically sent to forklift operators' mobile apps.

**BAYO** *(grinning, clapping Emeka on the back)*:
Look at that. Zero paper. Zero phone arguments. The trucks are already loading.
