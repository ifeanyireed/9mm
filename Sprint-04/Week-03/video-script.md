# Video Production Script: Sprint 04 Week 03

* **Title:** The Exit-Door Leak: 5 Offline-to-Online Showroom Bottlenecks (And How to Fix Them)
* **Sprint & Week:** Sprint 04 (Omnichannel Retail) · Week 03 (The Tangible Pains)
* **Topic:** Turning Undecided Walk-In Showroom Visitors into Completed Online Buyers via QR Plaques, Live Warehouse Tablets, and Attributed Digital Wishlists
* **Format:** Dual-Track Talking Head (TH) cut with Narrative Showroom Skit & Connected Checkout Walkthrough
* **Target Runtime:** ~4:15 (~625 spoken words at 145 WPM)
* **Dedicated Cutdown:** 60-Second Vertical Video (9:16) for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
* **Cast:**
  * **Host:** @Ifeanyi Felix (Head of Commerce Editorial, Ofia.ng)
  * **Tunde:** Managing Director of *Lekki Living Interiors & Appliances* (Lekki Phase 1, Lagos)
  * **Kemi:** Showroom Sales Associate
  * **Mr. & Mrs. Adeyemi:** High-intent couple furnishing a new apartment
  * **Walk-In Shopper:** Impatient buyer looking for an emerald green armchair
* **Brand in the Skit:** *Lekki Living Interiors & Appliances* (Showroom in Lekki Phase 1; Central Warehouse in Isolo, Lagos)
* **Product Pitched:** **Ofia POS Terminal & Omnichannel Retail ERP** (`https://www.ofia.ng/products`)

---

## Technical & Casting Notes

* **Visual Aesthetic:** Sunlit, upscale Lagos interior and appliance showroom realism—warm walnut dining tables, velvet sectional sofas, brushed-steel smart inverter refrigerators, acrylic QR plaques, and sleek floor tablets.
* **Sound Design:** Soft showroom lounge ambiance, pen scratching on paper card, unanswered telephone ringing tone, crisp QR camera scan ping, WhatsApp notification chime, and reassuring checkout confirmation sound.
* **Tone:** Warm, consultative, commercially grounded, and crisp. Zero mathematical equations or academic formulas.
