### [0:25 – 1:15] Scene 1: The Midnight Reconciliation (The Shoebox Audit)
**[VISUAL]**: Chioma's Lagos showroom at 11:45 PM on a Sunday. Receipts, paper bank printouts, and poly-mailers cover the conference table. Chioma and Tunde are surrounded by three calculators and an open laptop.

**CHIOMA** *(rubbing her temples, voice shaking)*:
"Tunde, according to our Instagram order book, we sold two hundred and forty units of the Holiday Velvet set this month at forty-five thousand naira each. That is ten point eight million naira in gross sales!"

**TUNDE** *(holding up three different bank statements)*:
"Yes Chioma! But when I add up the money that actually entered your GTBank, the Zenith Bank account, and the Moniepoint POS terminal... we only have eight point nine million naira accounted for!"

**CHIOMA** *(gasps)*:
"What?! That is almost two million naira missing! Who took the money?!"

**TUNDE** *(flipping frantically through sticky notes)*:
"Remember the weekend our banking app was slow and we dispatched forty packages based on customer transfer screenshots? Three of those transfers were reversed! And last week we sent thirty riders across Lekki and paid four thousand naira per rider out of pocket because the customers said delivery was included in the price!"
