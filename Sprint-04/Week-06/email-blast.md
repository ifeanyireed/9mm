As documented in the prime commercial retail corridor photo above (Source: Punch Nigeria), retail property surveys reveal that standard 10-hour daily operating schedules leave prime storefronts unproductive for over 58% of the operational week. Progressive retailers are countering exorbitant rental overhead by converting physical display glass into interactive digital storefronts: by mounting illuminated, SKU-specific QR code plaques on exterior windows, merchants allow evening window shoppers to scan and buy items directly from their phones.

> *"If you are paying tens of millions in annual rent only to keep your cash register open for ten hours a day, your retail economics are structurally upside down. The physical display window must function as an autonomous 24/7 digital billboard and point of purchase. Evening window traffic converts at extraordinary rates when friction is eliminated."*

Retail brands piloting 24/7 dynamic QR window checkouts report capturing an average of ₦4.2M in monthly after-hours orders that would otherwise have been lost to digital competitors.

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR OMNICHANNEL OPERATORS & RETAILERS:**  
> Never let prime retail glass sit idle after closing time. Place scannable, backlit QR plaques beside premier display items with copy that invites evening pedestrians to scan for instant dimensions, home delivery, and exclusive evening discounts.
