### Editor's Opening Letter
**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

In high-growth digital enterprise management, leadership teams focus intensively on product velocity, customer acquisition, and infrastructure scaling. Yet in Nigeria's current regulatory climate, how you handle customer and employee personal data is subject to intense statutory scrutiny.

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA 2023), simply having a "Privacy Policy" link in your website footer is legally insufficient. If your organization is a Data Controller of Major Importance, you are statutorily required to maintain an active Record of Processing Activities (RoPA) and file an annual audit through a licensed DPCO by March 15th.

Failure to comply carries statutory fines of up to ₦10,000,000 or 2% of annual gross revenue. In this edition, we break down the operational blueprint to construct a Living Data Inventory (RoPA) that achieves permanent audit readiness.
