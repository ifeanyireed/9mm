## The Abuja Engineering Firm Hit With a ₦42.5M Retroactive Demand Notice

On a humid Tuesday morning in Wuse II, Abuja, Mrs. Toyin Adewale—CFO of *Redrock Civil & Infrastructure Ltd*, a 65-person civil engineering contractor—sits across from her Compliance Director, Engr. Funke Adebisi, holding two regulatory letters delivered three weeks after an unannounced on-site inspection.

Six months earlier, *Redrock* had won a ₦1.2 billion federal road rehabilitation contract carrying valid annual PenCom and ITF certificates. From the outside, the contractor looked fully shielded—yet as Nigeria's revenue authorities tighten compliance oversight (illustrated in Punch Nigeria's coverage above), framed certificates no longer stop five-year forensic audits.

But Mrs. Toyin and Engr. Funke have a problem.

Accredited PenCom recovery agents and ITF compliance officers audited five years of physical wage books, bank payroll transfers, PFA receipts, and Audited Financial Statements, uncovering **₦42.5 million in retroactive liabilities**:

* **The "Basic Salary Only" Trap (₦18.5M ITF Reclassification):** Finance calculated 1% ITF and 18% pension strictly on Basic Salary, omitting housing, transport, and site bonuses—violating PRA 2014 Section 4 and the ITF Act's gross payroll baseline.
* **The Late-Remittance Penalty Trap (₦24M PenCom Assessment):** During delayed federal milestone payments, *Redrock* remitted pension deductions 14 to 30 days past the mandatory 7-working-day window—triggering PRA 2014's **not less than 2% per month** retroactive penalty across five years.
* **The Off-Payroll Site Labor Ambush & BPP Freeze:** Thirty-eight recurring site technicians paid via cash project imprests without RSA PINs or vendor clearances were reclassified as undisclosed payroll, prompting regulators to suspend *Redrock*'s BPP Interim Registration Report (IRR) within 48 hours.

Her firm has top engineers and valid annual certificates. What it lacks is a forensically reconciled payroll compliance system.

## 10 Ways to Bulletproof Your Payroll Against On-Site PenCom & ITF Audits

### 1. Reconfigure Payroll Formulas to Capture Full Statutory Monthly Emoluments

  <img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https://punchng.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/zacc_1782282343.webp&w=800&output=jpg" alt="Nigeria Revenue Service executive emphasizing strict corporate statutory compliance one year after national fiscal reforms" referrerpolicy="no-referrer" loading="lazy" style="width: 100%; max-width: 720px; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />

Excluding housing, transport, or site bonuses from statutory levies creates instant retroactive assessments—especially under Nigeria's tighter post-reform compliance standards captured in Punch Nigeria's report above.

Configure monthly payroll so 18% PenCom (10% employer, 8% employee) computes on Basic + Housing + Transport under PRA 2014 Section 4, while the 1% ITF accrual tracks full gross annual payroll including bonuses and overtime.

**Why it matters:** Disarms the "Basic Salary Only" trap before inspectors open your wage books.

### 2. Enforce an Automated 7-Working-Day Post-Salary Pension Remittance Lock

Delaying RSA transfers by two or three weeks during cash crunches triggers PRA 2014 Section 11(6)–(7), where recovery agents assess **not less than 2% per month** retroactively across five years.

Ring-fence monthly pension deductions in a dedicated payroll sub-account and trigger automated Pension Fund Custodian (PFC) transfers within 72 hours of payday, archiving every bank timestamp automatically.

**Why it matters:** Stops compounding 2% monthly PenCom fines from snowballing into tens of millions of Naira.

### 3. Eliminate Off-Payroll Site Imprest Payments for Recurring Labor

Paying recurring artisans and operators through project cash imprests invites immediate reclassification as undisclosed payroll when inspectors audit bank vouchers.

Place recurring site staff onto formal temporary payroll with verified RSA PINs—or contract exclusively through incorporated, PenCom- and ITF-cleared labor vendors under signed Service Level Agreements.

**Why it matters:** Closes the casual site labor ambush that triggers abrupt BPP IRR suspensions.

### 4. Reconcile Audited Financial Statement "Staff Costs" Against ITF and PenCom Returns Quarterly

Waiting until year-end accounts are signed lets "Direct Project Labor" or director emoluments push audited staff costs above declared ITF payroll—giving inspectors conclusive proof of underpayment.

Run a quarterly three-way reconciliation comparing General Ledger personnel costs, monthly PFA schedules, and ITF gross payroll accruals before annual accounts are signed.

**Why it matters:** Guarantees your Audited Accounts and statutory filings tell one identical, indisputable story.

Scrambling through dusty storeroom cartons during an unannounced inspection invites hostile best-of-judgment estimates—falling short of the structured transparency championed in Punch Nigeria's fiscal reform coverage above.

Host an indexed five-year digital vault containing monthly wage schedules, PFC transfer advices, PFA confirmations, Group Life policies, ITF Remita receipts, and Audited Accounts retrievable in under 15 minutes.

**Why it matters:** Places primary evidence in front of inspectors immediately, neutralizing inflated estimates.

### 6. Capture and Reimburse Eligible ITF Staff Training Programs Properly

Many firms pay 1% ITF faithfully yet miss up to 50% statutory training reimbursement while lacking structured training records during inspections.

Register annual training calendars with your ITF Area Office in Q1 and log every accredited engineering and safety workshop certificate inside your compliance archive.

**Why it matters:** Turns ITF compliance from an audit risk into a partially recoverable talent investment.

### 7. Verify Active Employee RSA PINs During Day-One Onboarding

Hiring new staff without verified RSA PINs leaves deducted pension funds trapped in unallocated suspense accounts that look like unpaid arrears during PenCom checks.

Make verified RSA PIN capture (or 14-day new-PIN enrollment) a mandatory digital onboarding gate before payroll activation.

**Why it matters:** Ensures 100% remittance allocation accuracy across PFAs with zero uncredited balances.

### 8. Document Subcontractor Statutory Independence With Verified Clearance Packets

Inspectors routinely challenge third-party labor invoices as disguised direct staff unless clear corporate independence exists on file.

Collect each labor subcontractor's CAC Status Report, TIN, PenCom Certificate, ITF clearance, and signed SLA before accounts payable releases mobilization funds.

**Why it matters:** Insulates your gross payroll base from being inflated by vendor invoices during audits.

### 9. Execute an Internal Mock PenCom & ITF Inspection Every Six Months

Discovering a missing PFA schedule or unmapped site bonus internally costs zero penalties; discovering it from a recovery agent triggers punitive surcharges.

Run semi-annual mock audits checking random payroll months, payday-to-remittance timestamps, and allowance mappings so minor drift gets fixed quietly.

**Why it matters:** Eliminates compliance blindspots months before regulators walk through your reception.

### 10. Establish a Formal Regulatory Inspection Response Protocol

When inspectors arrive unannounced, anxious junior staff often hand over incomplete draft spreadsheets that spark wild misinterpretations.

Train reception and finance to verify inspector authorization letters, route all requests strictly through the CFO and Compliance Director, and release only reconciled packs from your digital vault.

**Why it matters:** Keeps on-site inspections calm, documented, legally bounded, and free from avoidable friction.
