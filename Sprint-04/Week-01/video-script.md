# Video Production Script: Sprint 04 Week 01

* **Title:** The 82% Walk-In Ghost: How Massive Physical Showrooms Turn Foot Traffic into Online Sales
* **Sprint & Week:** Sprint 04 (Omnichannel Retail) · Week 01 (The Core Problem)
* **Topic:** Connecting Physical Showroom Foot Traffic to Digital Checkout via SmartPOS & WhatsApp Receipts
* **Format:** Dual-Track Talking Head (TH) cut with Narrative Showroom Skit & Product Walkthrough
* **Target Runtime:** ~4:15 (~620 spoken words at 145 WPM)
* **Dedicated Cutdown:** 60-Second Vertical Video (9:16) for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
* **Cast:**
  * **Host:** @Ifeanyi Felix (Head of Commerce Editorial, Ofia.ng)
  * **Funke:** General Manager of *Royale Living Homeware & Furniture* (Victoria Island & Lekki)
  * **Tunde:** Showroom Cashier at Victoria Island flagship
  * **Ikoyi Homeowner:** Walk-in luxury dining buyer
* **Brand in the Skit:** *Royale Living Homeware & Furniture* (Adeola Odeku, Victoria Island, Lagos)
* **Product Pitched:** **Ofia SmartPOS Terminal & Enterprise ERP** (`https://www.ofia.ng/products`)

---

## Technical & Casting Notes

* **Visual Aesthetic:** Crisp architectural lighting inside a multi-floor luxury homeware and furniture showroom—warm walnut tables, velvet sofas, brass floor lamps—contrasted with cluttered standalone bank POS terminals and paper rolls, then transitioning to sleek Ofia Android SmartPOS hardware.
* **Sound Design:** Soft ambient showroom jazz, loud mechanical thermal paper tear, flatline click on laptop dashboard, crisp barcode beep, and clean WhatsApp message chime.
* **Tone:** Warm, authoritative, commercially sharp, and practical. Zero mathematical formulas or equations.
