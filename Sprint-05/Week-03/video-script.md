# Video Production Script: Sprint 05 Week 03

* **Title:** Resumption Morning Chaos: 5 Bursary and PTA Tasks Your School Should Automate Immediately
* **Sprint & Week:** Sprint 05 (K-12 School Administration) · Week 03 (The Tangible Pains)
* **Topic:** Eliminating Unidentified Bank Transfers, Paper Gate Clearance Slips, and Mixed PTA Funds
* **Format:** Dual-Track Talking Head (TH) cut with Narrative School Campus Drama & SchoolHub Architecture
* **Target Runtime:** ~4:15 (~625 spoken words at 145 WPM)
* **Dedicated Cutdown:** 60-Second Vertical Video (9:16) for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
* **Cast:**
  * **Host:** @Ifeanyi Felix (Head of Institutional Governance / Public Sector Strategy, ResultsPro.ng)
  * **Mrs. Adeyemi:** Proprietor of a thriving nursery, primary, and secondary school in Ikeja GRA, Lagos
  * **Mr. Balogun:** School Bursar (stressed, juggling WhatsApp screenshots and printed bank statements)
  * **Engr. Okafor:** PTA Chairman (calling regarding the new school bus development levy)
* **Brand in the Skit:** *Mrs. Adeyemi’s School* (Ikeja GRA, Lagos)
* **Product Pitched:** **ResultsPro SchoolHub & Bursary Engine** (`https://www.resultspro.ng/products`)

## Technical & Casting Notes

* **Visual Aesthetic:** Bright, authentic Nigerian private school realism in Ikeja GRA. Freshly painted classrooms, crisp uniforms, and yellow school buses contrasting sharply with a crowded administrative corridor, piles of printed bank statements, and later a serene, tablet-powered reception counter.
* **Sound Design:** Cheerful morning schoolyard chatter, rapid WhatsApp notification pings, heavy stapler and rubber stamp thuds, phone ringing, transitioning to crisp, reassuring digital confirmation chimes.
* **Tone:** Warm, grounded, practical, and executive. Strictly zero mathematical equations or calculation containers.
