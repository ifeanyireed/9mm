### Part 3: Executive Implementation Blueprint (The 5 Boardroom Audit Questions)

Table these 5 questions at your next quarterly board meeting:

1. **"What Is Our Exact Live Status on the CAC Public Search Portal Today?"**
   * *The Standard*: Must show **ACTIVE**. If "INACTIVE", annual returns for the last 2 years are in default, exposing the company to delisting under Section 692.
2. **"Are Our Registered Directors on CAC 100% Identical to Our Boardroom Roster?"**
   * *The Standard*: Every appointment and resignation must be filed within 14 calendar days under Section 321. Unfiled director changes trigger personal fines on defaulting directors.
3. **"Have We Officially Certified Our Persons with Significant Control (PSC)?"**
   * *The Standard*: All natural persons holding 5%+ equity or voting control must be filed on the digital PSC register under Section 119. Non-compliance attracts up to ₦5,000,000 in statutory penalties.
4. **"Are All Issued Shares Fully Paid Up and Free of the 'Unissued Share Capital' Trap?"**
   * *The Standard*: Zero unissued shares under CAMA Section 124. FIRS 0.75% stamp duty paid and certified on every allotment before filing Form CAC 2A.
5. **"Do We Rely on Annual Paper Interventions or Continuous Automated Monitoring?"**
   * *The Standard*: Deploy continuous software telemetry that monitors the CAC CRP database 24/7, catching filing queries and renewals 60 days before deadlines.
