## Dual-Track Master Production Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] Cold Open: The Masked Customer (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: Close-up on IFEANYI holding up a printed shipping label from an aggregated marketplace. The buyer's phone number reads `080****1290` and the email reads `buyer_7849@marketplace-proxy.com`.

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"If you sell on a mass marketplace aggregator, you do not have ten thousand customers.
The marketplace has ten thousand customers.
You have a temporary permission slip to pack their boxes.
Look at this shipping label: phone number masked, email masked, physical address routed through a proxy hub.
The day the platform changes its search algorithm, your revenue drops by 80% overnight.
And you cannot send a single email, WhatsApp, or SMS to the buyers who paid you fifty million naira last year.
You aren't an e-commerce entrepreneur. You are an unpaid warehouse clerk for someone else's platform."

### [1:15 – 2:40] Scene 2: The Crash (The Wall of Asterisks)
**[VISUAL]**: Close-up on Kemi's screen. She exports the CSV customer report and opens it in Excel.
Every single phone number is masked with asterisks: `090*****412`. 
The email column reads: `relay-user-9982@marketplace.net`.

**KEMI** *(horrified, calling the marketplace vendor support)*:
"Hello? Seller Support? I am trying to export my customer phone list for our new headphone drop, but all the contact fields are masked!"

**AGGREGATOR REP (V.O., indifferent corporate tone)**:
"Good morning, seller. Under Section 14 of our Merchant Agreement, customer contact data belongs exclusively to the platform. Contacting platform buyers off-site is a violation punishable by immediate store suspension. If you wish to reach shoppers, you may purchase sponsored banner ads inside our app for ₦850,000 per week."

**[VISUAL]**: Two days later. Kemi searches for her smartwatch on the marketplace app. 
Her listing has been pushed down to page 4. 
At the top of the search results is a "Sponsored Recommendation" for an identical watch sold directly by the platform's own private-label brand at a 15% lower price.

**[VISUAL]**: Split-screen display:
* *Left Screen (Marketplace Renter)*: Kemi staring helplessly at zero sales, locked out of customer communication, watching the platform steal her category margin.
* *Right Screen (Ofia Owned Commerce)*: A tech merchant using Ofia. Every sale captures full, unmasked customer data (`/erp/admin/marketplace`). The merchant launches a new product with an automated WhatsApp Broadcast directly to their 5,000 verified past buyers, generating ₦8.4 million in sales in the first three hours with zero platform fees.

**[VISUAL]**: Cut to IFEANYI in the analytical studio. On-screen graphic illuminates the Customer Lifetime Value Equation.

$$Enterprise Asset Value = Owned Customer Base × Annual Repeat Order Frequency (3.2) × Margin Difference (15%)$$

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"Let’s examine the economics of independent e-commerce.
Customer acquisition cost on paid ads rises by **28% every year**.
If you rely on marketplace aggregators, you pay to acquire the customer once, but you have to pay the marketplace again every time they reorder.
A business with **10,000 verified, owned customer records** can generate 3.2 repeat orders per customer annually.
At a ₦15,000 average order value, that is **₦48 million in high-margin repeat revenue** that costs zero naira in ad spend or aggregator commissions.
A store without a customer database is not an asset—it’s just a job."
