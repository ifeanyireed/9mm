## The Resumption Morning That Turned the Bursary Into a Waiting Room

On the first Monday morning of a new term, Mrs. Adeyemi's nursery, primary and secondary school in Ikeja GRA is full of energy—echoing the organised learning environment pictured in Punch Nigeria's school feature above.

Classrooms are freshly painted and yellow buses roll smoothly into the compound. From the outside, resumption morning looks spotless.

But Mrs. Adeyemi has a problem.

Inside the administrative block, eighteen parents stand in a tense queue outside the bursar's office. Over two hundred families paid over the weekend from company accounts, spouses with different surnames or POS agents with vague narrations like *"SCHOOL FEES."* Now the bursar is scrolling through three hundred WhatsApp screenshots against a printed bank statement by hand.

Outside the bookshop, pupils cannot collect textbooks without stamped paper clearance slips, while the PTA chairman phones asking how much has been collected for the school bus project because tuition and PTA levies landed in the exact same account.

Her school has dedicated teachers, supportive parents and strong enrollment. What it lacks is a connected bursary and parent clearance system.

## 10 Ways to Connect Your School Bursary, PTA and Parent Clearance

### 1. Assign a Dedicated Virtual Bank Account to Every Student

When a father pays from his company account or a mother uses a maiden name, general bank statements never reveal which child owns the transfer.

In `/schoolhub`, every enrolled student receives a permanent, unique virtual bank account linked to their student ID—crediting the exact pupil's ledger in seconds from any bank app or POS agent.

**Why it matters:** Unidentified transfers and WhatsApp screenshot backlogs disappear completely.

### 2. Send Itemised Digital Fee Invoices With Approved Installment Options

Broadcasting generic fee lists into WhatsApp groups causes constant underpayments and overpayments across classes, bus routes and boarding tiers.

Personalised digital invoices in `/schoolhub` display each student's exact class tuition, optional services and approved installment milestones on the parent's phone.

**Why it matters:** Parents see exact figures upfront, cutting billing errors and awkward phone disputes.

Automated SMS and WhatsApp schedules in `/schoolhub` send polite, personalised balance reminders two weeks before resumption, three days before due dates and seven days after deadlines.

Itemised billing in `/schoolhub` automatically routes core tuition to school operations while splitting PTA and graduation levies straight into dedicated sub-ledgers.

**Why it matters:** Both the proprietor and PTA treasurer view clean, auditable project balances anytime.
