### Cover Story: Data Privacy & Enterprise Statutory Auditing
**HEADLINE: [Nigeria Data Protection Commission Issues March 15 Statutory Audit Deadline for Data Controllers](https://punchng.com/ndpc-issues-compliance-deadline-warns-firms-of-n10m-fine/)**

* <img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https://cdn.punchng.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/24150114/WhatsApp-Image-2026-09-24-at-3.00.43-PM_1790258474.webp&w=800&output=jpg" alt="Nigeria Data Protection Commission National Regulatory Stakeholder Assembly" referrerpolicy="no-referrer" width="100%" />
* **Source**: Punch Nigeria · [Read Full Story →](https://punchng.com/ndpc-issues-compliance-deadline-warns-firms-of-n10m-fine/)

**ABUJA & LAGOS** — The Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) has issued a formal regulatory advisory reminding all enterprises classified as Data Controllers of Major Importance (DCMI) of the statutory **March 15th** deadline for filing their annual Data Protection Compliance Audit Returns. As documented in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national corporate data governance assemblies (featured photo above), the commission’s enforcement division will initiate compliance audits immediately following the cutoff.

Under Section 48 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), organizations processing the personal information of more than 1,000 data subjects or operating within financial, digital lending, healthcare, educational, and logistics sectors must undergo a mandatory audit conducted by a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organization (DPCO).

The National Commissioner emphasized that publishing a generic privacy policy on an enterprise website does not satisfy legal requirements. Organizations failing to submit their verified audit filings by March 15th face administrative fines up to ₦10,000,000 or 2% of annual gross turnover, accompanied by public naming on the National Non-Compliance Gazette.

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR GENERAL COUNSEL, CTOS & FOUNDERS:**
> Do not wait until March to begin your data protection audit. A thorough statutory audit requires compiling a comprehensive Record of Processing Activities (RoPA), reviewing cloud vendor Data Processing Agreements (DPAs), and testing security telemetry. Partnering with an accredited DPCO today guarantees your enterprise meets the March 15 cutoff smoothly.
