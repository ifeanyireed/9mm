### Long-Form Thought Leadership Post
```text
Resumption week should not feel like an unsolved mystery in your school’s bursary office.

Here is what happens in hundreds of private schools this week:

Forty identical bank credit alerts land on the school statement:
`₦450,000 — SCHOOL FEES`
`₦450,000 — TUITION`
`₦450,000 — BESPOKE LOGISTICS LTD`

Not a single student surname.
The transfer came from a parent's company account, an uncle in Port Harcourt, or a third-party POS agent.

Here is the operational arithmetic:
Bursary Friction = Enrolled Students × Anonymous Rate (38.4%) × Matching Time (15 Mins) ÷ 60

For a school with 600 students:
600 × 38.4% = 230.4 unidentified transfers.
230.4 × 15 minutes ÷ 60 = 57.6 staff hours.

Your bursar spends nearly six working days playing detective on WhatsApp.
Meanwhile, children are stopped at the school entrance gate and parents are humiliated.

Top private schools have retired the single commercial bank account.
They deploy dedicated virtual student accounts:
1. Every pupil has a unique virtual account number (e.g. `9942-0182-David`)
2. Incoming transfers reconcile to David’s profile in 1.2 seconds
3. Instant digital gate pass with dynamic QR code sent to the parent’s phone
4. Automated installment billing that recovers up to ₦23M in historical arrears
```
