## The Silent Creep: How Active Companies Become "Inactive" Outlaws

In Nigeria's commercial centers—from Lagos Marina to Abuja Central Business District—thousands of highly profitable, tax-paying businesses operate under a dangerous regulatory illusion:

> *"Our business is fully active. We process hundreds of millions in monthly turnover, we have over 100 staff on payroll, and we pay our Company Income Tax (CIT) to FIRS every fiscal year. The CAC has no reason to disturb us."*

This conflation between **Tax Compliance** and **Corporate Registry Standing** is the single most common cause of sudden corporate paralysis in Nigeria. 

The Corporate Affairs Commission and the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) are entirely separate statutory authorities:
* **The FIRS** cares about your taxable revenues, withholding deductions, and value-added tax remittances.
* **The CAC** administers your corporate legal existence under the **Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA 2020)**.

Under Section 417 and Section 421 of CAMA 2020, every registered Nigerian company is legally mandated to file an Annual Return (Form CAC 19) accompanied by audited financial statements every single calendar year, not later than **42 days after the Annual General Meeting (AGM)**. 

When a company skips this statutory filing for two consecutive calendar years, the CAC’s automated surveillance algorithm executes an administrative status switch:
1. The company's profile on the public Companies Registration Portal (CRP) is marked **"INACTIVE"**.
2. Compounding daily late default penalties begin accumulating against both the corporate entity and every individual director on record under Section 422.
3. The company enters the administrative pipeline for publication in the Federal Government Gazette and subsequent striking off under Section 692.

As shown in Figure 1 below (Source: Nairametrics), commercial banking institutions bound by Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) AML/CFT supervisory guidelines now monitor this registry standing via automated API feeds. The moment your status reflects "INACTIVE", automated risk triggers restrict corporate credit lines and high-value foreign exchange transfers.

## The Financial Mathematics of Default: Penalty Compounding Under CAMA 2020

Many directors assume that clearing 4 or 5 years of filing arrears simply requires paying the base filing fee of ₦5,000 per unfiled year. This assumption leads to severe budget shocks during regularization.

Under Section 422 and Section 862 of CAMA 2020, together with the Companies Regulations 2021, the CAC imposes compounding administrative penalties for every day a filing is in default:

| Company Classification | Base Statutory Filing Fee | Late Penalty (Per Default Year) | Director Liability (Personal Fine) | 3-Year Default Exposure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Small Private Company** | ₦5,000 / year | ₦15,000 – ₦25,000 | ₦10,000 / director / year | ₦75,000 – ₦150,000 |
| **Medium / Large Private Company** | ₦10,000 / year | ₦50,000 – ₦100,000 | ₦25,000 / director / year | ₦225,000 – ₦500,000 |
| **Public Limited Company (Plc)** | ₦20,000 / year | ₦100,000 – ₦250,000 | ₦50,000 / director / year | ₦500,000 – ₦1,200,000 |
| **Company Limited by Guarantee (Gtd)** | ₦10,000 / year | ₦50,000 – ₦100,000 | ₦20,000 / trustee / year | ₦210,000 – ₦450,000 |

> [!WARNING]
> **Personal Fiduciary Liability:** Notice that late penalties are not merely corporate liabilities. CAMA 2020 explicitly penalizes each officer in default. A company with 4 directors in 3-year default can see total regularization liabilities exceed ₦600,000 before legal professional fees.

### Step 1: Execute a Comprehensive Registry Forensic Audit

Before drafting board resolutions or paying fees, you must know the exact historical state of your company on the CAC CRP database. 

1. **Pull the Live Public Search Extract**: Confirm whether your status is officially **INACTIVE** or if the Commission has initiated Section 692 gazette striking-off proceedings.
2. **Identify the Last Accepted Filing Year**: Often, companies assume they filed in 2022, only to discover that the filing was queried by a registry examiner and never concluded. The arrears clock starts from the date of the last formally approved return.
3. **Audit Directorship & Share Capital Discrepancies**: Compare your current corporate secretarial file with the CAC records. Have directors passed away, resigned, or been appointed without formal Form CAC 7A filings? Has share capital been altered without stamp duty certification? All discrepancies must be regularized concurrently.

---

### Step 2: Multi-Year Financial & Secretarial Document Reconciliation

Under Section 421 of CAMA 2020, an annual return cannot be submitted in a vacuum. It must be anchored by approved financial statements:

* **For Small Companies**: A Statement of Financial Position signed by two directors and certified by an external auditor (or an exemption certificate where the entity qualifies under Section 394).
* **For Medium and Large Companies**: Full Audited Financial Statements, including the Auditor’s Report, Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit or Loss, and Notes to the Accounts for each unfiled financial year.

In parallel, secretarial documentation must be prepared:
1. **Board Resolution for Regularization**: A formal resolution passed by the board authorizing the company secretary or accredited agent to execute multi-year regularization.
2. **Form CAC-PSC (Persons with Significant Control)**: Under Section 119, any multi-year filing submitted today will be immediately queried and blocked unless the company simultaneously files its digital PSC beneficial ownership declaration for any individual holding 5% or more equity or voting rights.
3. **Director Identity Attestation**: Valid National Identification Numbers (NIN) for Nigerian directors and certified passport copies for foreign expatriate directors.

### Step 3: Sequential CRP Digital Ingestion and Penalty Settlement

The Companies Registration Portal operates on strict chronological logic. You cannot file the 2025 return while the 2022 return remains unsubmitted.

1. **Chronological Submissions**: Upload financial statements and Form CAC 19 sequentially, starting from the oldest unfiled year (e.g., 2021 ➔ 2022 ➔ 2023 ➔ 2024 ➔ 2025).
2. **Automated Remita Invoice Generation**: The CRP engine calculates statutory filing fees plus accrued late default penalties automatically for each year. Payments must be routed through approved Remita channels with verified RRR receipts.
3. **Concurrent Post-Incorporation Regularization**: If director changes or registered office address changes occurred during the delinquent years, file Form CAC 7A and Form CAC 3 simultaneously to align the corporate history.
4. **Active Query Monitoring**: A dedicated secretarial clerk or compliance platform must monitor the CRP submission queue daily to answer examiner queries regarding balance sheet dates or signature clarity within the 48-hour portal window.

---

### Step 4: Digital Status Verification and Bank Mandate Reinstatement

Once the final delinquent annual return is approved by the Commission:

1. **Download the Official CAC Status Report**: The CRP generates a digital, QR-code certified **Status Report** displaying all active directors, registered share capital, beneficial owners, and the critical designation: **STATUS: ACTIVE**.
2. **Retrieve Certified True Copies (CTC)**: Download certified copies of all approved annual returns and payment receipts for the enterprise corporate vault.
3. **Dispatch Bank Mandate Reinstatement Packs**: Issue an official compliance package to the legal and compliance departments of all corporate banking partners, including:
   * The updated CAC Status Report with live verifiable QR code;
   * Official Remita receipts proving payment of all statutory annual returns;
   * Formal board resolution requesting immediate unfreezing of credit facilities and removal of internal PND risk tags.

## 4 Costly Traps to Avoid During Corporate Regularization

1. **The "Single Year Filing" Shortcut**: Many businesses attempt to pay only the most recent year's annual return to quickly satisfy a bank request. The CAC portal will reject this submission instantly; CAMA 2020 strictly requires every single unfiled historical year to be submitted and cleared in sequence.
2. **Unissued Share Capital Omission**: If your company still maintains "authorized share capital" with unissued shares, CAMA 2020 Section 124 mandates that all shares must be fully issued. Attempting to file annual returns without regularizing unissued share capital will result in filing queries.
3. **Neglecting Beneficial Ownership (PSC)**: Any post-incorporation filing submitted without an updated digital PSC register will be placed on indefinite hold by CAC examiners under statutory anti-money laundering directives.
4. **Failing to Notify Primary Commercial Banks**: Don't assume banks update their records automatically. Commercial banks refresh external KYC profiles on periodic cycles. Always deliver an updated CAC Status Report directly to your relationship manager immediately after regularization is finalized.
