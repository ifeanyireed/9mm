NDPC Escalates Enforcement Against Data Controllers of Major Importance: Mandatory Registry Compliance and DPCO Audits

The Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) has issued a nationwide regulatory directive mandating all commercial organizations categorized as Data Controllers of Major Importance (DCMI) to finalize statutory registration and file their annual Data Protection Audit Reports through licensed DPCOs before the statutory deadline. Defaulting platforms face statutory enforcement orders and administrative penalties reaching 2% of gross annual turnover.

► What This Means for CTOs & CISOs:

Managing data privacy through annual Word documents or static legal retainers guarantees eventual regulatory sanctions. Technology organizations must integrate automated privacy scanning into developer CI/CD pipelines, ensure all mobile tracking SDKs are consent-gated, and maintain a rolling compliance dossier with accredited DPCO partners.
