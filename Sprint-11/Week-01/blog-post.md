## 1. The Relatable Opening Persona Story: The Lekki Real Estate and Auto Venture Frozen Mid-Closing

On a crisp Thursday afternoon along Admiralty Way in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Mr. Femi Adesanya is standing in the sunlit glass showroom of *Lekki Crest Estates & Luxury Autos Ltd*. Over the past five years, Mr. Femi has scaled his dual-division venture into one of the corridor's most sought-after names—developing contemporary off-plan terraces while importing executive SUVs for returning diaspora clients and corporate directors.

As highlighted in Punch Nigeria's coverage of Nigeria's 28 million housing deficit and institutional mortgage leadership (featured photo above), demand for prime Lagos real estate and high-value mobility assets has never been stronger. That afternoon alone, *Lekki Crest* receives two flagship deposits into its primary corporate escrow account: ₦42 million for a four-bedroom semi-detached terrace and ₦18 million as a commitment deposit on a custom-order luxury SUV.

*But Mr. Femi has a problem.*

By 9:45 AM the next morning—just as his finance controller attempts to wire ₦26 million to concrete suppliers and port clearing agents at Tin Can Island—every transfer is rejected. A stark email from his Tier-1 commercial bank notifies him that *Lekki Crest*'s escrow and operating accounts have been placed on immediate **Post-No-Debit (PND)** under Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML / EFCC) enforcement directives.

Pulling his legal counsel, Barrister Chioma Okafor, into an emergency boardroom review, Mr. Femi discovers three interlocking failures under the **Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 (MLA 2022)** that triggered the dragnet:

### 1. The Unrenewed SCUML Registration and Lapsed Annual Compliance Filing
Mr. Femi had obtained a SCUML registration certificate when launching his first property venture four years ago, hanging the printout behind reception. He had never updated the company's SCUML profile when adding the luxury automobile division, nor had his accounts team filed the mandatory annual AML compliance returns—causing the bank's automated regulatory refresh to flag *Lekki Crest* as non-compliant.

### 2. Unfiled Weekly Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) Above the ₦5M / ₦10M Threshold
Under Section 11 of the MLA 2022, Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs)—including real estate developers, auto dealers, legal practices, accountants, hospitality operators, and jewelry merchants—must report any single cash or electronic transaction exceeding **₦5,000,000 for an individual** or **₦10,000,000 for a corporate entity** to the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) within **seven consecutive days**. Over the previous quarter, *Lekki Crest* had cleared eleven deposits above ₦10 million without filing a single weekly CTR.

### 3. The Unscreened Politically Exposed Person (PEP) Buyer Behind an Opaque Company
The ₦42 million terrace deposit arrived from a newly incorporated Lagos investment vehicle whose silent majority shareholder was a close associate of a senior state official under active financial scrutiny. Because *Lekki Crest*'s showroom advisors collected only a WhatsApp photo of a driver's license—with zero PEP screening, zero Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) verification, and zero Source-of-Funds documentation—the bank's AML monitoring engine froze the entire escrow ledger immediately.

Mr. Femi stares at his idle construction crew WhatsApp messages and mounting port demurrage charges on his clearing desk. His business has prime Lagos developments, luxury inventory, and eager high-net-worth buyers. What it lacks is an automated AML/CFT compliance system.

### 2. Automate 7-Day NFIU Currency Transaction Report (CTR) Detection and Staging

When your sales desk closes four property installments and two luxury vehicle sales in a single busy week, relying on an accountant to manually scan bank statements for threshold inflows guarantees missed statutory deadlines. Under Section 11 of MLA 2022, missing the 7-day clock on any inflow above ₦5,000,000 (individual) or ₦10,000,000 (corporate) is a direct statutory breach.

Deploy an automated CTR staging workflow that connects directly to your escrow and sales ledger, flagging every individual payment above ₦5M, every corporate transfer above ₦10M, and any linked split payments—pre-populating compliant NFIU goAML CTR reports inside 48 hours of receipt.

**Why it matters:** Permanently eliminates late-filing exposure and builds an unbroken NFIU reporting trail that satisfies bank compliance inspections instantaneously.

---

### 3. Digitize Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Before Issuing Any Sales Contract

Collecting a blurry driver's license photo on WhatsApp after the customer has already wired ₦42 million leaves your company legally defenseless if the funds come under anti-graft tracing.

Replace paper forms with a mandatory digital KYC onboarding link issued prior to reservation signing—capturing verified National Identification Number (NIN) or International Passport details, Tax Identification Number (TIN), proof of residential address, and verified bank account ownership before any escrow deposit is accepted.

**Why it matters:** Ensures every single buyer dossier is complete, timestamped, and verified before a single Naira touches your corporate account.

### 4. Verify Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Behind Every Corporate Buyer

In Lagos and Abuja luxury real estate and auto corridors, newly incorporated holding companies and Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) are frequently used to mask the true human owner behind high-value purchases. Accepting a corporate wire without knowing who owns 5% or more of the buyer's shares violates MLA 2022 directly.

Run automated CAC Status Report and Persons with Significant Control (PSC) checks on every corporate purchaser before signing a Deed of Assignment or Vehicle Sales Agreement, archiving verified ID credentials for every natural person holding significant ownership or board control.

**Why it matters:** Prevents opaque shell entities from pulling your business into money laundering investigations or EFCC asset forfeiture orders.

### 6. Mandate Digital Source-of-Funds and Source-of-Wealth Declarations

When a buyer pays ₦60 million via fragmented third-party transfers—or uses an unfamiliar corporate account unrelated to the name on the purchase contract—commercial bank compliance teams flag the deposit immediately as a high-risk structuring anomaly.

Embed a clean digital Source-of-Funds declaration inside your onboarding workflow for every high-value property or vehicle deal, capturing verifiable corroborating evidence (business revenue statements, salary/bonus letters, property sale proceeds, or mortgage sanction letters) and rejecting unverified third-party wires without documented legal justification.

**Why it matters:** Gives your bank's compliance desk immediate forensic clarity on legitimate client wealth, preventing precautionary Post-No-Debit holds.

### 7. Formally Appoint and Register a Management-Level Compliance Officer

Under Section 10 of the Money Laundering Act 2022, every DNFBP must designate a management-level Compliance Officer—not a junior front-desk clerk—with independent authority to approve high-risk PEP deals, sign off NFIU submissions, and liaise directly with SCUML and commercial banks.

Pass a formal Board Resolution designating your accredited Compliance Officer, register their profile on both the SCUML and NFIU goAML portals, and equip them with direct read-only visibility across all corporate escrow and sales accounts.

**Why it matters:** Closes the number-one governance gap that EFCC inspectors and Tier-1 banking compliance committees cite during DNFBP reviews.

### 8. Install a Confidential 24-Hour Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) Protocol

Suppose a prospective luxury SUV buyer tries to break a ₦35 million payment into eight separate ₦4.5 million cash transfers from relatives' accounts specifically to avoid triggering a ₦5 million CTR alert. That tactic—known as structuring—requires an immediate Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) to the NFIU within 24 hours, with a strict statutory ban on alerting ("tipping off") the customer.

Configure a private internal escalation button allowing showroom advisors and accountants to alert your Compliance Officer confidentially whenever a buyer refuses KYC documentation, attempts payment structuring, or substitutes sudden third-party senders.

**Why it matters:** Shields company directors from criminal complicity allegations by documenting proactive statutory reporting inside the 24-hour legal window.

### 9. Preserve a Five-Year Encrypted Cloud AML Audit Vault

Section 8 of MLA 2022 legally mandates that DNFBPs preserve all customer KYC records, PEP screening logs, Source-of-Funds proofs, and NFIU CTR acknowledgments for **at least five years** following the end of the business relationship—and produce them swiftly upon regulatory demand.

Eliminate vulnerable paper sales binders stored in Lekki site offices or scattered employee laptops. Archive every deal's complete AML dossier automatically inside an encrypted, tamper-evident 5-year cloud vault searchable by client name, RC number, or transaction date.

**Why it matters:** Enables your legal counsel to answer any commercial bank, SCUML, or EFCC document request cleanly in minutes instead of weeks of panic.

### 10. Deliver Proactive Annual Bank AML Assurance Packets and Staff Training

Waiting for a sudden Post-No-Debit freeze before emailing your SCUML certificate to your banking relationship manager guarantees costly operational disruption. Equally dangerous is leaving commission-driven sales staff untrained on KYC and anti-tipping-off rules.

Every year, conduct certified AML/CFT refresher training for all showroom, real estate sales, and accounts staff (archiving signed attendance certificates), and proactively dispatch a verified **Bank AML Assurance Packet**—containing your updated SCUML certificate, Compliance Officer appointment letter, Board-approved AML Policy Manual, and summary of active goAML reporting—directly to every banking partner.

**Why it matters:** Keeps corporate escrow accounts permanently green during high-value closings and turns your sales team into a disciplined compliance shield.
