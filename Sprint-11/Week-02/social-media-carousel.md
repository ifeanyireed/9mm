Why does this happen to legitimate, tax-paying businesses?

Because most founders believe that "SCUML compliance" is a static paper certificate framed on their office wall.

Under the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 (MLA 2022), a SCUML certificate is merely an authorization to establish compliance operations. It is not an exemption from ongoing regulatory duties.

Here are the 4 non-negotiable operational requirements every Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession (DNFBP) must maintain:

1. The Mandatory AML Compliance Officer (Section 10)
Compliance cannot be an informal chore handed to a sales rep. The board must formally pass a resolution appointing a designated Compliance Officer at management level, formally registered with SCUML.

2. The 7-Day CTR Mandate (Section 11)
Every transaction exceeding ₦5,000,000 for an individual or ₦10,000,000 for a corporate body must be reported to the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) within 7 consecutive days. Missing this window triggers automated banking red flags.

3. Automated Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) Screening
Onboarding serving or former public officials, senior military officers, or their families without Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) exposes your company to severe criminal liabilities and asset forfeiture.

4. 5-Year Encrypted Document Retention
Section 8 mandates preserving customer photo IDs, utility bills, source-of-funds declarations, and transaction receipts for a minimum of 5 years.
