### Cover Story: The Statutory Quad & Public Procurement Governance
**HEADLINE: [Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) Reports 65% Disqualification Rate at Technical Tender Opening](https://punchng.com/bpp-reports-high-tender-disqualification-rate/)**

* <img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https://cdn.punchng.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/24150114/WhatsApp-Image-2026-09-24-at-3.00.43-PM_1790258474.webp&w=800&output=jpg" alt="Federal Public Procurement Stakeholders and Statutory Quad Assembly in Abuja" referrerpolicy="no-referrer" width="100%" />
* **Source**: Punch Nigeria · [Read Full Story →](https://punchng.com/bpp-reports-high-tender-disqualification-rate/)

**ABUJA** — The Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) and federal procurement directorates have revealed that over 65% of all bids submitted for federal capital projects fail at the preliminary statutory evaluation phase. As documented in Punch Nigeria’s coverage of national procurement governance assemblies (featured photo above), the overwhelming majority of disqualified contractors fail due to preventable administrative defects across the "Statutory Quad"—Tax, Pension, Industrial Training, and Employees' Compensation clearances.

A principal evaluation director at the public procurement oversight agency noted the rigidity of federal procurement rules:

> "The Public Procurement Act 2007 leaves zero room for administrative discretion. Section 16(6) is clear: if an engineering firm submits a proposal with an expired PENCOM certificate, an unverified ITF clearance, or conflicting turnover figures between their TCC and audited accounts, the evaluation committee MUST declare the bid non-responsive. We cannot evaluate technical methodology if the statutory foundation is broken."

👉 **[Read full story at Punch Nigeria →](https://punchng.com/bpp-reports-high-tender-disqualification-rate/)**

> **► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR CONTRACTORS & BIDDERS:**
> Winning public tenders requires institutional compliance discipline. Never submit a tender package without auditing your Quad certificates against the live BPP database, reconciling financial turnover across FIRS and audited statements, and verifying professional council licenses for all technical personnel.
