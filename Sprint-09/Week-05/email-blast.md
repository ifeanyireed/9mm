### Editor's Opening Letter
**Dear {{first_name|Valued Partner}},**

In Nigerian commerce, corporate executives often assume that once a company is incorporated, its legal existence is guaranteed forever.

Inside the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), the reality is starkly different. Incorporation is an ongoing statutory contract.

Under Section 692 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA 2020), where a company fails to file annual returns for two consecutive years, the Commission possesses full legal authority to strike its name off the register and dissolve the company.

More than 100,000 Nigerian companies have been gazetted for strike-off. The real danger is not just dissolution—it is the operational paralysis that occurs long before the final strike-off notice: commercial bank account restrictions, tender disqualifications, and frozen corporate transactions.

In this edition, we break down the three unmistakable warning signs that your enterprise is on the CAC blacklist and explain how to restore active standing in 24 hours.
