EFCC & State Treasury Cleansing Task Force Recover ₦2.4B in Deceased Personnel Pension Cloning Scam

Photo: Inter-agency enforcement operations dismantle pension cloning cartel. Source: Punch Nigeria / Economic and Financial Crimes Commission.

ABUJA — An inter-agency forensic task force established by state ministries of finance in conjunction with the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) has recovered ₦2.4 billion from a civil service payroll syndicate that systematically cloned service records of deceased public educators across twelve sub-national registries. As documented in the anti-graft enforcement proceedings (featured photo above, Source: Punch Nigeria / Economic and Financial Crimes Commission), investigators found that corrupt registry clerks leveraged obsolete paper folios to suppress death notifications, routing monthly salaries into proxy accounts for up to seven years. The commission recommended immediate migration to cryptographic identity ledgers, noting that automated cross-referencing between National Identification Numbers (NIN), Bank Verification Numbers (BVN), and biometric school gate logs immediately severs syndicate operations.
