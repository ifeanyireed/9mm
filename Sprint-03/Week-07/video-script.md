### [1:06 – 1:40] Talking Head Breakdown: Why Split Carts Break Logistics (TH)
**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI returns. Graphic on screen breaks down the "Multi-Vendor Logistics Conundrum": Uncoordinated Direct-Drops (3x delivery bikes, 3x tracking links, 68% cart abandonment), vs. Monolithic Warehousing (Expensive long-term leases, high inventory holding costs).

**IFEANYI (Host)**:
"In a single-vendor store, logistics is simple: one warehouse, one box, one courier bike.
In a multi-vendor marketplace, single orders fragment across multiple origins.
If you force each vendor to dispatch direct to the consumer:
The customer pays triple shipping.
Three different riders ring their doorbell at different times.
And when one package arrives late or damaged, the entire brand reputation is trashed.
On the other hand, forcing small independent merchants to pre-ship all inventory into an expensive central warehouse destroys their agility.
The solution is an automated **Hub-and-Spoke Consolidation Engine**."

**[VISUAL]**: IFEANYI standing center-frame with sleek animated callouts showcasing the Ofia Multi-Vendor Logistics Suite.

"Here is how the Ofia Hub-and-Spoke Engine turns multi-vendor logistics into a competitive moat:
