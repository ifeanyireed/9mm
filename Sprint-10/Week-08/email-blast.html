Bureau of Public Procurement Enforces Real-Time Inter-Agency Audits Across Federal MDAs

Source: Punch Nigeria · Read Full Story →

The Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) has mandated all federal ministries, departments, and agencies (MDAs) to execute real-time inter-agency validation of statutory documents during Stage 1 technical evaluations. Evaluation committees cross-reference Tax Clearance Certificates, PENCOM clearances, ITF Form 5A letters, and NSITF employee compensation certificates directly against central regulatory databases in Abuja.

► WHAT THIS MEANS FOR FEDERAL CONTRACTORS & EPC SUPPLIERS:

Technical excellence and competitive pricing cannot rescue a bid that fails preliminary screening. Procuring entities are legally barred from requesting retroactive document submissions after public bid opening. Contractors must enforce continuous 90-day statutory expiry buffers across all four regulatory pillars to ensure uninterrupted tender eligibility.

The 5 Questions Every Managing Director & Bid Manager Must Ask

1. "Are all four statutory quad certificates valid through the entire tender evaluation window?"

Never submit a tender if any certificate has less than 60 days of remaining validity. If evaluation extends into a new quarter or year, an expired certificate triggers immediate Stage 1 disqualification.
2. "Does our BPP National Database classification match the project scope and threshold?"

Verify your Interim Registration Report (IRR) classification tier. Submitting a bid exceeding your approved financial category or with outdated audited accounts invalidates the IRR barcode.
3. "Are our PENCOM remittance schedules 100% reconciled across all PFAs?"

Resolve all uncredited RSA PINs 60 days before certificate renewal. Submitting bank payment slips or PENCOM acknowledgment receipts is strictly prohibited under BPP procurement guidelines.
4. "Does our ITF Form 5A assessment match the payroll in our audited accounts?"

Evaluators execute mathematical cross-checks between Audited Accounts Notes (Staff Costs) and the ITF Form 5A declaration. Discrepancies trigger disqualification for inconsistent statutory declaration.
5. "Have our Joint Venture (JV) partners been audited against the exact same standards?"

Under public procurement law, every consortium partner must satisfy statutory quad requirements independently. A single partner's expired NSITF certificate disqualifies the entire joint bid.
